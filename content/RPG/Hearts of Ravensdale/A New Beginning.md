It's been a while since I sat down to write anything in here, and starting a new RPG campaign seemed like a really good opportunity to do so. *Legends in the Mist* really appealed to me, both the system, which is simple and yet incredibly extensible, and the setting, which is just... Let me put it this way; It is both homely and hopeful and both of those appeal to me right now.

We have our [[Characters/index|cast of characters]]. We've played a couple of sessions with pre-generated ones, so we could find our collective feet, and one with this band who have yet to establish their fellowship, but we'll deal with that at the finish of this first adventure, I think.

Fun times!
