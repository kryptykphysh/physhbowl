# Recording

[Jonathan Sims](https://the-magnus-archives.fandom.com/wiki/Jonathan_Sims "Jonathan Sims") starts to record the statement of Nathan Watts when he hears a noise. He calls out, inquiring if anyone is there and stating that the Archives are off limits. He asks if [Martin](https://the-magnus-archives.fandom.com/wiki/Martin_Blackwood "Martin Blackwood") is there and swears that he will peel him if he has brought another dog into the Archives.

He leaves to investigate and a mysterious voice is heard. It chants "Vigilo. Audio. Opperior." several times but fades as John comes back. He mutters to himself about IKEA shelves and notices that the tape recorder is still running. He decides to start over and turns it off.

# Continuity
- The chanted Latin translates to "I watch. I listen. I wait."
- This chant parallels the official description of episode description of [[MAG 160 - The Eye Opens]]: "Vigilo, Audio, Supervenio", which roughly translates to "I watch. I listen. I surpass."
- Martin let a dog into the Archives in [[The Magnus Archives Liveshow]].
