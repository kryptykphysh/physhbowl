# Statement

[[Adrian Weiss]] moved to the rural village of Cratfield as a child. While attending school he met [[Gordon Goodman]], a quiet, easygoing boy who was notably small for his age.

At the edge of Cratfield was a property dubbed 'Maggie's Dump', owned by a reclusive hoarder named [[Margaret Carnegie]]. [[Margaret Carnegie|Margaret]], or [[Margaret Carnegie|Maggie]], was rarely seen by locals except when fussing with her hoard, which consisted primarily of household garbage and spilled out of her bungalow onto the rest of the property. Other residents used the property as a place to dump their own oversized garbage.

[[Margaret Carnegie|Maggie]] was an elderly woman, and [[Gordon Goodman|Gordon]] had been tasked by his parents to deliver food to her multiple times a week. The children at [[Adrian Weiss|Adrian]]'s school circulated many rumours about [[Margaret Carnegie|Maggie]], including that that she was a witch, that she had chased after one of the schoolchildren with a knife, and that she had murdered her husband and hid his dismembered corpse under her hoard.

[[Adrian Weiss|Adrian]] would join [[Gordon Goodman|Gordon]] on his delivery to [[Margaret Carnegie|Maggie]], but fled before getting close to the house. He would watch as [[Margaret Carnegie|Maggie]] answered and let [[Gordon Goodman|Gordon]] in. [[Gordon Goodman|Gordon]] would claim to have only been asked to stack things while inside, but his fingernails would be stained and dirty the following day.

This continued for about a month, until [[Gordon Goodman|Gordon]] did not show at school the day after a delivery. His father claimed [[Gordon Goodman|Gordon]] had a fever but [[Adrian Weiss|Adrian]] believed he was lying. [[Adrian Weiss|Adrian]] suspected [[Gordon Goodman|Gordon]] was still at [[Margaret Carnegie|Maggie]]'s and went to find him, but as he approached the house the dump's stench overcame him and he fell to his knees. There, stretching from where he was all the way to the house, he saw countless bags full of the food that [[Gordon Goodman|Gordon]] had brought [[Margaret Carnegie|Maggie]]. [[Adrian Weiss|Adrian]] touched one of the cans, which started to shake and warp as though the contents were growing quickly.

[[Adrian Weiss|Adrian]] scrambled backwards and his arm sunk into the earth. When he pulled it out, earthworms clung to his skin. In the hole he had made he saw a mask of [[Gordon Goodman|Gordon]]'s face made out of paper maché. Remembering why he had come, [[Adrian Weiss|Adrian]] approached the house and climbed up onto the garbage stacked nearby to look through a window. There, he saw [[Gordon Goodman|Gordon]] sitting still with [[Margaret Carnegie|Maggie]] bent over him. Small shapes moved over [[Gordon Goodman|Gordon]]'s skin as [[Margaret Carnegie|Maggie]] placed paper maché over his face and sung to him softly. [[Adrian Weiss|Adrian]]'s foot slipped and nudged a bottle, frightening him and causing him to flee.

[[Gordon Goodman|Gordon]] was at school the next day, seemingly fine. He didn't mention anything strange happening the day before and [[Adrian Weiss|Adrian]] believed he stopped delivering to [[Margaret Carnegie|Maggie]]. A year later, [[Adrian Weiss|Adrian]] moved away and the two lost touch.

Years later, [[Adrian Weiss|Adrian]] went to Ipswich for work and stopped in Cratfield. While making conversation with residents he discovered that [[Margaret Carnegie|Maggie]]'s Dump was still in use, but was now called 'Gordie's Dump' for the strange recluse who lived there.

# Post-Statement

[[Martin]] states that the Archival assistants are filling in for [[Jonathan Sims|John]] following his absence. [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] had looked up the address for the Cratfield dump and found that it had been bequeathed to [[Gordon Goodman|Gordon]] by [[Margaret Carnegie]] after her death in 1982. [[Gordon Goodman|Gordon]] had identified the body before it went missing the following night. [[Margaret Carnegie|Maggie]]'s cause of death had been listed as "natural causes" and assumed to be pneumonia, but her lungs were full of newspaper pulp and numerous insect-like legs were found growing from her back and sides. [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] begins to speculate on [[Gordon Goodman|Gordon]]'s potential involvement in the body's disappearance but is interrupted.

[[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] stops the follow up when he finds an injured [[Melanie King]] wandering the corridors. She is looking for [[Jonathan Sims|John]], mentioning she was shot in India and wanted to discuss her experience with him. [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] explains that [[Jonathan Sims|John]] is missing and that most of the archival staff thinks he murdered [[Jurgen Leitner]] or suspect he was involved in [[Not-Sasha]]'s disappearance. [[Melanie King|Melanie]], being able to recognise the separate entities [[Sasha James]] and [[Not-Sasha]], asks which [[Sasha James|Sasha]] was the one to disappear and becomes frustrated when [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] doesn't understand her question.

[[Elias Bouchard|Elias]] interrupts [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] and [[Melanie King|Melanie]]'s conversation and abruptly offers [[Melanie King|Melanie]] a job as an Archival assistant. [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] tries to protest this, arguing that [[Melanie King|Melanie]] is underqualified for such a role, but [[Elias Blackwood|Elias]] dismisses his comments and alludes to [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] being underqualified himself. [[Melanie King|Melanie]] accepts the position and [[Elias Blackwood|Elias]] escorts her to his office for an interview, leaving [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] to shout to himself in frustration.
