# Pre-Statement

[[Georgie Barker|Georgie]] leads [[Jonathan Sims|John]] and [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] to the Panopticon stairs, which is surrounded by the old [[Archivists]]. [[Georgie Barker|Georgie]] leaves to return to [[Cult in the Post-Change World|her cult]] and [[Melanie King|her girlfriend]]. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] and [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] begin the long climb to the top of the tower. The pair reach the top and meet [[Rosie Zampano]], who is still acting as an assistant to [[Jonah Magnus]]. [[Rosie Zampano|Rosie]] does not recognise them and firmly states that the two of them will need an appointment to see Magnus, but agrees to inform him that they have arrived. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] and [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] leave to have a private conversation, and [[Jonathan Sims|John]] feels the need to make a statement.

# Statement

[[Rosie Zampano|Rosie]] recalls her interview with [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]]. He had quietly called her an upsetting nickname from her childhood while looking over her CV, but denied saying anything when asked. [[Rosie Zampano|Rosie]] thought bitterly of [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]] and of the interview, but was polite and pleasant when asked why she wanted the job.

At present, [[Rosie Zampano|Rosie]] bears witness to the horrors of the [[The Change|new world]]. She considers calling [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]] but decides against it and puts on a smile, fearing that she is being watched and scrutinised.

[[Rosie Zampano|Rosie]] recalls [[Breekon & Hope|two strange delivery men]] with a package for [[Jonathan Sims]]. They had been impatient and rude, and she decided to sign for the package and pass it on to [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] rather than call [[Jonathan Sims|John]] to sign for it himself. [[Rosie Zampano|Rosie]] was aware of [[Jonathan Sims|John]]'s insecurity and abrasive personality and wanted to avoid any confrontation with him, as well as indulge her own curiosity on what the [[Web Table|delivery]] was.

At present, [[Rosie Zampano|Rosie]] is certain that [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]] is the reason why the world has changed based on what she has overheard from his office. He has had no visitors and they have not interacted since she clocked in, but she continues to smile.

[[Rosie Zampano|Rosie]] recalls telling [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]] of the Jane Prentiss attack in 2016. Despite receiving no calls or visitors, [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]] was calm and dismissive of the building-wide evacuation order, which left [[Rosie Zampano|Rosie]] suspicious and intent to find out more. She tried to listen in on a conversation between [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]] and a panicked [[Sasha James]], but was caught and returned to her desk.

At present, [[Rosie Zampano|Rosie]] wants to tell [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]] to stop in the hopes that he would change the world back, but is afraid of his anger and of losing her job. She pushes the thought aside and puts on a smile.

[[Rosie Zampano|Rosie]] recalls [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]]'s meeting with [[Alice Daisy Tonner|Daisy]], [[Jonathan Sims|John]], [[Timothy Stoker|Tim]], [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]], and [[Melanie King|Melanie]]. She wanted to warn them about [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]], but did not know what specifically she would be warning them about. She was too afraid to eavesdrop on the conversation, but after [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]] instructed her to send away the police officers that arrived and the others had left the office looking utterly defeated, she knew she was working for [[The Eye|something evil]]. [[Rosie Zampano|Rosie]] was upset by the knowledge, but knew the depth of information [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]] had on his employees and was too afraid of being targeted by him to change careers or confront him on his actions.

At present, [[Rosie Zampano|Rosie]] wonders why she does not simply ask [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]] what is happening, as she knows he thinks well of her and is unlikely to be upset by the question. However, she is immobilised by her fear of the man and remains where she is.

[[Rosie Zampano|Rosie]] recalls [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]]'s arrest, and how the tension and unease she felt in his presence had not lessened even as he was handcuffed. When [[Peter Lukas]] introduced himself as her new boss, the feelings had simply transferred onto him. She did not like this new situation, but felt that she owed something to [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]] for his indulgence of her intrusions and was not in a position to leave.

At present, [[Rosie Zampano|Rosie]] continues to act as [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]]'s assistant. Strange figures have asked to see him, but she simply gives a false, painful smile and tells them he is unavailable.

[[Rosie Zampano|Rosie]] recalls [[Trevor Herbert]] and [[Julia Montauk]]'s violent intrusion into the Archives. They had run past her without acknowledging her presence, and she went home knowing that [[Peter Lukas]] was dead and [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]] missing. She waited there for a time but eventually returned to work, determined to know what was going on.

She stayed at work for several days, unable to leave until she understood what was happening. Finally, as [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]] returned to his office and the world began to change, [[Rosie Zampano|Rosie]] realised that any action she might have been planning to take was already far too late.

# Post-Statement

As [[Jonathan Sims|John]] finishes, [[Rosie Zampano|Rosie]] comes up to him and [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]], declaring they have an appointment. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] and [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] enter the office, where Magnus is channeling all of the fear and pain in the world. [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] tries to confront him, but Magnus is unresponsive. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] realises that Magnus's plan was successful, as he is the pupil of [[the Eye]].
