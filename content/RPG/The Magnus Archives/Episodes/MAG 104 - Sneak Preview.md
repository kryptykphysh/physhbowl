# Pre-Statement

[[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] begins to start recording a statement from [[Eduardo Acosta]] before being startled by the sound of a sudden crash of tumbling boxes, revealing [[Timothy Stoker|Tim]] to have been moving them in the background.

[[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] calms down after the shock and proceeds to ask [[Timothy Stoker|Tim]] about a book he took from the library related to circuses. [[Timothy Stoker|Tim]] asks why and [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] explains he is looking into anything regarding [[The Unknowing]] and, after [[Timothy Stoker|Tim]] expresses his confusion, explains it is a [[Rituals|ritual]] being performed by the [[Circus of the Other]] and can be world-ending. [[Timothy Stoker|Tim]] is outraged that no-one had informed him about other circus-related statements, thinking [[MAG 24 - Strange Music|Leanne Denikin's statement]] was the most recent one from a year ago. [[Timothy Stoker|Tim]] protests that he can tell [[Jonathan Sims|John]] face to face, but [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] points out that the two of them always end up fighting these days when they talk.

# Statement

[[Timothy Stoker|Tim]]'s younger brother [[Danny Stoker|Danny]] was always more handsome, more charismatic and more successful, instead of jealous, [[Timothy Stoker|Tim]] was always very proud of him and loved him a lot. In 2013, [[Danny Stoker|Danny]] became obsessed with urban exploration. He was particularly fascinated by "ghost buildings," the remnants of old buildings that had been incorporated into new ones.

In August of that year [[Danny Stoker|Danny]] decided to break into the Royal Opera House in search of the remains of the Covent Garden Theatre designed by [[Robert Smirke]]. [[Timothy Stoker|Tim]] objected, afraid that [[Danny Stoker|Danny]] would get into trouble, but [[Danny Stoker|Danny]] went anyway.

[[Danny Stoker|Danny]] had not returned by the time [[Timothy Stoker|Tim]] went to bed. In the middle of the night, [[Timothy Stoker|Tim]] woke up and found [[Danny Stoker|Danny]] in an armchair, muttering to himself. [[Timothy Stoker|Tim]] heard the name "Joey" and "the show must go on." He urged [[Danny Stoker|Danny]] to lie down on the couch and get some sleep. In the morning, [[Danny Stoker|Danny]] had vanished, leaving behind some drawings of a grotesque and terrifying clown that somewhat resembled [Joseph Grimaldi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_Grimaldi).

[[Timothy Stoker|Tim]] borrowed [[Danny Stoker|Danny]]'s notes on the opera house and snuck in himself, assuming he had returned there. He found his way to a huge replica of the Covent Garden Theatre carved out of white stone and located twenty feet below street level. There was even a carved audience of human-like shapes. He spotted someone he took to be [[Danny Stoker|Danny]] on the stage, and called to him.

A spotlight appeared overhead, revealing the clown from [[Danny Stoker|Danny]]'s drawing. It crept slowly up to the figure on stage, and then pulled [[Danny Stoker|Danny]]'s skin off the figure standing on stage. Underneath was a beautiful play of colours that resembled an impressionist painting of a dancer.

The next thing [[Timothy Stoker|Tim]] knew, he was standing back at street level, holding a flier for the [[Circus of the Other]]. He noticed the clown from the auditorium was on the flier, billed as a guest performer. Then the flier crumbled to ash.

# Post-Statement

[[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] asks if [[Timothy Stoker|Tim]] ever tried to return to the Covent Garden site, but [[Timothy Stoker|Tim]] replies he is not sure if he would be allowed to leave a second time. He joined [[The Magnus Institute]] in order to find answers, but admits he became complacent about it until recently. He vehemently declares that he wants to help find the [[Circus of the Other|Circus]].

[[Elias Bouchard|Elias]] appears, having observed [[Timothy Stoker|Tim]]'s statement from afar. He asks [[Timothy Stoker|Tim]] to stay away from the [[Circus of the Other|Circus]] and [[The Unknowing]], so as not to jeopardize [[Jonathan Sims|John]]'s mission. [[Timothy Stoker|Tim]] angrily declares that [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]] will have to kill him if he wants to keep him out of it.
