# Recording

[[Basira Hussain|Basira]] and [[Jonathan Sims|John]] are traversing the web across [[The Gap in Reality]] as they head toward the middle, where [[Annabelle Cane|Annabelle]] and [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] are waiting. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] is able to think more clearly now that the [[Camera of Occultation|camera]] has been destroyed but he cannot Know [[Annabelle Cane|Annabelle]]'s plan. When he tries, his mind is drawn in by the chasm below them and he almost loses his balance. All he manages to Know is that [[Annabelle Cane|Annabelle]]'s plan is tied up with the rift and the rift itself is simply "somewhere else".

[[Basira Hussain|Basira]] asks about the [[Tape Recorders|tapes]] and [[Jonathan Sims|John]] confirms that the [[The Web]] has been using them from the start but he also finds it difficult to look closely at them. They discuss how to deal with [[Annabelle Cane|Annabelle]]. [[Basira Hussain|Basira]] wants to hear her out and [[Jonathan Sims|John]] tentatively agrees but warns that all bets are off if she hurts [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]].

In [[Hill Top Road]], [[Annabelle Cane|Annabelle]] senses that [[Jonathan Sims|John]] is getting close. She awkwardly tries to make small talk with [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]], who accuses her of using him as bait to lure [[Jonathan Sims|John]] into a trap. [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] and [[Annabelle Cane|Annabelle]] realize that [[Basira Hussain|Basira]] is with [[Jonathan Sims|John]], which [[Annabelle Cane|Annabelle]] says may complicate things. [[Annabelle Cane|Annabelle]], attempting to create a more dramatic atmosphere, gags [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] (much to his annoyance) and shape-shifts into a spider creature.

[[Basira Hussain|Basira]] and [[Jonathan Sims|John]] arrive in [[Hill Top Road]], and [[Jonathan Sims|John]] immediately tries to smite [[Annabelle Cane|Annabelle]]. She dangles [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] over the Rift, saying if he smites her, [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] will fall in. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] relents. [[Annabelle Cane|Annabelle]] explains that she wants to offer them a way out of the apocalypse, and sets [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] down. She explains that [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] came here of his own free will, and begins to explain her plan.

The post-[[The Change|change]] world is unsustainable, explains [[Annabelle Cane|Annabelle]], but only two of [[The Entities]] realize this: [[The End]] and [[The Web]]. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] recalls the [[MAG 168 - Roots|statement]] of [[Oliver Banks]]. [[Annabelle Cane|Annabelle]] explains that [[The Web]] has an escape plan, to ensure [[The Entities]] won't starve. She explains the nature of [[Gap in Reality|The Rift]], and reveals that their dimension is the only one among countless realities with [[The Entities|Entities]]. [[Annabelle Cane|Annabelle]] says that [[The Web]] intends to escape to the other dimensions, and by [[The Entities]]' natures, drag the others with it. She reveals that the Archivist was created by [[The Web]], which marked [[Jonathan Sims|John]] young and guided him, using the tapes to spin a web. She explains that if the plan works, the world will revert to normal, and the universes [[The Entities]] spread into will become like the world was before the Change, with [[The Entities]] being unable to directly manifest, working through [[Avatar|Avatars]]. [[Basira Hussain|Basira]] asks how they would do it, and [[Annabelle Cane|Annabelle]] tells them if they can destroy the Panopticon and [[Jonah Magnus|the pupil of the Eye]] simultaneously, the [[Tape Recorders]] would become a powerful anchor, and would drag [[The Entities]] through [[Gap in Reality|The Rift]].

[[Basira Hussain|Basira]] asks how they would destroy the Panopticon, and [[Annabelle Cane|Annabelle]] reveals that the gas main underneath [[The Magnus Institute]] is still intact. Igniting it will blow up the Panopticon. She reveals that [[Jonathan Sims|John]]'s [[Web Lighter|lighter]] is the perfect tool for the job. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] threatens to throw the [[Web Lighter|lighter]] into [[Gap in Reality|The Rift]], but [[Basira Hussain|Basira]] reminds him that [[Annabelle Cane|Annabelle]] still has [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]]. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] puts the [[Web Lighter|lighter]] back in his pocket, and [[Annabelle Cane|Annabelle]] releases [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]]. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] asks what's stopping him from throwing away the [[Web Lighter|lighter]] now, and [[Annabelle Cane|Annabelle]] tells him just his own indecision. She asks if [[Jonathan Sims|John]] still intends to kill her, but he just tells her to go. [[Basira Hussain|Basira]], [[Jonathan Sims|John]] and [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] leave to think about what to do next.
