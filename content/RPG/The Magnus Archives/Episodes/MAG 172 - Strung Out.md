# Pre-Statement

[[Jonathan Sims|John]] and [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] have travelled through a [[Domains|domain]] of [[The Web]], which appears to be a large theatre venue of a sort, and they are about to leave. They have a very brief discussion in which [[Jonathan Sims|John]] mentions that he needs to do a statement, and [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] asks if he has to as they have nearly left. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] says it is not up to him and [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] understands, though he seems on-edge and unhappy.

# Statement

The statement is presented as a play that is being performed onstage by Francis in front of a laughing audience. This is the 48,067th act of the play and it shows how Francis, who has recovered from drug addiction, is manipulated back into their addiction by a massive spider hanging above the stage.

Throughout the play various hooks attach to Francis, allowing the Spider to puppet them around the stage. Francis' parents, a friend, and a lover make brief appearances; their parents insult them, their friend says they do not smile when sober, and their lover tries to encourage Francis to take something with them. Francis repeatedly tries to turn away and leave but the Spider, talking to them throughout, continues to manipulate them into staying.

They end up seated before a table with various temptations, each of which contains tiny spiders. Francis is puppeteered into drinking a bottle. With a cry of exultation the Spider’s abdomen ruptures, drowning Francis in a cascade of spiders.

The scene ends.

# Post-Statement

The next scene begins. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] starts to narrate it but is then snapped out of it by [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] slapping him. [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] explains that he did so because it seemed that [[Jonathan Sims|John]] had already done his statement and because he thought he heard something. [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] says that he went exploring during the statement, to which [[Jonathan Sims|John]] responds with worry and asks if he was compelled to do so. [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] admits that he does not know if it was curiosity or manipulation that led him to explore. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] offers to Know for him but [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] declines, as he does not want [[Jonathan Sims|John]] looking in his mind.

[[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] asks what [[The Web]] wants and [[Jonathan Sims|John]] explains that although he can See that [[The Web]] has a plan, it is too intricate for him to comprehend. He cannot See [[Annabelle Cane]] either. The pair agree to leave [[The Web]]'s [[Domains|domain]].
