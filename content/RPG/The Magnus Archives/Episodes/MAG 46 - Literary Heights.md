# Statement

[[Herbert Knox]] owns a bookshop in Chichester called [[Lion Street Books]]. [[Michael Crew]], a student at the university, comes by on a semi regular basis to browse the older books. He is short and thin and has a branching scar up his neck that he normally keeps concealed.

The first time [[Michael Crew|Michael]] came to [[Lion Street Books]], he came looking for _[Dictionnaire infernal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dictionnaire_Infernal)_, by [Jacques de Plancy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacques_Collin_de_Plancy), the older the better. A smell appeared, similar to that before a storm breaks, and [[Michael Crew|Michael]] paid in cash and left. Three weeks later he purchased more books on demonology, each time that strange smell appeared and he quickly left. [[Herbert Knox|Herbert]] also started to notice that the light bulbs got brighter when [[Michael Crew|Michael]] was around and he would occasionally get shocked.

In January, [[Kirsten Bowman]], another book dealer in Salisbury, who [[Herbert Knox|Herbert]] had had a fling with many years ago, passed away, leaving her book collection to him. [[Herbert Knox|Herbert]] remarked that many book dealers had died recently, but the police were not able to find any connections between the deaths. Among [[Kirsten Bowman|Bowman's]] books was _[[Ex Altiora]]_, a [[Jurgen Leitner|Leitner]] tome. [[Herbert Knox|Herbert]] is first annoyed when he discovered that the book was a one of a kind as it makes it an artifact more fit for a museum than for sale which is more work than is worth the profit. But as he read the book, he became dizzy and nauseous. He even fell off his chair a few times and booked an appointment with his doctor to help with the dizzy spells.

A week later, [[Michael Crew|Michael]] returns to the shop and rejects the books [[Herbert Knox|Herbert]] had set aside for him to look at, although he doesn't find that behavior abnormal. However, when [[Michael Crew|Michael]] spots _[[Ex Altiora]]_, he states that he needs to buy it. [[Herbert Knox|Herbert]] described the furious desperation in [[Michael Crew|Michael]]'s expression and thought that [[Michael Crew|Michael]] would kill him to acquire the book. [[Herbert Knox|Herbert]] named an extremely high figure and [[Michael Crew|Michael]] wrote him a cheque before leaving quickly. When [[Herbert Knox|Herbert]] tried to cash the cheque it bounced, and he headed to [[Michael Crew|Michael]]'s house to confront him about it. The sky was overcast and seemed to be ready to storm. When he arrived, the room looked and smelt like [[Michael Crew|Michael]] hadn't left it in weeks, and there were pages of Latin all over the room. [[Michael Crew|Michael]] didn't acknowledge [[Herbert Knox|Herbert]], and as it started to rain he went pale and fled with _[[Ex Altiora]]_ in hand.

Giving chase, [[Herbert Knox|Herbert]] followed [[Michael Crew|Michael]] into the Chichester Cathedral bell tower, unclear on how or why the tower was unlocked. At its summit, he found [[Michael Crew|Michael]] holding _[[Ex Altiora]]_ in front of him, chanting in Latin, including "Vertigo" and "[[The Vast]]". A branching, glowing figure stood before him. As he crescendoed, he shouted, "I am yours" and jumped out of the window. The branching figure screamed and was pulled through the window with [[Michael Crew|Michael]]. The body of [[Michael Crew|Michael]] was not found, and from the outside the bell tower's windows are clearly sealed permanently shut.

# Post-Statement

[[Jonathan Sims]] believes that [[Michael Crew|Michael]] was using the book as some kind of ward, and says that [[Herbert Knox|Herbert]] didn't mention anything about the starless sky with the [[Lichtenberg Figure]] that [[MAG 4 - PAge Turner|Dominic Swain]] talked about. [[Jonathan Sims|Jonathan]] wonders if [[Michael Crew|Michael]] tried to use [[The Boneturner's Tale]] but ultimately found it didn't help.

# Supplemental

The trap door has been unlocked. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] suspects someone has been down there, although his colleagues deny anything. There is nothing amiss, although spiders have moved in and have eaten the worms. This disturbed [[Jonathan Sims|John]], and he left the tunnels.
