# Pre-Statement

[[Nikola Orsinov]] is using [[Jonathan Sims|John's]] [[Tape Recorders|tape recorder]], addressing [[Elias Bouchard]] directly, saying that she has taken [[Jonathan Sims|John]] and is holding him captive. She asks [[Breekon & Hope]] to leave, whereupon the pair allude to needing to stay close to "it" (implied to be the [[The Coffin|coffin]] from [[MAG 2 - Do Not Open]] and [[MAG 61 - Hard Shoulder]]), before withdrawing carrying something.

[[Nikola Orsinov|Nikola]] resumes talking to [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]] saying that she had planned to just follow [[Jonathan Sims|John]] around until he found the skin that [[Gertrude Robinson|Gertrude]] had taken, but then decided it would be more fun to take him directly. She tells [[Jonathan Sims|John]] that she is going to peel his skin off, and asks him what lotion she should get as he has not kept his skin in good condition. She leaves, presumably to acquire some, and turns the recorder off.

An unspecified time later, the recorder turns back on and Michael appears, saying he is here to kill [[Jonathan Sims|The Archivist]]. Before he does so he is going to act against his nature and answer his questions so that he can understand what is happening.

[[Jonathan Sims|John]] first asks how [[The Distortion|Michael]] found him, [[The Distortion|Michael]] responds that while [[The Eye]] watches, and [[The Stranger]] conceals, [[The Spiral|the power he serves]] deceives and nothing can be hidden from him.

The next question is what [[The Distortion|Michael]] has to do with [[The Unknowing]], but [[The Distortion|Michael]] laughs and says nothing - other than he would like it if it were to fail.

When asked why he is here, [[The Distortion|Michael]] repeats that it is to kill [[Jonathan Sims|John]]. Pressed on it, he states that he does not want [[Circus of the Other|the circus]] to win, but he also does not want [[The Magnus Institute|the Archives]] to win. Killing [[Jonathan Sims|John]] would accomplish both goals, but also satisfy his revenge.

[[Jonathan Sims|John]] says he still does not know who [[The Distortion|Michael]] is, and [[The Distortion|Michael]] says that he is [[The Distortion|Michael]], but that he was not always [[The Distortion|Michael]] and does not want to be [[The Distortion|Michael]]. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] thinks that [[The Distortion|Michael]] used to be [[Gertrude Robinson|Gertrude's]] assistant, but [[The Distortion|Michael]] says that the "[[The Distortion|Michael]]" on that tape was not him. When that "[[The Distortion|Michael]]" existed, the being that [[Jonathan Sims|John]] is talking to was something else. Then, someone caused "[[The Distortion|Michael]]" to become a part of him. He realises that [[Jonathan Sims|John]] is asking him to make a Statement and begins the story of what happened.

# Statement

[[The Distortion|Michael]] is not sure where to begin his story, as in some way he has always existed, so picking a starting point for his story is not simple. [[Michael Shelley]] would be easier to keep track of, as he was born and before he could die, he went to work for [[The Magnus Institute]]. When [[Michael Shelley|Shelley]] was younger, he lost a friend to a supernatural being which drove him to despair, [[The Magnus Institute|the Institute]], and [[Gertrude Robinson]].

[[Michael Shelley|Shelley]] was protective of Gertrude, believing her to be forgetful and frail yet wise, and he trusted her. [[The Distortion|Michael]] says that Gertrude fed [[Michael Shelley|Shelley]] to the Spiral to prevent the Transcendence. One day, Gertrude told [[Michael Shelley|Shelley]] that they were going on a research trip to Russia to visit Zemlya Sannikova, or [[Sannikov Land]](https://the-magnus-archives.fandom.com/wiki/Sannikov_Land "Sannikov Land"). As [[Michael Shelley|Shelley]] fully trusted Gertrude, he did not research their destination and remained unaware that Sannikov Land did not exist. They were taken by [[Peter Lukas]](https://the-magnus-archives.fandom.com/wiki/Peter_Lukas "Peter Lukas") on a further journey North, and once the ice froze over, [[Michael Shelley|Shelley]] finally began to be concerned.

Gertrude says that they were going to fight a great evil, which [[The Distortion|Michael]] notes was unfair as he was just acting in nature with what he is. [[The Worker-of-Clay]](https://the-magnus-archives.fandom.com/wiki/Gabriel "Gabriel") had spent decades creating an impossible edifice of doors in order to bring him forth into the world. The two of them landed on an island that [[The Distortion|Michael]] says did not exist, and they walked to stop the ritual by following the spiralling laughter.

Though [[Michael Shelley|Shelley]] was deeply unsettled by the sight of the ritual, [[The Distortion|Michael]] says that [[Gertrude Robinson|Gertrude]] Robinson was unafraid and sought out his door. [[The Distortion|Michael]] was reveling in his own apotheosis, and did not notice [[Gertrude Robinson|Gertrude]] walking up and opening the door. [[Gertrude Robinson|Gertrude]] told [[Michael Shelley|Shelley]] that [[The Distortion|Michael]] could be stopped by walking through the door, which [[Michael Shelley|Shelley]] had believed and walked through [[The Distortion|Michael]]'s door willingly.

Inside the labyrinth, [[Michael Shelley|Shelley]] brought with him a map given to him by [[Gertrude Robinson|Gertrude]] which guided him to [[The Distortion|Michael]]'s heart. By following the directions on the map, [[Michael Shelley|Shelley]] managed to tear out [[The Distortion|Michael]]'s heart, and become forever twisted together. All of The Spiral's worshippers were exiled by the force of the uncreation. The island also disappeared but [[Gertrude Robinson|Gertrude]] had already retreated back to the boat.

In the aftermath, the being that [[Jonathan Sims|John]] knows as [[The Distortion|Michael]] was born. He asks why [[The Distortion|Michael]] never took revenge on [[Gertrude Robinson|Gertrude]], and is told that [[Gertrude Robinson|Gertrude]] had taken protections, but also that she was not as good an Archivist as [[Jonathan Sims|John]] and thus not as large a threat. [[The Distortion|Michael]] had hoped [[Jonathan Sims|John]] would be able to stop [[The Unknowing]], but now it seems like he will be bringing it about instead.

[[Jonathan Sims|John]] asks if there is anything he can do to stop [[The Distortion|Michael]] from killing him, [[The Distortion|Michael]] says if he screams he will alert The Circus and that his death would be more painful that way. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] agrees to [[The Distortion|Michael]]'s offer, and [[The Distortion|Michael]] conjures a door and tells him to step through. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] tries the door but finds it is locked, [[The Distortion|Michael]] does not believe him and also tries it. He becomes worried and seems to realise something before letting out a long, pained scream.

[[The Distortion|Michael]] is gone and a door creaks open to reveal [[Helen Richardson]]. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] asks what happened to [[The Distortion|Michael]] and [[The Distortion|Helen]] says "[[The Distortion|Michael]] isn't me," anymore because he let himself be overwhelmed and "lost my way." She is not sure if she is [[The Distortion|Helen]] either, but [[The Distortion|Helen]] is better than [[The Distortion|Michael]] at least. Killing [[Jonathan Sims|John]] was [[The Distortion|Michael]]'s desire, but [[The DIstortion|Helen]] will help him leave because [[Helen Richardson]] liked him. Out of options, he agrees to follow her through the door.
