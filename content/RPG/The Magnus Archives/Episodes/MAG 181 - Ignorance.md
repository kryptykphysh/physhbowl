# Pre-Statement

[[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] and [[Jonathan Sims|John]] awake in [Upton House](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Upton_House,_Warwickshire), apparently having slept for 71 hours and begin to talk with [[Mikaele Salesa]]. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] realises that he is unable to Know anything or use compulsion.

When pressed on it Salesa tells them that though he knows [[Annabelle Cane]] works for [[The Web]] and may have bad intentions, he feels no need to try and make her leave as she mainly keeps to herself and even cooks for him. He is also fairly sure he could not make her leave if he tried. Additionally, he wants her to be kindly disposed enough towards him to kill him quickly in the event that her plans destroy his bubble of safety.

[[Mikaele Salesa|Salesa]] says that he made the bubble of Upton House but refuses when [[Jonathan Sims|John]] uses compulsion, as [[The Eye]]'s power does not work here, and says he wants to tell his story at his own pace.

He reveals that he is isolated from [[The Change]] and does not actually know what has become of the world, as he spent years preparing for this.

# Statement

[[Mikaele Salesa|Salesa]] started as an assistant to [[Jurgen Leitner]] and learned about [[The Entities]] from him, but left before the attack on [[Jurgen Leitner|Leitner]]'s library to try and bury his supernatural knowledge. He became a wealthy and well known smuggler that dealt in non-entity-related items but found himself unable to stay away from what he had learned.

He started dealing in entity aligned [[Artefacts]] when he found a, "Leather pouch, filled with 1888 Morgan silver dollars," that was aligned with [[The Slaughter]]. He found that in selling these artefacts he was able to walk away unharmed from the fate that befell the buyers without serving an entity and with even more wealth.

[[Mikaele Salesa|Salesa]] admits that part of the reason he became an artefact dealer is because of how characters of his trade were portrayed in fiction, "and how rarely the merchant themself is ever punished in those stories."

[[Mikaele Salesa|Salesa]] stayed neutral to the entities. He was somewhat close to [[The Stranger]] but stayed out of it given his extroverted personality and desire to have his name known. As his business grew, he became increasingly afraid that with the rise of human population a [[Rituals|ritual]] would eventually be able to succeed. He sought to insulate himself from this and specifically began to track items that would protect against the entities.

At the start of his career he found and sold, "An old broken camera," that had the ability to hide its owner from all other powers. He expended a great deal of resources to find it again and [[MAG 141 - Doomed Voyage|faked his death while retrieving it]]. He spent a long time building his protection wondering if he was overreacting but is now relieved that he was right and can live his apocalyptic retirement in peace.

# Post-Statement

[[Jonathan Sims|John]] becomes increasingly unresponsive and sick the longer he and [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] stay in the house and so, despite [[Mikaele Salesa|Salesa]]'s offer for them to stay as long as they like in the peace, they leave.

Annabelle confronts them as they leave and [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] becomes angry with her. He is upset by her attempts at manipulating them and wants answers but [[Jonathan Sims|John]] is too disorientated to help him. Annabelle says that her phone calls to [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] were hasty and not as planned as they could have been but [[Jonathan Sims|John]] points out that this may be an attempt to humanize herself to them so that they may underestimate her. [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] is still unhappy and angry with her but resolves to leave.

Once outside the house [[Jonathan Sims|John]] is revitalized again and able to hold a conversation. They both mourn the loss of the peace that was in the house but [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] refuses [[Jonathan Sims|John]]'s offer to let him stay there, he would rather keep trying to help fix things with [[Jonathan Sims|John]] than be alone and in ignorance. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] is unable to remember much of what happened due to being cut off from The Eye and asks [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] to fill him in. The episode ends with [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] describing the house wistfully.
