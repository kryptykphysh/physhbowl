# [[Elias Bouchard|Elias']] Statement

On Tuesday [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]] had been going over the budgets when the fire alarm went off. As he started to evacuate, [[Sasha James|Sasha]] appeared with a tape recorder saying she had set off the fire alarms to get everyone away from [[Jane Prentiss]]. [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]] agreed that they would have to go and turn on the fire suppression system, but as they left they were confronted with a huge wave of worms and the two became separated. Eventually, [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]] got to the fire suppression system, now filled with a CO2 suppressing agent, and eventually turned it on. He then heard a scream, "Like tens of thousands of things without mouths screaming as one".

An hour later, [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] burst out of the trap door where [[Timothy Stoker|Tim]] and [[Jonathan Sims|John]] found [[Jane Prentiss]], saying that he had found the body of [[Gertrude Robinson]]. [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]] called the police.

[[Elias Bouchard|Elias]] was there when the ECDC burnt [[Jane Prentiss]]' body.

During the statement [[Jonathan Sims|John]] demands [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]] tell him what happened to [[Gertrude Robinson|Gertrude]]. [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]] says that on March 15, he had gone to find [[Gertrude Robinson|Gertrude]], but there was no sign of her and her desk was covered in a gallon of her blood. As she had no assistants and the police could not find anything, he decided she must be dead and looked into hiring a replacement.

# [[Timothy Stoker|Tim]]'s Statement

On Tuesday, he came back into the Archives after lunch to find it very quiet. [[Sasha James|Sasha]] ran out of the safe room and [[Timothy Stoker|Tim]] saw [[Jane Prentiss]] who tried to say something to him. [[Timothy Stoker|Tim]] told [[Sasha James|Sasha]] to run and get help and he tried to escape into the office, but found more worms. He fell on some CO2 canisters and used them to kill all the worms in that room.

Afterwards the paramedics said he may have been suffering from respiratory acidosis from the lack of oxygen, as he became light headed and dazed.

He proceeded through the hole in the wall into the maze like tunnels below the Archives, which were cold and dry with a rotten earthy smell. There were worms here, but they were faster than the ones in the Archives and they did not make the same noise. [[Timothy Stoker|Tim]] suspected that there was something in the Archives that slowed them down, maybe the light or air-con. They had clearly been down there for months and it is likely that [[Jonathan Sims|John]] finding the passage forced them into action.

The tunnels below the Archives were maze-like, although he did not find [[Gertrude Robinson|Gertrude]]'s body, he found a room where there were many worms forming into a ring or a door. He killed them with the CO2.

# [[Sasha James|Sasha]]'s Statement

[[Sasha James|Sasha]] explains how she pulled the fire alarm to get people to evacuate before going to [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]]. When the fire suppression system went off, she heard Jane scream. She went into the tunnels and found the worms, including the ones that were burrowing into [[Timothy Stoker|Tim]] and [[Jonathan Sims|John]], dead. She removed the dead worms from her colleagues and took them outside where they could get fresh air.

[[Elias Bouchard|Elias]] arrived with the police, the fire brigade, and men in hazmat suits. [[Sasha James|Sasha]] notes that [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]] was giving her an odd look. An hour later they heard [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] screaming about [[Gertrude Robinson|Gertrude]]'s body.

[[Sasha James|Sasha]] claims that the tape recorder was dropped and that the tape must have been ejected, so there is no official record of the events in the artefact storage room.[[[[1]]]](https://the-magnus-archives.fandom.com/wiki/MAG_40:_Human_Remains#cite_note-1) [[Jonathan Sims|John]] notes that [[Sasha James|Sasha]] looks rather "out of it".

# [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]]'s Statement

[[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] was checking the background of case 0081709 when the worms arrived. He accidentally got split up from [[Timothy Stoker|Tim]] and [[Jonathan Sims|John]] in the tunnels and got lost in their maze-like structure. He felt like he was going away from the Archives, but could still hear [[Jane Prentiss]]' death scream. Eventually he found a door to a room, inside of which were cardboard boxes with tapes in them and the body of [[Gertrude Robinson|Gertrude]] Robinson. Everything was covered in dust and although [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] says he does not know what killed her, when pressed by [[Jonathan Sims|John]] he says that she was shot 3 times in the chest.

# Post-Statement

[[Jonathan Sims|John]] notes that [[Gertrude Robinson|Gertrude]] was murdered and there was no supernatural involvement, and it was probably done by someone in [[The Magnus Institute]]. He states that there must be some deeper mystery to the statements, which [[Gertrude Robinson|Gertrude]] was killed for uncovering.

Some of the case files are missing. It is not clear what happened to them, as [[Jane Prentiss|Jane]] was busy destroying written copies of statements, but cases [[MAG 24 - Strange Music|0051701]] and [[MAG 26 - A Distortion|0160204]] are gone.

[[Jonathan Sims|John]] says he will figure out what is going on and nothing will stop him.
