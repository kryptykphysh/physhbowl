# Pre-Statement

[[Jonathan Sims|John]], [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]], and [[Basira Hussain|Basira]] are in a [[Domains|domain]] of [[The Flesh]], which is a massive factory containing millions of real and fake humans. It feeds off the fear of you and your products being useless.

[[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] asks [[Basira Hussain|Basira]] and [[Jonathan Sims|John]] to quiet down, and [[Jonathan Sims|John]] reminds him that they cannot hear him. [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] argues that they are still human, to which [[Jonathan Sims|John]] replies that most of them technically are not. He explains that the fear this domain feeds on requires massive agonizing queues, requiring far more people than the amount of humans inside the [[Domains|domain]].

He says that around 1% of the beings they can see are real humans, and he can point them out if they want. [[Basira Hussain|Basira]] prefers not to know. She asks about the [[Avatar]], to which [[Jonathan Sims|John]] replies that it is not anyone they have seen, and they are in the main processing room, somewhere none of them want to be. [[Basira Hussain|Basira]] asks about [[Alice Daisy Tonner|Daisy's]] location, and [[Jonathan Sims|John]] replies that they are getting closer, she has been here, and to exit they need to pass another one of her victims.

[[Jonathan Sims|John]] feels another statement coming on, and asks them to enter a storage closet while he reads it. [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] opens the door, and is disgusted and horrified by the instruments inside. He urges [[Jonathan Sims|John]] inside while he and [[Basira Hussain|Basira]] remain outside. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] obliges, saying that it could be worse; at least the hooks are clean.

# Statement

Tyler waits in a line. He feels like he has been waiting there for years, and he cannot recall much of his life before being in the processing line. He wants to escape, but knows that attempting to do so will only lead to his body going to waste.

He approaches a desk, behind which is a person wearing a loose hood of coarse black leather, with a featureless mask of the same material without a gap for eyes or mouth or even the shape of a nose. They hand Tyler a form which he fills out by scratching in the information with his fingernails.

Tyler waits in another line for an indeterminate length of time before being lead into another room where he is weighed, measured, and branded.

Tyler waits in the final line, which seems to be much quicker than the others. In the room before the killing floor, Tyler uses a marker to mark cuts of meat on his body. Finally, Tyler is paralyzed and slowly dismembered by the butchers, who then throw his meat into the garbage and remark that he is useless.

# Post-Statement

The group finds the body of [[Alice Daisy Tonner|Daisy's]] latest victim, [[Isabelle Moran]]. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] tells [[Basira Hussain|Basira]] that she needs to accept all the horrible things that [[Alice Daisy Tonner|Daisy]] did before she kills her. [[Basira Hussain|Basira]] gets defensive but says she knows that she ignored everything [[Alice Daisy Tonner|Daisy]] did. [[Basira Hussain|Basira]] says that she wanted to help people when she first joined the police, but seeing what people were capable of changed her, and she wanted to hurt those who she felt deserved it. She says that [[Alice Daisy Tonner|Daisy]] was always there for her.

[[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] asks if [[Alice Daisy Tonner|Daisy]]'s victims were innocent, and [[Jonathan Sims|John]] explains that even the guilty ones didn't deserve what happened to them. [[Basira Hussain|Basira]] says that [[Alice Daisy Tonner|Daisy]] was trying to be a better person. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] responds that although that was true, even [[Alice Daisy Tonner|Daisy]] had never asked him for forgiveness. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] elaborates that he had never felt as scared in his life as when [[Alice Daisy Tonner|Daisy]] almost killed him in [[MAG 91 - The Coming Storm]]. [[Basira Hussain|Basira]] asks [[Jonathan Sims|John]] if he would've forgiven [[Alice Daisy Tonner|Daisy]], [[Jonathan Sims|John]] says he wouldn't have, but that [[Alice Daisy Tonner|Daisy]] knew what she did was unforgivable.

[[Basira Hussain|Basira]] acknowledges that she will have to kill [[Alice Daisy Tonner|Daisy]]. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] tells [[Basira Hussain|Basira]] that [[Alice Daisy Tonner|Daisy]] is happy in her current state.
