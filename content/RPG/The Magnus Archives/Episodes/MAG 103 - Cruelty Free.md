# Statement

[[Dylan Anderson|Anderson]] operated a vineyard in the Marlborough Forest, and kept a few pigs on the side. One day during the height of the Merlot harvest, a massive "monster pig" appeared in the sty, though no one at the farm recalled where it came from. The monster pig weighed a good three or four hundred kilograms, and [[Dylan Anderson|Anderson]] sensed malice from it almost immediately.

The monster pig ate far more than any of the other pigs, and eventually killed and ate the others, starting with one called Toby that [[Dylan Anderson|Anderson]] was very fond of.

A few months after the appearance of the monster pig, [[Dylan Anderson|Anderson]]'s brother [[Kurt Anderson|Kurt]] came to visit from London. The first night of [[Kurt Anderson|Kurt]]'s visit, [[Dylan Anderson|Anderson]] found him outside the pigsty, apparently sleepwalking. [[Kurt Anderson|Kurt]] kept chanting, "Long pig, short pig, wide pig, narrow pig" and appeared to be trying to enter the sty. [[Dylan Anderson|Anderson]] woke him, and [[Kurt Anderson|Kurt]] returned home after just a few days.

[[Dylan Anderson|Anderson]] had an arrangement with the [[Carley Brothers Circus]] from Australia: he let them use one of his fields as a staging area while they were bringing over their equipment and personnel for a tour of New Zealand. During this time, one of Carley Brothers' clowns, a man named [[Angus Dale]], vanished. [[Dylan Anderson|Anderson]] immediately assumed that the monster pig had eaten him.

He began having a recurring nightmare in which Dale, in his clown costume, chewed on his own flesh while occasionally chanting "Long pig, short pig, wide pig, narrow pig." The morning after the first nightmare, he found a human femur in the pigsty. Terrified, he laid down on the ground, assuming the monster pig would eat him, too. However, the pig just lay down next to [[Dylan Anderson|Anderson]], as if to indicate it considered him a friend.

Shortly after this, [[Gertrude Robinson]] arrived, looking into [[Angus Dale]]'s disappearance. When [[Dylan Anderson|Anderson]] explained the situation to her, she suggested surrounding the pigsty in scrap metal and burying the monster pig in a block of concrete, which [[Dylan Anderson|Anderson]] did. He considers engraving some kind of memorial on the concrete to the pigs (and clown) that the monster ate.

# Post-Statement

[[Jonathan Sims]] assumes [[Gertrude Robinson|Gertrude]] was looking for evidence of [[The Unknowing]]; certainly a mysterious disappearance linked to a circus would have drawn her attention. [[Angus Dale]] was never found, and the case has had no new leads since a possible sighting in 2016.

[[Jonathan Sims|John]] approaches [[Kurt Anderson|Kurt]] [[Dylan Anderson|Anderson]] to ask about his brother. [[Kurt Anderson|Kurt]] reveals that [[Dylan Anderson|Dylan]] had sent him a large parcel of documents and other items [[Gertrude Robinson|Gertrude]] had left at his farm, which he never got around to returning to her. He is unwilling to hand over the parcel to [[Jonathan Sims|John]] until he compels him to give up his darkest secret: he sometimes takes bribes in his work as a traffic warden. Armed with this blackmail material, [[Jonathan Sims|John]] asks again for the parcel.

Later, [[Jonathan Sims|John]] meets with [[Alice Daisy Tonner|Daisy]] in the tunnels under [[The Magnus Institute]], where he suspects [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]] will not easily be able to spy on them. He asks her to keep an eye on the others while he is abroad, and they begin to discuss what it would take to persuade the police to move on [[Elias Boucahrd|Elias]]. [[Alice Daisy Tonner|Daisy]] suggests they should not be talking about it with a running [[Tape Recorders|tape recorder]], and the tape ends.
