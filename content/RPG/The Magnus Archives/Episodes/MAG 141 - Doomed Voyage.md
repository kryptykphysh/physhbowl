# Pre-Statement

[[Basira Hussain|Basira]] and [[Jonathan Sims|John]] are aboard a ship on their way to disrupt [[The Dark's]] [[Rituals|ritual]] plans. [[Basira Hussain|Basira]] is suffering from seasickness. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] asks a shiphand when they are expected to arrive and is told it will be another two days. As the hand leaves, [[Jonathan Sims|John]] notices a recorder has appeared, indicating something is about to happen.

[[Jonathan Sims|John]] realizes the shiphand has potentially important information. He confronts the man, [[Floyd Matharu]], who [[Jonathan Sims|John]] realises was part of the _[[Dorian]]_'s crew serving under [[Mikaele Salesa]] from 2011 to 2014. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] Compels the reluctant [[Floyd Matharu|Matharu]] to give a statement about his service and [[Mikaele Salesa|Salesa's]] death, despite [[Basira Hussain|Basira]]'s warnings.

# Statement

[[Floyd Matharu|Floyd]] says that [[Mikaele Salesa|Salesa]] was a good boss. He treated his people well, and never lied to them about the illegal things they were doing. They trusted him because he knew what he was doing. It was a weird ship to be on, because the Captain, Gaultier, reported to [[Mikaele Salesa|Salesa]], who was not even really a part of the crew. It was a weird dynamic, [[Floyd Matharu|Floyd]] says, but it never caused any problems.

[[Mikaele Salesa|Salesa]] was the only person ever allowed in the cargo bay during a voyage. Only one person ever tried to break that rule, a man named Jésus, from Colombia. [[Mikaele Salesa|Salesa]] threw Jésus overboard when he caught him trying to enter the cargo bay, and none of the crew was unhappy he did it, because they all saw what happened when someone else got in the cargo bay.

[[Floyd Matharu|Floyd]]'s last voyage with [[Mikaele Salesa|Salesa]] was the trip that killed [[Mikaele Salesa|Salesa]], seven years ago. [[Mikaele Salesa|Salesa]] seemed tired. He had been doing this job for as long as anyone could remember. [[Floyd Matharu|Floyd]] once found him poring over a photo album of the ship, with a different captain and crew, who [[Mikaele Salesa|Salesa]] said were all "dead now".

That was a month before Gantulga, a fellow crew member, died. They took on cargo that a crew member told [[Floyd Matharu|Floyd]] was a carpet from a Burmese beggar woman who fed children to a crocodile head. That night, [[Floyd Matharu|Floyd]] was woken by the sound of Gantulga screaming as he was being attacked by something [[Floyd Matharu|Floyd]] describes as a "pattern". [[Floyd Matharu|Floyd]] screamed and fell, knocking himself out on a table. When he woke a few moments later, [[Mikaele Salesa|Salesa]] was there, dragging Gantulga onto the deck. [[Floyd Matharu|Floyd]] followed and watched as [[Mikaele Salesa|Salesa]] threw Gantulga and what looked like a blank rug overboard.

[[Mikaele Salesa|Salesa]] spent the next two days drunk, and disappeared when they landed in port. When he returned, he was full of "wild energy", and [[Floyd Matharu|Floyd]] was worried about the next job. [[Mikaele Salesa|Salesa]] called the crew together and said he was retiring, but he wanted them to do one last job to send them off with a big payday. They sailed to the Maldives, where the plan was for [[Mikaele Salesa|Salesa]] and four crew members to take a small boat to land on one of the islands to pick up the cargo. The rest of the crew was to prepare the ship to leave as quickly as possible once [[Mikaele Salesa|Salesa]] returned, and they should be prepared to defend the ship should anyone attempt to stop them.

When [[Mikaele Salesa|Salesa]] returned, only two of the four crew members were with him. [[Floyd Matharu|Floyd]] got a brief look at the cargo and says no matter how many times he goes over it in his head, it just looked like an old camera with a broken lens. As the ship prepared to leave, [[Floyd Matharu|Floyd]] looked over at the island and saw that in the moment he had looked away, a storm had brewed, covering the area in clouds and lightning. In the flashes from the lightning, [[Floyd Matharu|Floyd]] could see the trees start to catch fire, and he saw a huge shape moving in the water, starting to surge up and surround the island. [[Floyd Matharu|Floyd]] closed his eyes and fell to the deck, and when he looked again, the island was gone.

When the crew returned to port, [[Mikaele Salesa|Salesa]] insisted on throwing a party, saying how much he would miss everyone when he was retired. That night a huge explosion rocked the port. [[Floyd Matharu|Floyd]] says that, though there was no reason to suspect it, they all knew it had something to do with them. No one could find the Captain or [[Mikaele Salesa|Salesa]]. As they all stood around arguing what to do, the Captain reappeared, clothes torn and hair matted with blood. He commanded them to get back on the ship and they left with haste. When anyone asked about [[Mikaele Salesa|Salesa]], the Captain just shook his head. They were able to piece together that [[Mikaele Salesa|Salesa]] and the Captain had gone to deliver the artefact, but they had been betrayed and [[Mikaele Salesa|Salesa]] was dead.

The Captain died soon after, taking the secret of exactly what happened with him to the grave. The crew broke up after that, although [[Floyd Matharu|Floyd]] thinks some of them managed to retain ownership of the Dorian.

# Post-Statement

[[Jonathan Sims|John]] thanks [[Floyd Matharu|Matharu]] for telling his story, and gently tells the upset and confused [[Floyd Matharu|Matharu]] to leave and take a break. [[Basira Hussain|Basira]] criticises [[Jonathan Sims|John]] for using his powers to forcibly extract [[Floyd Matharu|Matharu's]] story, subjecting the man to a future of nightmares.

[[Jonathan Sims|John]] retorts that [[Floyd Matharu|Matharu]] would not have told his story willingly and that [[Jonathan Sims|John]] needed it, both for the intel and to keep his own strength up. He appeals to [[Gertrude Robinson|Gertrude's]] ruthless but effective example. [[Basira Hussain|Basira]] reminds him that he, unlike [[Gertrude Robinson|Gertrude]], used to care about the human cost of his actions.

[[Jonathan Sims|John]] accuses [[Basira Hussain|Basira]] of wanting [[Floyd Matharu|Floyd]]'s story too and holds her responsible for not stopping him.
