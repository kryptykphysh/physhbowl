# Pre-Statement

[[Jonathan Sims|John]] and [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] have relocated to one of [[Alice Daisy Tonner|Daisy's]] safe houses in Scotland and are laying low after the events in the [[Panopticon]]. They have been there for a few weeks when [[Basira Hussain|Basira]] manages to send them some statements, as sustenance for [[Jonathan Sims|John]]. [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] leaves to give him some privacy as he starts to read.

# Statement

The statement appears to be from a [[Hazel Rutter]], regarding a fire in her childhood home, but the opening is a fake-out. The statement is actually from [[Jonah Magnus]] and having started it, [[Jonathan Sims|John]] is now unable to stop reading.

It is the statement of [[Jonah Magnus]], regarding [[Jonathan Sims]], [[The Archivist]].

[[Jonah Magnus|Magnus]] bares his ambitions. He will condemn the world to an eternity of suffering in order to gain immortality and power, and he feels nothing but satisfaction in that choice.

He describes the events that set him on this path. He learned about [[The Entities]] from [[Robert Smirke]], alongside Lukas, Scott and other unnamed scholars. As he saw his fellow students fall to their influence, he feared that one of them might complete a [[Rituals|ritual]]. Though he initially sought to simply prevent this, [[Jonah Magnus|Magnus]] eventually concluded that the only way to guarantee that he would never fall victim to someone else's ritual was to complete his own ritual first.

He convinced [[Robert Smirke|Smirke]] to build [[Millbank Prison]] as a temple to all of the entities, and made his own modifications to the [[Panopticon]]. The dread of the prisoners seeped into the [[Panopticon]], and years later, [[Jonah Magnus|Magnus]] attempted the [[Rituals|Watcher's Crown]]. It failed, killing all the inmates, but granted him semi-omniscience. Wanting to keep the site under his control, he moved [[The Magnus Institute]] to its current location above the tunnels.

[[Jonah Magnus|Magnus]] prolonged his life through body hopping as he spent years preventing other rituals while trying to figure out what went wrong with his own. Things changed when [[Gertrude Robinson]] became the Archivist. She was different from the other archivists, not interested in collecting statements but single-mindedly dedicated to preventing rituals. By now [[Jonah Magnus|Magnus]] suspected that no ritual had succeeded because of some underlying problem with the actual ritual, rather than outside interference, and [[Gertrude Robinson|Gertrude]] had the same suspicion.

She made no effort to stop [[The People's Church of the Divine Host]] and their efforts to complete the [[Extinguished Sun]] in order to see if the ritual would collapse on its own. It did, and [[Jonah Magnus|Magnus]] realized the problem with the rituals: The entities could never be truly separated from each other. They overlapped far too much and even those that seemed to exist in direct opposition needed the contrast of each other to define themselves. For example, a world with only The Buried would make as much sense as a world where the only direction was down.

With this knowledge he devised a new ritual, one that would summon all 14 Fears simultaneously. At the heart of this was the Archivist, someone who could serve as a living record of fear. To complete the ritual, [[Jonah Magnus|Magnus]] needed them to be marked by all 14 Fears, in both mind and body. The former could be easily accomplished by feeding them the appropriate statements but the latter would take considerably more planning.

[[Jonah Magnus|Magnus]] saw [[Jonathan Sims|John]] as an excellent candidate for the position as he was already marked by [[The Web]] when he joined [[The Magnus Institute]]. At first he simply observed him and waited for [[The Magnus Institute|The Institute]] to be attacked, wanting to test [[Jonathan Sims|John]]'s mettle in a life-or-death situation. When [[Jane Prentiss]] attacked, [[Jonah Magnus|Magnus]] waited to activate the fire suppression system until the [[Silver Worms|worms]] had burrowed into [[Jonathan Sims|John]] to ensure that he was marked by [[The Corruption]].

He was also aware of [[Not-Sasha]] and saw it as another opportunity, [[Jonathan Sims|John]]'s close encounter with her and his experience during [[The Unknowing]] left him marked by [[The Stranger]].

Next, [[Jonah Magnus|Magnus]] arranged for [[Helen Richardson]] to give a statement, thus bringing [[Jonathan Sims|John]] to [[The Distortion]]'s attention. Multiple encounters with [[The Distortion]] left him deeply marked by [[The Spiral]].

[[Jonah Magnus|Magnus]] had not anticipated the arrival of [[Jurgen Leitner]], and, as he could have revealed too much to [[Jonathan Sims|John]], [[Jonah Magnus|Magnus]] killed him. This also served as an excuse to send [[Jonathan Sims|John]] out into the world, where he could be marked by further entities.

[[Jonah Magnus|Magnus]] requested [[Alice Daisy Tonner|Daisy]] was assigned to [[Jurgen Leitner|Leitner's]] murder, knowing that she was connected to [[The Hunt]]. Additionally, he sent [[Jonathan Sims|John]] statements that would lead him to other avatars, and [[Jonathan Sims|John]] was marked by [[The Desolation]] and [[The Vast]] through interactions with [[Jude Perry]] and [[Michael Crew]]. Though [[Jonah Magnus|Magnus]] did not send [[Melanie King|Melanie]] to her encounter with [[The Slaughter]], he took advantage of the situation and bound her to the institute, knowing [[Jonathan Sims|John]] would be a good target for her anger, resulting in his being marked by [[The Slaughter]] during [[Melanie King|Melanie]]'s surgery.

[[The Unknowing]] served to test [[Jonathan Sims|John]]'s power, and also resulted in him being marked by [[The End]] when he was left near-dead in a coma. [[Jonah Magnus|Magnus]] had planned to go into hiding after [[The Unknowing]], as he worried [[Jonathan Sims|John]]'s growing power would allow him to prematurely figure out the plan if they met face-to-face. Going to jail was an unexpected but acceptable deviation as it ultimately served the same purpose.

Only four marks remained: [[The Dark]], [[The Flesh]], [[The Buried]], and [[The Lonely]]. [[Jonah Magnus|Magnus]] pushed [[Jared Hopworth]] to attack [[The Magnus Institute|the Institute]], but was annoyed when it happened prior to [[Jonathan Sims|John]]'s release from the hospital. In the end, [[Jonathan Sims|John]] went to confront him voluntarily, and came out of it marked by [[The Flesh]]. Once [[The Coffin]] turned up, [[Jonah Magnus|Magnus]] got [[Basira Hussain|Basira]] out of town so [[Jonathan Sims|John]] would enter it and become marked by [[The Buried]]. Knowing the [[Dark Star]] was sitting around, [[Jonah Magnus|Magnus]] manipulated [[Jonathan Sims|John]] and [[Basira Hussain|Basira]], sending them to [[Ny-Ålesund]] to find it, resulting in [[Jonathan Sims|John]] being marked by [[The Dark]].

Finally came [[The Lonely]]. [[Peter Lukas]] knew [[Jonah Magnus|Magnus]]' plan and was unwilling to help until [[Jonah Magnus|Magnus]] made him a bet about [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]]. Thus [[Jonathan Sims|John]]'s foray into [[The Lonely]] provided the final mark.

[[Jonathan Sims|John]] is now ready, he is marked, and with [[The Eye]]'s power within him it is time for the world to be remade. [[Jonah Magnus|Magnus]] forces [[Jonathan Sims|John]] to read the incantation, summoning all the [[Fears]].

The ritual is complete.

[[Jonathan Sims|John]] knows that what is happening outside is happening everywhere, the whole world is afraid because of what he did. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] tells [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] to look at the sky.

It is looking back.
