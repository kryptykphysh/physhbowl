# Statement

[[Sunil Maraj]] quit their job last week. They were not fired, they just could not keep working there after what happened to [[Samson Stiller|Samson]], and the company would rather they stop asking questions about it.

[[Sunil Maraj|Sunil]] worked as a security guard alongside [[Samson Stiller|Samson]] Stiller. They had gotten unlucky with their contract and been shuffled between numerous sites in just two years. It worked just fine for [[Sunil Maraj|Sunil]] but it was hard on [[Samson Stiller|Samson]] as he had a three year old and lived down in Morden. When they are eventually reassigned to a shopping centre in Stratford, [[Samson Stiller|Samson]] is not in a great place.

The security office is a mess of mismatched technology and jumbled cables. [[Samson Stiller|Samson]] sets about sorting it out and actually gets it working decently. There is one piece of equipment that still refuses to cooperate, an old Tecton multicamera recorder from the late 80s. He swallows his pride and looks for the manual. It is a small thing, no more than ten pages of A5 paper, worn and yellowed, with [[Jurgen Leitner|someone's name]] written on the front. [[Sunil Maraj|Sunil]] swears that they see a picture of themself in it when glancing at it.

[[Samson Stiller|Samson]] seems almost entranced by it and from that point he grows increasingly creepy. He is _always_ at work and simply brushes it off when asked about it. [[Sunil Maraj|Sunil]] always finds him staring wide-eyed at the monitors, drinking it all in.

They start to feel watched when they are in the actual shopping centre. They know where all the cameras are, and keep an eye on them. Many of the cameras are fixed, looking at one point, but [[Sunil Maraj|Sunil]] sees them swivel impossibly to focus on them. They throw a can of deodorant at one of them and for the next two days, [[Samson Stiller|Samson]] wears sunglasses. [[Sunil Maraj|Sunil]] catches a glimpse of his eyes and there is a crack right down the centre of one of them.

[[Sunil Maraj|Sunil]] is not at work when [[Samson Stiller|Samson]] disappears. They get a frantic call from Dave, another security guard. He is incoherent, repeating [[Samson Stiller|Samson]]’s name and asking “what do we do with his eyes?”. [[Sunil Maraj|Sunil]] does not know what to say and hangs up after Dave says “He won’t stop, we can’t get rid of his face.” while sobbing.

When they go back to work, both Dave and [[Samson Stiller|Samson]] are gone. Several other workers also quit suddenly and [[Sunil Maraj|Sunil]] is not able to find out what happened as no one will answer their questions. They never see [[Samson Stiller|Samson]] again, just find his shredded work shirt wrapped around the old instruction manual. [[Sunil Maraj|Sunil]] throws the shirt away and sticks the manual back in a cabinet.

They start asking too many questions after that. Why had [[Samson Stiller|Samson]] not signed out of the building before disappearing? Why, no matter who tried to reset the security system, did it always log back in as him? Why, whenever they were watching the monitors alone, did they see [[Samson Stiller|Samson]] on one of the old CRT screens. Staring right back at them, quietly calling for [[Sunil Maraj|Sunil]] to join him.
