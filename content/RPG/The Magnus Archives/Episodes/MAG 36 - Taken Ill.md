# Statement

[[Nicole Baxter]] works for [[Baxter & Gordon]], a funeral directors in Manchester. Her uncle, [[George Baxter]], founded the business with [[William Gordon]] who died 5 years ago, but the two founders fell out later in life. She works with her cousin, Josh. [[Nicole Baxter|Nicole]] tends to work with clients, as [[George Baxter|George]] has become less sociable in his old age.

Although she worked with various care homes, [[Nicoles Baxter|Nicole]] liked [[Ivy Measdows Care Home|Ivy Meadows]] the most, as the patients there were very friendly and lively. The building used to be a country house and homes around 30 people appointed by the state.

In mid-August, [[Nicoles Baxter|Nicole]] received a call from [[Alenka Kozel]] to say that [[Bertrand Miller]] had "taken ill" and passed away, and was about to say more before she was cut off. [[Nicoles Baxter|Nicole]] and Josh drove to the house to find that [[Hannah Ramirez]], the lady who ran the home, had left 3 months earlier. When they arrived at the home, there was no one around and no cars in the car park. The door unlocked remotely and the Baxters entered.

Inside, the beige walls were dirty with dark marks about hand height. The bell on the reception desk was covered in a greasy residue. When [[Nicoles Baxter|Nicole]] rang the bell, a tall man stepped out from a side room. He wore an ill-fitting brown suit, had pale blue eyes and dark messy hair. He introduced himself as [[John Amherst|John]], offering his hand to Josh, who was unnerved when a fly landed on [[John Amherst|John's]] face and crawled across his eye without a reaction. [[John Amherst|John]] asked who they were and why they had come. When [[Nicoles Bater|Nicole]] explained about the phone call, [[John Amherst|John]] sharply asked who the call was from, but [[Nicoles Baxter|Nicole]] responded by saying she didn't know.

[[John Amherst|John]] led them to [[Bertrand Miller|Bertrand]]'s room, asking for a standard affair with no ceremony, and the ashes be returned in a brass urn. Inside the room, [[Bertrand Miller|Bertrand]]'s body lay, dead for only a few hours, but covered in a thick creamy yellow rash and [[Nicoles Baxter|Nicole]] could smell the faint smell of some dark taint. Although [[John Amherst|John]] said that the disease was not contagious, the Baxters were very careful to make sure they didn't touch the body. [[Nicoles Baxter|Nicole]]'s left hand brushed the rash and she muffled a cry, even though she wore gloves. When she got home, she cleaned her hand until it bled.

Josh and [[Nicoles Baxter|Nicole]] explained to [[George Baxter|George]] what happened and he quickly got the cremation underway and disposed of the ashes.

Two weeks later, [[Alenka Kozel|Alenka]] called again, repeating, "Come quickly, we've taken ill. We've passed away." [[Nicoles Baxter|Nicole]] returned to [[Ivy Meadows Care Home|Ivy Meadows]], but couldn't go near the house for the smell. She heard a tapping, and when she followed the source she found [[Alenka Kozel|Alenka]] pressed up against the window covered in the terrible rash in a room with black smeared walls. As she screamed a swarm of flies flew from her mouth.

[[Nicoles Baxter|Nicole]] fled, but bumped into an old man with unblemished pink skin and a young tall woman with close cropped hair and a scar over her right eye, who carried a large canvas bag. They said leaving quickly would be a good idea and [[Nicoles Baxter|Nicole]] obliged.

# Post-Statement Follow-Up

[[Ivy Meadows Care Home]] was closed on July 2011, a month before this case started, and it burned down on the 4th September 2011. There are no records of [[Bertrand Miller|Bertrand]] being burnt and his death certificate said he died on the 19th July, before the Care Home was decommissioned. Only 7 transfer records could be found, leaving 22 patients lost in the system. There is no record of [[Alenka Kozel|Alenka]].

[[Hannah Ramirez]] moved to Brighton and there are no records of [[John Amherst|Amherst]]. The Baxters had no follow-ups when asked, but [[Nicoles Baxter|Nicole]]'s left hand has been lost in a, "work place accident".

[[Jonathan Sims]] mentions that the way [[Nicoles Baxter]] talks about fear reminds him of [[Jane Prentiss]]' statement in [[MAG 32 - Hive]], and the old man reminds him of [[Trevor Herbert|Trevor the Tramp]] from [[MAG 10 - Vampire Killer]].

[[Timothy Stoker|Tim Stoker]] then interrupts to ask if [[Jonathan Sims|John]] has had any time to take stock of the delivery brought to the [[The Magnus Institute|Institute]] for him several days prior (at the end of [[MAG 35 - Old Passages]]). [[Jonathan Sims|John]] recovers the first item from his desk: [[Web Lighter|an old Zippo lighter]] with a spiderweb pattern on it. The second item, [[Timothy Stoker|Tim]] says, has already been placed in [[Artefact Storage]], and is [[Web Table|a table with a fascinating pattern on it]]. From that description, [[John Amherst|John]] seems to immediately recognize it as the table that was in [[Graham Folger]]'s flat in [[MAG 3 - Across the Street]].
