# Recording

The recording begins with [[Jonathan Sims|John]] and [[Sasha James|Sasha]] panicking as flesh worms flood out of the wall in [[Jonathan Sims|John's]] office. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] is trying to retrieve the [[Tape Recorders|tape recorder]] from the mass of [[Silver Worms|worms]] while [[Sasha James|Sasha]] shouts at him to leave it behind. [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] hears the commotion and retrieves a fire extinguisher to spray the [[Silver Worms|worms]] with, then leads his coworkers to the safe room where he had been staying once the recorder is free. The recording clicks off.

The recording clicks back on inside the safe room, where [[Sasha James|Sasha]] is removing a [[Silver Worms|flesh worm]] from [[Jonathan Sims|John]]. The two question [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] on his removal tool, a corkscrew, and he explains that he has been thinking about a potential worm invasion extensively in the time he has been living in the archives. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] thanks [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] for his diligence and the three relax slightly with the knowledge that the room they are in is secure.

[[Sasha James|Sasha]] asks [[Jonathan Sims|John]] why he would retrieve the [[Tape Recorders|tape recorder]] rather than flee immediately. He explains that, as he still does not know what happened to [[Gertrude Robinson]], he is deeply upset by the prospect of dying or otherwise disappearing and his successor not knowing what happened to him.

[[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] looks out into the hallway and sees the worms waiting for something. As he watches, [[Sasha James|Sasha]] asks [[Jonathan Sims|John]] why he values some statements over others. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] explains that, regardless of how dismissive he appears, he considers some statements to have genuine substance. [[Sasha James|Sasha]] agrees with him and acknowledges that the more, "Real," statements do not record digitally and can only be archived using [[Tape Recorders|analogue technology]].

[[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] spots [[Timothy Stoker|Tim]] outside the safe room, and both he and [[Sasha James|Sasha]] call out to him frantically. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] sharply reminds them that the room is soundproofed as they watch him pick up a [[Tape Recorders|tape recorder]] that was dropped in the hallway. Desperate, [[Sasha James|Sasha]] opens the door and calls out to Tim as she runs into the hall. The recording clicks off.

The recording clicks back on, now recording from the [[Tape Recorders|tape recorder]] in the hallways seconds before the last recording stopped. [[Timothy Stoker|Tim]] picks up the tape recorder and speaks into it briefly before he is tackled by [[Sasha James|Sasha]]. The two flee, followed closely by Jane Prentiss. The recording clicks off.

The recording clicks back on to [[Jonathan Sims|John]] and [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] inside the safe room. [[Sasha James|Sasha]] was able to escape the archives and is presumably getting help, but [[Timothy Stoker|Tim]] ran into the office where [[Jonathan Sims|John]] previously made a hole in the wall. [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] grows upset as he confronts [[Jonathan Sims|John]] on his scepticism toward the worms. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] insists that he is not a sceptic, but admits that he feels a presence as he records the more substantial statements. He is afraid of what will happen if the entity knows he believes the stories. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] asks [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] why, after everything he has been through, he hasn't quit his job. [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] admits he feels trapped in the archives, much like [[Jonathan Sims|John]]. The recording clicks off.

The recording clicks back on in [[Elias Bouchard|Elias's]] office as the fire alarms sound. [[Sasha James|Sasha]] tells [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]] that she pulled the fire alarm to get people to leave the building, and he admits he underestimated the danger of the worms. On [[Jonathan Sims|John]]'s request, [[Eliad Bouchard|Elias]] had previously replaced the fire suppression system with CO2, but it has not activated because there is no actual fire. Although the gas might kill [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] and [[Jonathan Sims|John]], the two agree that death is preferable to becoming a [[Flesh Hive]] and leave to activate the system manually. The recording clicks off.

The recording clicks back on to [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] and [[Jonathan Sims|John]] in the safe room as something pounds at the wall. [[Timothy Stoker|Tim]] breaks through the wall, having found the CO2 canisters that [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] hid in the office. He brings [[Jonathan Sims|John]] and [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] into the tunnels, where quieter, faster worms reside. The recording clicks off.

The recording clicks back on to [[Sasha James|Sasha]] walking through the [[Artefact Storage]] room after she and [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]] were separated. She is recording herself for [[Jonathan Sims|John]]'s records and discusses her time working for The Magnus Institute. She briefly worked as a practical researcher in Artefact Storage, but transferred due to the intensity of the work. She finds the web table that was previously delivered to [[Jonathan Sims|John]] and looks over it briefly, before noticing someone else in the room. She calls out to the person, but her words are drowned out by a strange sound and [[Sasha James|Sasha]] screams. After the screams stop, a [[Not-Sasha|different woman]] approaches the [[Tape Recorders|tape recorder]]. She repeats the words that [[Sasha James|Sasha]] called out before her death and turns off the [[Tape Recorders|tape recorder]].

The recording clicks back on to [[Jonathan Sims|John]] and [[Timothy Stoker|Tim]], lost in the tunnels beneath [[The Magnus Institute]]. After becoming separated from [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] during a worm attack, the two find a trapdoor that opens into the Archives. They open the trapdoor to discover countless worms as well as [[Jane Prentiss]], who calls out to [[Jonathan Sims|John]]. The recording ends.
