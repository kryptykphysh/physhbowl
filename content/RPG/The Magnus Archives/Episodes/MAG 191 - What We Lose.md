# Recording

The recording begins and [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] wakes up, startled. His shouts wake [[Jonathan Sims|John]] up, who was sleeping with his eyes open (something he says he cannot help). [[Jonathan Sims|John]] tells [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] that he doesn't remember his dreams anymore, and his, "sleep," is more like being on, "standby mode"; in between being awake and being asleep. [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] says that at [[Mikaele Salesa|Salesa]]'s house [[Jonathan Sims|John]], "slept," all the time (with his eyes open) and did not dream. He then asks [[Jonathan Sims|John]] if he recognises [[Celia]], which he does not, and recollects her giving a [[MAG 100 - I Guess You Had To Be There|statement]] once. He assumes she cannot remember it, seeing as she does not know her own name, and is averse to asking her.

They are interrupted by [[Cult in the Post-Change World|a cult member]] knocking and walking in, having been asked to check on them. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] and [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] introduce themselves and ask for the cult member's name, who refuses to answer. In their domain, names were how, "They," found you in files and took you away, which is why they do not tell people their name. They advise the others to do the same, apart from the prophets whose names are safe. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] says he Knows they are mistaken, but they doubt his knowledge because [[Jonathan Sims|John]] has not seen it himself. They say that [[Georgie Barker|Georgie]] and [[Melanie King|Melanie]] are out and do not know when they will return. They then offer biscuits and tins of food, which [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] accept. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] asks [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] to get food for him too since he feels shaky. Despite this, he has not needed to make a statement since being in the tunnels. [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] and the unnamed cult member leave to get the tins.

In a new recording, [[Arun]] introduces himself and gives [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] a [[Tape Recorders|tape recorder]] he had found turned off. [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] explains that [[Tape Recorders|tape recorder]]s have been following him and [[Jonathan Sims|John]] around for a while, even before [[The Change]], and that there is no need to worry about them. He tells [[Arun]] to ask the prophets if he has questions and [[Arun]] accuses him of not believing in their power. [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] says that he has known them a long time, but [[Arun]] retorts that [[Laverne]], a believer, knew the prophets beforehand too. [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] tries to dismiss him but [[Arun]] persists and asks about his and [[Jonathan Sims|John]]’s ability to walk safely above the tunnels too. [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] explains that they are different from [[Georgie Barker|Georgie]] and [[Melanie King|Melanie]], which [[Arun]] agrees with. He says that he sees hope in the prophets but does not know what he sees when he looks at him and [[Jonathan Sims|John]]. [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] is shocked by his rudeness. He continues, blaming [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] and [[Jonathan Sims|John]] of treating the cult members like idiots and pitying them, which [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] denies. [[Arun]] shouts, calling him a liar, and asks who he is and why they are here with their [[Tape Recorders|tape recorder]]s following them. [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]], agitated, says they are here to save the world, tells him again to ask the prophets, grabs the recorder and leaves.

A new recording starts, and [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] places tins of food down, telling [[Jonathan Sims|John]] that he did not see [[Georgie Barker|Georgie]] or [[Melanie King|Melanie]] and that it is not unusual for them to leave for long periods. They start to eat, and [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] doubts how welcome they are in the tunnels. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] says that he is alright, better than at [[Mikaele Salesa|Salesa]]’s, and does not believe he will forget everything upon leaving the tunnels. [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] says he thinks that the cult will help them, as [[Jonathan Sims|John]] is humanity’s only hope and the other option is to do nothing but think that [[Georgie Barker|Georgie]] and [[Melanie King|Melanie]] will not allow the cult to be endangered. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] says he cannot clearly find a way up from the tunnels as the layout is different, even though [[The Eye]] is calling to him. [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] considers trying anyway, but [[Jonathan Sims|John]] says it would take a long time and that there are creatures that could put up resistance. He explains that the creatures are [[Archivists]], like in [[MAG 52 - Crusader|Sergeant Heller's statement]], which did not die and are called to the Panopticon by [[The Eye]]. [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] asks what will happen when they defeat [[The Eye]]. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] says that it will not be good – if they find a way to banish [[The Entities]] to where they came from, [[Jonathan Sims|John]] will likely survive as he is now only weaker, but this scenario only buys time. If there is a way to destroy the powers, [[Jonathan Sims|John]] will not be alright as too much of him is [[The Eye]]. He is unsure what would happen exactly, but that he would lose something – his identity, mind or memories. They embrace, say they love each other, and [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] promises not to stop [[Jonathan Sims|John]] when the time comes. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] promises not to sacrifice himself at the first opportunity. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] says he is going to rest, and [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] says he will wake him if [[Georgie Barker|Georgie]] and [[Melanie King|Melanie]] arrive.

A new recording starts, and [[Georgie Barker|Georgie]] has just visited the [[Admiral]]. She and [[Melanie King|Melanie]] sit on a bench, joking about how they come up into a city of cameras and eyes for privacy. [[Georgie Barker|Georgie]] says that the [[Admiral]] looked happy and that the domain looked like, "The gorier bits of a nature documentary on repeat". [[Melanie King|Melanie]] suggests they pull him out, but [[Georgie Barker|Georgie]] declines, saying that it is safer with him in the domain. [[Melanie King|Melanie]] feels bad for doing nothing, but [[Georgie Barker|Georgie]] disagrees, saying that surviving and helping others is something. [[Melanie King|Melanie]] says she knows that the apocalypse is not [[Jonathan Sims|John]]'s fault, but that she is sick of being told to sympathise with him and dislikes him. [[Georgie Barker|Georgie]] says that she and [[Jonathan Sims|John]] are quite similar. Despite this, they decide to help [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] and [[Jonathan Sims|John]] since they cannot find a way into the [[The Magnus Institute|Institute]]. [[Georgie Barker|Georgie]] expresses concern about the creatures near the stairs, but [[Melanie King|Melanie]] says her lack of fear has made her judgement of the risk too high, and that she cannot think of another way out. [[Georgie Barker|Georgie]] says she misses making her podcast, and that [[The Change]] happened during a recording. They get up to leave and promise to help them.
