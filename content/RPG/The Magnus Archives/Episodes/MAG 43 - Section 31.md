# Statement

Police Constable [[Basira Hussain]] has signed the [[Section 31]] form, forever marking her apart from her police colleagues. '[[Section 31]]' refers to [Section 31 of the Freedom of Information Act](http://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2000/36/section/31), and is an exclusion for information regarding law enforcement, so details that could interfere with the prevention or detection a crime can not be given to the public. These are usually used for those cases that are paranormal in nature, such as the case of [[Jane Prentiss]] attacking [[The Magnus Institute]] and [[Gertrude Robinson|Gertrude's]] discovered body.

Officers who have signed a [[Section 31]] form, or been, "Sectioned", are informally marked out by the others and from then on assigned to work with other sectioned officers and called on to handle potentially paranormal cases. Other officers try to avoid weird cases, to avoid being sectioned themselves. One of her partners who had also signed a [[Section 31]] was [[Richard Carver]].

Her first [[Section 31]] case was in August 2011, about a year after she joined the force when she was partnered with [[John Spencer]]. At a fire near Clapham a man had become violent. When they arrived at the scene, they found the bald Hispanic man in his mid-to-late forties detained by two firemen. He shouted about, "Cleansing fire," "All shall be ash," and, "[[Asag]]". Although he had come out of the burning buildings he had no burns, but when [[Basira Hussain|Basira]] went to cuff him the metal of the handcuffs became red hot immediately, burning her. The Hispanic man whispered something to [[John Spencer|Spencer]]. They later discovered his name was [[Diego Molina]] and took from him, amongst other things, a red bound leather [[List of Books|book]]. Before [[Diego Molina|Diego]] was released, [[John Spencer|Spencer]] was suspended for trying to burn the book, before apparently killing himself by bathing in boiling water (officially via a kettle, which [[Basira Hussain|Basira]] notes as being a particularly weak cover-up).

[[Basira Hussain|Basira]]'s next genuine [[Section 31]] case was on 18th July 2014. [[Alice Daisy Tonner|Alice "Daisy" Tonner]] was her partner at the time, she had also been Sectioned years before [[Basira Hussain|Basira]] was on the force, originally due to "spider husks" (possibly human sized, or spider-like husks of people), and had mentioned [[Vampires|vampires]]. In Kensington they were called to a set of flats where gunshots had been heard. They approached the room to find a man in a room with painted over windows and smashed lights, full of expensive trinkets including many old domino sets in glass cases. He had been repeatedly shot in the face from close range. When the two approached he lurched into life and grabbed the gun, attempting to point it at his head, but [[Alice Daisy Tonner|Daisy]] was able to wrestle it away from him. He was taken away by paramedics and the death was ruled a suicide.

[[Harry Altman]] had also been a part of [[Section 31]] and had seen some deeply disturbing things whilst on these cases.

[[Basira Hussain|Basira]] confirmed that [[Gertrude Robinson|Gertrude]]'s body is being investigated as a murder, but because it is related to [[The Magnus Institute]] her colleagues are not willing to help. At present, [[Alice Daisy Tonner|Daisy]] cannot help as she is CID and currently the only [[Section 31]] detective.

# Post-Statement

[[Jonathan Sims]] confirms that they are not able to get the tapes as they are in police custody. [[Basira Hussain|Basira]] does not think that those at the [[The Magnus Institute|Institute]] can help the investigation.

He remarks that the name of the man from case 0121102 ([[MAG 12 - First Aid]]) is now known to be [[Diego Molina]] and that he has a suspicion as to where he got the book. He expresses sadness that the man is dead but states that a piece of the puzzle cannot be ignored. 

# Supplemental

[[Jonathan Sims|John]] reveals that [[Basira Hussain|Basira]] actually agreed to giving him access to tapes when she could, although she admitted that this would be quite infrequent. He worries about the possibility of not finding anything on the tapes.

# Trivia

- In the UK, to be "sectioned" commonly means to be compulsorily committed to a psychiatric hospital, in accordance with some section of the Mental Health Act.
