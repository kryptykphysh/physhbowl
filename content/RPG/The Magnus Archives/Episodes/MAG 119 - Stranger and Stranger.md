# Unknown

The Unknowing has begun, and the strangers are beginning their dance.

[[Jonathan Sims|John]] encounters [[Sarah Baldwin]], then [[Daniel Rawlings]] speaking in [[Sarah Baldwin|Sarah's]] voice, two people who have been taken by [[The Stranger]]. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] is convinced by [[Sarah Baldwin|Sarah]] that she is "Tim", and he gives her the detonator without knowing what it is.

Meanwhile, [[Alice Daisy Tonner|Daisy]] is being pursued by the couriers [[Breekon and Hope]]. They claim to be [[Basira Hussain|Basira]], and [[Alice Daisy Tonner|Daisy]] threatens to kill them. They taunt her, and she laughs as she kills [[Breekon & Hope|Hope]].

[[Timothy Stoker|Tim]] sees [[Basira Hussain|Basira]], but does not know who [[Basira Hussain|Basira]] is, who tries to comfort him. She says names do not matter here, and she knows she is here. [[Timothy Stoker|Tim]] does not believe her and tries to strike her, before running away. [[Basira Hussain|Basira]] does not understand.

[[Jonathan Sims|John]] says it is not real and [[Nikola Orsinov|Nikola]] taunts him, saying, "Just because you don't understand, doesn't mean it's a lie". She claims to be [[Timothy Stoker|Tim]], then [[Sasha James|Sasha]] back from the dead. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] tries to threaten her, but she tells him to make a fist. He cannot, not even knowing what a hand is. Nikola, now wearing [[Gertrude Robinson|Gertrude's]] skin, calls him pathetic, and chides him for failing to prevent [[The Unknowing]]. She spent years disrupting [[Rituals]] and saving the world, and [[Jonathan Sims|John]] fails at preventing even this one. [[Nikola Orsinov|Nikola]] starts wearing [[Jurgen Leitner]]'s skin and, "joins in". He says it is [[Jonathan Sims|John]]'s fault that he was bludgeoned to death in the basement of the Archives while [[Jonathan Sims|John]] left to smoke a cigarette, and mockingly reminds [[Jonathan Sims|John]] that smoking kills. [[Jurgen Leitner|Jurgen]] asks if [[Jonathan Sims|John]] even remembers what a cigarette is, or a pipe. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] asks what they want, and [[Nikola Orsinov|Nikola]], now acting as herself, asks him to dance. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] screams in surprise as he is presumably pulled away.

[[Alice Daisy Tonner|Daisy]] is in a brawl with [[Breekon Breekon Hope|Breekon]]. She attacks [[Breekon Breekon Hope|Breekon]], while laughing hysterically. [[Breekon Breekon Hope|Breekon]] catches her by the throat and tosses her in [[The Coffin]], as the courier calls her a stupid animal

[[Basira Hussain|Basira]] is trying to ignore the madness. She knows that she is in a place, and if it is a now, then it has an end. The other things are too much, and too many, and want to hurt her. She decides to leave by picking a where and moving.

[[Nikola Orsinov|Nikola]] tells [[Jonathan Sims|John]] "it's almost there", a terrible new world, and it is all his fault. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] sees the sad clown, bitter and hateful, going to a circus to find his fame before being torn apart by a cruel ringmaster. He is sometimes a doll, sometimes a mannequin, but is always hiding with another's skin and another's name. [[Nikola Orsinov|Nikola]] and [[Sarah Baldwin|Sarah]] talk and argue, with [[Sarah Baldwin|Sarah]] urging [[Nikola Orsinov|Nikola]] to finish the dance.

[[Timothy Stoker|Tim]] leaps in and attacks [[Sarah Baldwin|Sarah]] and wrestles the detonator from her. He is then disorientated and starts to attack [[Jonathan Sims|John]] in a fit of confused rage. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] uses his [[The Eye|Beholding]] to ask [[Timothy Stoker|Tim]] what he sees, which breaks the strangeness and lets [[Timothy Stoker|Tim]] get his bearing. [[Timothy Stoker|Tim]] sees the clown that took his brother and for a time, wore his face. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] uses his Beholding again to ask [[Timothy Stoker|Tim]] what is in his hand, which is the detonator. [[Nikola Orsinov|Nikola]] whisks [[Jonathan Sims|John]] away, begins to taunt [[Timothy Stoker|Tim]] and say it is too late. [[Timothy Stoker|Tim]] replies with, "You sound stressed. You know I hear the Great Grimaldi's in town. You should go see him, cheer yourself up,"

[[Nikola Orsinov|Nikola]] says his joke is not funny and [[Timothy Stoker|Tim]] simply responds "I know." before activating the detonator.

There is an explosion and the tape cuts off.

# Trivia

- It is unknown if chaos of [[The Unknowing]] is merely the result of [[The Stranger]]'s power or of the entity physically manifesting, though if the latter is true then this marks the first time we see [[Jonathan Sims|John]] inside of an entity.
- [[Timothy Stoker|Tim]]'s joke about "The Great Grimaldi" references [Joseph Grimaldi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_Grimaldi), a Regecy-era clown whose latter years were marked by depression and illness. [[Nikola Orsinov|Orsinov's]] makeup in [[MAG 104 - Sneak Preview|Sneak Preview]] is said to resemble Grimaldi's, and [[Danny Stoker]] is heard mumbling the name, "Joey", implying that [[Nikola Orsinov|Orsinov]] may have _been_ Joseph Grimaldi at some point.
