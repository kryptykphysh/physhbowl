# Recording

[[Laverne]] is trying to calm [[Celia]], who is worrying about [[Melanie King|Melanie]] and [[Georgie Barker|Georgie]] being gone for too long. [[Georgie Barker|Georgie]] and [[Melanie King|Melanie]] return with [[Jonathan Sims|John]] and [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] in tow just as [[Celia]] finds a running [[Tape Recorders|tape recorder]] among their supplies.

As [[Georgie Barker|Georgie]] introduces everyone to each other, [[Celia]] mentions that the domain she was trapped in took her name and [[Celia]] is the new name she picked for herself. She asks where [[Jonathan Sims|John]] and [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] were trapped and [[Jonathan Sims|John]] explains how they have been wandering the world and know [[Melanie King|Melanie]] and [[Georgie Barker|Georgie]] from before. Laverne recognises [[Jonathan Sims|John]] as [[Melanie King|Melanie]]'s old boss from [[Melanie King|Melanie]] talking about him during therapy. [[Celia]] asks [[Georgie Barker|Georgie]] about the tape recorder and [[Georgie Barker|Georgie]] has her turn it off.

The recording stops and starts several times as [[Georgie Barker|Georgie]] keeps turning the recorders off and even attempts to destroy them. She reluctantly lets the tapes run after [[Jonathan Sims|John]] promises her that they will not hurt anybody. [[Georgie Barker|Georgie]] tells [[Jonathan Sims|John]] that there are seven people in the tunnels now and that there used to be more, but they attracted attention and their numbers have dropped. She apologises for distancing herself from the [[The Magnus Institute|Institute]] before, but [[Jonathan Sims|John]] assures her that she could have done nothing to stop [[The Change]] since he, manipulated by [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]]/[[Jonah Magnus|Jonah]], was the catalyst.

[[Georgie Barker|Georgie]] then tells him that her and [[Melanie King|Melanie]] rescue people from fearscapes and bring them to the tunnels where they can eat and drink (thanks to [[Jurgen Leitner]]'s supply caches and a nearby supermarket fearscape). [[Jonathan Sims|John]] asks about [[The Admiral]], and she says that he was not immune to [[The Change]] like her and [[Melanie King|Melanie]] and is currently residing in a domain full of cats and prey. [[Georgie Barker|Georgie]] thinks he is happy there, but he does not recognise her when she visits him. She asks [[Jonathan Sims|John]] how to turn back the apocalypse, but he does not know.

[[Melanie King|Melanie]] notices the recorder start and she lets it run after [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] tells her that it will stay on if it wants. She tells him that she has been working on her anger issues, even though her former therapist Laverne thinks she is a, "Chosen one". She then congratulates [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] on his new relationship with [[Jonathan Sims|John]]. They realise that they haven't seen each other since before they were affected by [[The Lonely]] and [[The Slaughter]] respectively and that they do not really know each other. They playfully reintroduce themselves as their new roles as prophet and sidekick, prompting [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] to ask about how their positions as [[Cult in the Post-CHange World|cult]] leaders came about.

[[Melanie King|Melanie]] explains that they are immune since she severed her connection to the Eye and [[Georgie Barker|Georgie]] has no fear. This immunity is imparted to the people they save, though only partially and for a short time. They found Laverne in a Spiral maze and took her back to the safety of the tunnels, which motivated them to save more people. She says that explaining the Eye’s ascendance to those unaware was strange – they believed her, but some interpreted it in a religious sense. [[Melanie King|Melanie]] admits that her title of prophet was due to telling them of her (fabricated) vision that the apocalypse would end, since she felt guilty that they all looked hopeless. She expresses discomfort at being called ‘The Blind Prophet’ and names it as part of the reason why she and [[Georgie Barker|Georgie]] leave the tunnels so much, even though she thinks of her cult as good people.

[[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] asks about her new relationship, and she answers that she thinks [[Georgie Barker|Georgie]] is too good for her. She asks [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] the same, who says that, despite communication issues, it is working well. [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] tells her that on their journey he and [[Jonathan Sims|John]] saw Daisy, who had turned into a monster, and Basira, who killed her and is now on her own route to the Panopticon. [[Melanie King|Melanie]] remembers to remind him to be careful of Helen, who tried to eat [[Celia]] and was in the tunnels a few times. She confessed that she was fond of Helen and thought she was an ally. [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] tells her that that [[Jonathan Sims|John]] destroyed her in a standoff.

A wooden knocking is heard, and [[Melanie King|Melanie]] tells [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] that she promised to listen some of Arun’s new work. [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] offers to join her, but [[Melanie King|Melanie]] refuses. Laverne enters and invites him to a dinner of cold baked beans which he declines. He asks her if she believes that [[Melanie King|Melanie]] and [[Georgie Barker|Georgie]] are prophets. She responds saying that she is unsure – her experiences as [[Melanie King|Melanie]]’s therapist did not lead her to believe she was holy. She has listened to both [[Melanie King|Melanie]]'s and the cult's explanation for their new world, neither of which make sense to her, but she chooses to have hope in the prophets anyway.

In a new recording, [[Arun]] asks [[Georgie Barker|Georgie]] and [[Melanie King|Melanie]] for feedback, who awkwardly express discomfort at the word, "Redeemers," used to describe them in his poem. [[Arun]] recognises that they dislike being seen as saviours but admires their humility. He suggests that their immunity is fed by hope, much like the rest of the world is run by fear. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] says he is wrong, and [[Arun]], irritated, asks why [[Jonathan Sims|John]] is allowed to join if it is dangerous for there to be more followers. [[Georgie Barker|Georgie]] explains that [[Jonathan Sims|John]] is not a follower, but is able to travel through the world like her and [[Melanie King|Melanie]]. [[Arun]] apologises and leaves. [[Georgie Barker|Georgie]] suggests that they get some rest and discuss what to do tomorrow, before alerting [[Jonathan Sims|John]] that the tape is running. The recording stops.
