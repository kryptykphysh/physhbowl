# Recording

A recording begins with [[Martin Blackwood]] walking in the tunnels below [[The Magnus Institute]], accompanied by [[Peter Lukas]].

[[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] expresses how uncomfortable he is about being there, and [[Peter Lukas|Peter]] mentions [[Timothy Stoker|Tim]], a conversation topic that [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] quickly dismisses. [[Peter Lukas|Peter]] then reveals that he has [[The Seven Lamps of Architecture]], a [[List of Books|Leitner]] that allows him to control the tunnel pathways. To demonstrate its power, against [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]]'s concerns, [[Peter Lukas|Peter]] releases the [[NotThem]] that [[Jurgen Leitner]] had trapped in the tunnels. The [[NotThem]] insists they have some, "Unfinished business to take care of," and scurries off. [[Peter Lukas|Peter]] tells [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] that it will preoccupy the others and, if he wishes, [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] could leave the tunnels to help his peers. [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] says nothing.

A new recording begins. [[Alice Daisy Tonner|Daisy]] and [[Basira Hussain|Basira]] explain to [[Jonathan Sims|John]] that [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]] is no longer in jail and has walked out of his prison cell with little effort, which both overwhelms and bewilders him. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] believes that [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]]' disappearance coincides with the tape that was left on his desk and that [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]] will be making a return to the [[The Magnus Institute|Institute]].

A new recording begins, joining [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] and [[Peter Lukas|Peter]] back in the tunnels. [[Peter Lukas|Peter]] tells him that they are now in the [[Panopticon]] of [[Millbank Prison]], a significant site of power for the [[Beholding]], and that he needs [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]]'s connections to both [[The Lonely]] and the [[Watcher]] in order to learn more about [[The Extinction]]. Before any of this can occur, though, [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] needs to _"_dispose of the current occupant._"_ [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] spots [[[[Jonah Magnus|Jonah]] Magnus']] body and realises, to his horror, that he is to kill him; [[Peter Lukas|Peter]] has a knife for him. [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] questions, shakily, where [[Jonah Magnus|Jonah]]'s eyes are. [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]] appears and declares that [[Jonah Magnus|Jonah]]'s eyes are exactly where they've always been: "watching over _my_ institute."

A new recording begins. [[Basira Hussain|Basira]], [[Alice Daisy Tonner|Daisy]] and [[Jonathan Sims|John]] are discussing the emergence of a new tape recorder on his desk, labelled 'play me!' and accompanied with a key to the tunnels. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] wants to listen to it. [[Basira Hussain|Basira]] insists that they do not have the time, but [[Alice Daisy Tonner|Daisy]] agrees with [[Jonathan Sims|John]] that it could be important. They play the tape.

It begins with the sounds of sloshing liquid, presumably gasoline. [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]] Bouchard, catches [[Gertrude Robinson|Gertrude]] in an attempt to burn down the Archives. The two confirm that [[Gertrude Robinson|Gertrude]] knows that [[Jonah Magnus]] can take on different host bodies, and that [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]] is [[Jonah Magnus|Jonah]]; she became aware some time after [[Jonah Magnus|Jonah]] became [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]], but it took her a decade to find the location of his body. [[Gertrude Robinson|Gertrude]]'s plan was to set the Archives on fire, distracting [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]], then to go after [[Jonah Magnus|Jonah]]'s body. She had planned to survive the attack on her own patron by destroying her own eyes, as she learned from [[Eric Delano]]. However, [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]] was not distracted by the [[Extinguished Sun|ritual of the Dark]] as she had hoped. [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]] cocks a gun. [[Gertrude Robinson|Gertrude]] insists that he make a move, whether it be killing her or something else, and before she can finish her sentence, a gunshot rings out. [[Gertrude Robinson]]'s last words are her acknowledging her death and claiming that she thought it would have hurt more. The recording ends.

[[Basira Hussain|Basira]] and [[Jonathan Sims|John]] come to the conclusion that [[Elias Bouchard]] is, and perhaps has been, for a very long time, [[Jonah Magnus]], and acknowledge his ability to 'body hop' like [[Maxwell Rayner]]. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] wonders if [[Peter Lukas|Peter]] wants to help [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]] or stop him. From afar, heavy footsteps and many screams of terror and confusion can be heard. [[Basira Hussain|Basira]] checks it out.

A new recording begins. [[Peter Lukas|Peter]] asks [[Jonah Magnus|Jonah]] what he is doing in the panopticon and [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]], baffled, asks for silence, before asking what would happen if he were to kill him. [[Jonah Magnus|Jonah]] says he is honestly not sure: he can guarantee that the experience will be unpleasant for [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] and his peers at the [[The Magnus Institute|Institute]], and they may or may not survive depending on the strength of their connection to the [[The Magnus Institute|Institute]] or other entities. He questions whether or not [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] genuinely cares about other people anymore, due to his new connection to [[The Lonely]]. Laughing, [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] expresses that he would like to kill [[Jonah Magnus|Jonah]]. [[Peter Lukas|Peter]] says that [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] and himself are the same, wanting to be alone and unjudged, and insists he will like [[The Lonely]], and [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] agrees. [[Peter Lukas|Peter]] urges him to kill [[Jonah Magnus|Jonah]], to help save the world. [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] says no.

A new recording begins. [[Basira Hussain|Basira]] tells [[Alice Daisy Tonner|Daisy]] and [[Jonathan Sims|John]] that [[Trevor Herbert|Trevor]] and [[Julia Montauk|Julia]] have made a return and, at the same time, [[Not-Sasha]] appears in the [[The Magnus Institute|Institute]]. [[Jonathan Sims|John]]'s thoughts immediately jump to [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] in the tunnels, and rushes away to search for him upon [[Basira Hussain|Basira]]'s request. [[Alice Daisy Tonner|Daisy]] allows [[The Hunt]] to overtake her, and requests that [[Basira Hussain|Basira]] come and kill her when "it's all over." [[Basira Hussain|Basira]] promises that she will and runs. [[Trevor Herbert|Trevor]] and [[Julia Montauk|Julia]] come to kill [[Alice Daisy Tonner|Daisy]], but are taken by surprise as she snarls at them.

Back in the panopticon, [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] reaffirms that he will _not_ kill [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]]. He confesses that at first he only came to [[Peter Lukas|Peter]] because he felt like he had lost everything -- his mother and [[Jonathan Sims|John]], namely. For awhile [[Peter Lukas|Peter]] had him convinced that only _he_ could stop the Extinction, but it never sat right with him because [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] does not consider himself a chosen one. After acknowledging these suspicions, [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] began to pretend that he was devoted to [[The Lonely]], telling [[Peter Lukas|Peter]] what he wanted to hear to distract him from harming [[Jonathan Sims|John]].

[[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] has been playing [[Peter Lukas|Peter]], and refuses to cooperate and kill [[Jonah Magnus|Jonah]] now. [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]] gleefully mocks [[Peter Lukas|Peter]] for losing their bet, and [[Peter Lukas|Peter]] grows enraged, before he fulfills his obligation and casts [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] into the realm of [[The Lonely]] itself. [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]] tells him to leave the door open.

[[Jonathan Sims|John]] enters the Panopticon, where he is met by [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]], who informs [[Jonathan Sims|John]] that he found his way through the tunnels because [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]] called him. [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]] reveals that [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] has been taken into [[The Lonely]] by [[Peter Lukas|Peter]]; [[Jonathan Sims|John]] determines that he must follow and attempt to bring him back, although [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]] advises that it is unlikely he will return from willingly walking into [[The Lonely]]. He instructs [[Jonathan Sims|John]] to _know_ and follow their route. When asked, [[Jonathan Sims|John]] confirms that he is scared, and static overtakes the recording.
