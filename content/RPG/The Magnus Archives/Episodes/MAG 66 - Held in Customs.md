# Statement

[[Vincent Yang]] is convinced [[Mikaele Salesa]] drugged him and imprisoned him, although he felt entombed: a new sensation for him under drug use.

[[Mikaele Salesa|Mikaele]] Salesa used the Portsmouth harbour regularly and [[Vincent Yang|Vincent]] had to detain his shipment due to problems with his papers. When detained, [[Mikaele Salesa|Mikaele]] was furious and offered [[Vincent Yang|Vincent]] the option to look through his shipping container. He did, but only because he could. The shipping container was icy cold as it was the 18th January, although Salesa wore a tank top and unbuttoned shirt.

As [[Vincent Yang|Vincent]] finished, he stood up and accidentally nudged an old wooden box open, releasing a dusty cloud of warm air. It was completely empty when [[Vincent Yang|Vincent]] checked it. When he turned back to [[Mikaele Salesa|Mikaele]], he no longer looked angry, just concerned. [[Vincent Yang|Vincent]] warned [[Mikaele Salesa|Mikaele]] to get his papers in order. As [[Vincent Yang|Vincent]] went to leave, the antiques dealer grabbed him by the arm and said, "Don't go to sleep."

Unsettled, that night [[Vincent Yang|Vincent]] had a few vodkas to help him sleep. He slept extremely well, but when he went to move in the night, he couldn't. He opened his eyes to pitch darkness and he felt unvarnished wood all around him. He surmised that he was in the sturdy wooden crate he touched the previous day.

He cried out for help before it occurred to him that he might have limited air if he was buried alive. It took him a while to make the link to [[Mikaele Salesa|Salesa's]] box he touched earlier in the day. He was not able to open the box, as though something was on top of the crate. It became extremely hot and he started to panic.

As he realised that he could not be buried since he could breathe, the crate seemed to constrict and get smaller. [[Vincent Yang|Vincent]] realised that he could see sunlight outside of the box, but as he started to enjoy it he felt the box get smaller again. Finding he was still wearing his watch he discovered it was 11:56. Eventually he slept, but awoke in the box.

Judging by the rise and fall of the sunlight, he was in that box for four days.

Abruptly the box was opened and icy air flooded in. [[Mikaele Salesa|Salesa]] and [[Peter Lukas|Captain Lukas]] looked into the box. The captain shrugged and gave [[Mikaele Salesa|Salesa]] a £20 note before walking off. The antiques dealer helped [[Vincent Yang|Vincent]] out of the box, which was in the shipping container. He handed in his notice but realized he had been returned on the day after he met [[Mikaele Salesa|Salesa]], and his watch no longer matched other clocks.

# Supplemental

[[Jonathan Sims|John]] has been going through [[Gertrude Robinson|Gertrude's]] laptop, but has not found much, although she had a budget spreadsheet which indicated she spent a lot of money travelling, and that the money was approved.

Her emails and browsing history showed that she did, indeed, travel a great deal to places such as Nairobi, Wichita, Budapest, Shanghai, and thus possibly [[MAG 53 - Crusader|Alexandria]]. She also ordered plenty of petrol, lighter fluid, pesticides, high power torches, and hundreds of different types of labels and filing systems.

It is also clear that she was trying to buy [[Jurgen Leitner|Leitners]] and is the grbookworm1818 mentioned in [[MAG 4 - Page Turner]] who won the [[Key of Solomon]], a book on demonology, on eBay. She also purchased a special printing of '[[The Seven Lamps of Architecture]]' by [[John Ruskin]] and a 1910 pamphlet entitled '[[A Disappearance]]'.

[[Jonathan Sims|Jonathan]] wonders if he is missing the point: maybe he should not be asking who killed her, but why.
