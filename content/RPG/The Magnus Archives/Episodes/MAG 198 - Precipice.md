# Pre-Statement

[[Basira Hussain|Basira]], [[Jonathan Sims|John]], and [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] are walking back to London. [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] wants to discuss what [[Annabelle Cane]] said, but [[Jonathan Sims|John]] says they should wait until London. [[Basira Hussain|Basira]] berates [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] for going off with [[Annabelle Cane|Annabelle]], but [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] says he thought that if there was a way to save [[Jonathan Sims|John]] and the world, it was worth the risk. [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] asks which way they are going, and [[Jonathan Sims|John]] says, "Forward". [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] and [[Basira Hussain|Basira]] are confused, as, "Forward," is an impossibly steep cliff with no visible bottom. [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] is upset, because he was looking forward to rowing. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] tells him some routes are only one-way.

The trio descends down a rickety ladder, until [[Jonathan Sims|John]] notices the ladder ends. [[Basira Hussain|Basira]] says they will have to jump, much to [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]]'s distress. [[Basira Hussain|Basira]] and [[Jonathan Sims|John]] point out that it will hurt when they hit the bottom, but they will not die. As [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] protests, [[Jonathan Sims|John]] realizes he needs to make a statement. [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] steels himself and hurls himself off the cliff, and [[Basira Hussain|Basira]] follows.

# Statement

The victims climb the rickety ladder, desperately not looking down at how long the fall is, or up at how far away the top is. Sometimes people fall and those on the ladder swear to themselves that they will not fall. The falling victims are slow enough that you can see the terror in their faces. As the inhabitants climb, they realize they do not know which way is up and which way is down. They consider flinging themselves off the ladder to the solid ground, but when they look down they can see the hard, painful earth. The victims cling desperately to the ladder, willing themselves not to give in to the urge to jump.

# Post-Statement

[[Jonathan Sims|John]] hurls himself off the cliff and lands hard at the bottom. [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] assures him the pain will go away soon. The three make their way to London, where they find [[Melanie King|Melanie]] and [[Georgie Barker|Georgie]] alone in the tunnels. They tell the trio that [[The Eye]]'s servants took [[Cult in the Post-Change World|the cultists]] away, like last time. [[Melanie King|Melanie]] realizes [[Basira Hussain|Basira]] is there and the two hug. [[Georgie Barker|Georgie]] and [[Melanie King|Melanie]] are happy [[Jonathan Sims|John]] and [[Basira Hussain|Basira]] got [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] back. They decide to get a drink and discuss [[Annabelle Cane|Annabelle]]'s plan.
