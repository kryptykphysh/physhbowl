# Pre-Statement

[[Jonah Magnus]] is in his office, channeling the fear of the world. [[Jonathan Sims]] tells [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] that [[Jonah Magnus|Jonah]]'s finally become an immortal king of the ruined world. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] speculates that for someone so close to [[The Eye]], being the eye's pupil is probably both agonizing and blissful. [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] asks if [[Jonathan Sims|John]] can smite him, but [[Jonathan Sims|John]] says that [[Jonah Magnus|Jonah]] is too close to [[The Eye]] to be smote. [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] suggests trying to reason with the real [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]], or even good old-fashioned murder. He suggests they find [[Jonah Magnus|Jonah]]'s true body and kill him. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] speculates that the original body is gone, lost in the terror. He says he needs to do a statement again.

# Statement

[[Elias Bouchard|Elias]] sits across from [[James Wright]], being interviewed for a job at [[The Magnus Institute]]. [[James Wright|Wright]]'s judging eyes remind [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]] of his own father. [[James Wright|Wright]] (whose lines are spoken by [[Jonah Magnus]] himself, as opposed to the Archivist) asks [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]] what he is afraid of. [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]] does not want to say that he is afraid of [[James Wright|Wright]]'s eyes, filled with curiosity and judgement, so instead he says he is afraid of spiders.

Suddenly, [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]] cannot recall if he was really afraid of spiders or not, and vividly imagines a spider crawling up his body. His daydream is broken by a cough from [[James Wright|Wright]], who almost appears satisfied, as if given an answer he likes.

[[James Wright|Wright]] asks if [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]] has had any supernatural experiences, and [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]] recalls a time when his friend, [[Allan Schrieber]], found an old [[List of Books|book]] with no library markings and only blank pages within. Later, [[Allan Schrieber|Allan]] told [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]] in a panicked state that some eyeless thing saw him through the pages. [[Allan Schrieber|Allan]] could sometimes see it, coming closer and closer to him. Finally, [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]] found [[Allan Schrieber|Allan]] dead, blood splattered all over the room, his eyes missing. [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]] does not tell [[James Wright|Wright]] about this.

[[James Wright|Wright]] asks [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]] why he wants the job. [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]] begins to give a prepared answer, but stops when he realizes that [[James Wright|Wright]] knows the real answer: the [[The Magnus Institute|Institute]] is simply the first step on the path to the position he actually feels he deserves. [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]] recalls suddenly that he was sent a letter inviting him to interview despite having not sent out a CV (résumé). He tries to start over and give the answer again, but [[James Wright|Wright]] stops him, asking why he chose to "heed the call" of the [[The Magnus Institute|Institute]]. [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]] responds that he somehow knows this is where he should be, and [[James Wright|Wright]] promptly gives him the job.

[[Elias Bouchard|Elias]] has a brief vision of an event which, unbeknownst to him, is his future possession by [[Jonah Magnus|Jonah]] Magnus. He tries to smile, and thanks his new employer for the opportunity.

# Post-Statement

[[Jonathan Sims|John]] ends the statement, and [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] notes that it was an intense one. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] tells [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] that [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]] is gone, dead, with no hope of getting him back. He says that [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] was right about [[Jonah Magnus]], that is still vulnerable through the body of [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]]. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] could destroy it, but that would mean [[The Eye]] would need a new pupil: [[Jonathan Sims|John]] himself. He tells [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] that this is likely exactly what [[The Eye]] wants.
