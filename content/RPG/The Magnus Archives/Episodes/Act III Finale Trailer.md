# Recording

[[Arun]] finishes reading a poem from the basement's limited library. [[Celia]] expresses concern over [[Georgie Barker|Georgie]] and [[Melanie King|Melanie]] being above ground, but [[Arun]] reassures her that they cannot be harmed. [[Celia]] has a feeling that something is happening, causing [[Laverne]] to admit that she went too close to the stairs and realised that there were more watchers, four or five as opposed to the previous two.

[[Arun]] encourages the others to have faith in their prophets. An unnamed person mentions that people named Song and Christopher were not protected by their cult leaders. [[Celia]] repeats her fears, but [[Laverne]] shares her doubts that anything can get worse, since the world has already ended.
