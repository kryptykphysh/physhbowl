# Pre-Statement

[[Jonathan Sims]] is arguing with [[Elias Bouchard]] over his recent kidnapping. He is angry that he has been gone a month without any of the archival staff being made aware of the kidnapping and that he had to rely on [[Helen RIchardson|Helen]] for rescue rather than [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]] sending a rescue party. [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]] is dismissive of [[Jonathan Sims|John]] and claims that he said nothing to the team to avoid distracting them with worry while they investigated the [[Rituals|ritual]] site. He insists that [[Jonathan Sims|John]] needs to develop his powers on his own rather than be walked through the process.

[[Jonathan Sims|John]] brings up [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]]'s murder of [[Jurgen Leitner]] as an instance of [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]] hindering him, but [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]] maintains that if [[Jonathan Sims|John]] were given answers directly it would stunt the development of his powers. Growing angry, [[Jonathan Sims|John]] presses [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]] for details on [[The Unknowing]]. [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]] admits that while the bulk of [[Gertrude Robinson's]] research was hidden from him, he knows that [[The Unknowing]] will take the form of a dance, that it can be stopped if the site of the [[Rituals|ritual]] is sufficiently disrupted, and that [[Gertrude Robinson|Gertrude]] had apparently acquired something that could disrupt it to the required degree.

While complaining about the lack of leads, [[Jonathan Sims|John]] offhandedly mentions that [[[[Gerard Keay|Gerard]] Keay]] was working with [[Gertrude Robinson|Gertrude]], something which makes [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]] very excited. While [[Jonathan Sims|John]] believes it was mentioned in one of the statements, [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]] instead asserts that it is [[Jonathan Sims|John]]'s powers beginning to develop. He now simply knows things without having read about it, and [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]] suggests they should look into [[Gertrude Robinson|Gertrude]] and [[Gerard Keay|Gerard]]'s movements before their deaths. They are interrupted when [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]] realises that a murderous, knife-wielding [[Melanie King]] is on her way to his office, and he asks [[Jonathan Sims|John]] to prevent any violence from occurring.

[[Jonathan Sims|John]] asks [[Melanie King|Melanie]] to put the knife down. She is viciously angry that [[Jonathan Sims|John]] would try to stop her after being absent for so long, but apologises when she learns he had been kidnapped. She is resolved to kill [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]], believing his threat about the assistants dying with him is a lie and unafraid of any legal consequences as the police tend not to investigate crimes committed at The Magnus Institute. [[Melanie King|Melanie]] argues bitterly with [[Jonathan Sims|John]] for taking [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]]'s side in the conflict, but backs down after he assures her that they will take care of [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]] eventually. She leaves.

# Statement

[[François Deschamps]] was a bartender at Le Papillon Blanc, an establishment that ran as a cafe during the day and a small bar at night. Having worked the bar for some years,[[François Deschamps|Deschamps]] was quite familiar with his coworker [[Benoît Maçon]], a deeply depressed, middle-aged man with no meaningful relationships. [[François Deschamps|Deschamps]] was not friendly toward [[Benoît Maçon|Benoît]], as he actively sought the pity of his co-workers for being so disconnected from others and would openly stare at couples patronising the bar, envious.

Five weeks before [[François Deschamps|Deschamps]] gave his statement, [[Benoît Maçon|Benoît]] suddenly became far more upbeat and cheerful. He eventually confided in [[François Deschamps|Deschamps]] that he had met a woman, his _petite scarabée_ (English: little beetle), and was deeply in love. He spoke to anyone who would listen about this woman and "the little ones", whom [[François Deschamps|Deschamps]] assumed to be her children, but would not give any names or specific details about his new family. [[Benoît Maçon|Benoît]]'s personal appearance and hygiene steadily deteriorated over the following month, and he was followed by a damp, woody smell that lingered in the air around him.

While they were working together one night, [[François Deschamps|Deschamps]] saw something moving under the skin of [[Benoît Maçon|Benoît]]'s right hand. Eventually a small insect emerged from under the nail of his ring finger and crawled away, with no blood and no reaction from [[Benoît Maçon|Benoît]].

[[François Deschamps|Deschamps]] was shaken and horrified, and shortly thereafter encountered [[Gerard Keay|Gerard]] Keay and [[Gertrude Robinson|Gertrude]] Robinson. He told them about [[Benoît Maçon|Benoît]] and was promised answers if he could provide them with an address. The two researchers debated whether [[Benoît Maçon|Benoît]] was under the sway of [[The Stranger]] or [[The Corruption]], and eventually all three went to [[Benoît Maçon|Benoît]]'s home. There, they found [[Benoît Maçon|Benoît]] in the embrace of his _petit scarabée_, a thing that [[François Deschamps|Deschamps]] found so repulsive that he and [[Gerard Keay|Keay]] suggested they burn the apartment to the ground. However, [[Gertrude Robinson|Robinson]] rejected the idea as it may have attracted the attention of the police.

# Post-Statement

[[Jonathan Sims|John]] realises that the entire statement was written in French, and that he is able to understand it perfectly despite never learning the language.

[[Benoît Maçon|Benoît]] Maçon died in late June 2014, shortly after the statement was given, and Toulouse police have tightly sealed the records of the matter. François [[François Deschamps|Deschamps]] refused a follow-up interview, but forwarded the Archives a badly stained wedding invitation. On it, the only name not obscured by stains is [[Benoît Maçon|Benoît]]'s.

Based on the travel documents he found in [[Gertrude Robinson|Gertrude]]'s laptop, [[Jonathan Sims|John]] concludes that she and Keay next went to New Zealand. Before he departs to follow them, he catches up with [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] as he promised [[Georgie Barker|Georgie]] he would. During their conversation, [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] apologises profusely for not knowing [[Jonathan Sims|John]] was kidnapped and asks about how he has been doing. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] avoids speaking about himself and instead asks after the other archival assistants, and is unsurprised to learn that [[Timothy Stoker|Tim]] is doing poorly and that [[Melanie King|Melanie]] has been acting strange. [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] asks if [[Jonathan Sims|John]] will be returning to work at the Archives, and is disappointed to learn that he is not. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] apologises for being so distant and leaves.
