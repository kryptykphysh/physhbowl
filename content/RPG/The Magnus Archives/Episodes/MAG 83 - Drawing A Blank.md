# Pre-Statement

[[Jonathan Sims|Jonathan]] receives this statement through the post, delivered to [[Georgie Barker|Georgie's]] house. [[Georgie Barker|Georgie]] is highly displeased that [[Jonathan Sims|John's]] "weird stalker" knows her address.

# Statement

[[Chloe Ashburt]] works in a large department store called [[Fanton's Department Store|Fanton's]] which is putting her through Art School. She enjoyed getting involved in the displays in the windows and shop floors and her line manager, Lana, usually allowed her to do so.

One of the displays that [[Chloe Ashburt|Chloe]] worked on was for the autumn collection with the theme 'Nights at the Circus', which included various mannequins, one dressed up as a ring mistress in a bright red top hat and whip. [[Chloe Ashburt|Chloe]] was interested in figure drawing and Lana would allow her to stay late and draw the mannequins. When the Circus displays went up, [[Chloe Ashburt|Chloe]] spent many evenings drawing them.

When [[Chloe Ashburt|Chloe]] came into work one morning she discovered that the ring mistress mannequin had been replaced with another, almost identical, mannequin. It had longer arms, a more twisted neck and a crack by its forehead. None of her colleagues recognised it, however. It did not fit with the other mannequins and [[Chloe Ashburt|Chloe]] always felt like it was watching her.

Lana called [[Chloe Ashburt|Chloe]] into her office one morning, asking if she had been involved in any other artistic pursuits. [[Chloe Ashburt|Chloe]] said that she had stayed late to draw. When asked, [[Chloe Ashburt|Chloe]] confirmed that she hadn't seen anyone last night, nor had she moved anything. Lana showed her some photos of one of the autumn displays.

The mannequin had been reassembled, torso upside down and arms jutting out of its pelvis, holding its head up which had a bright red pompom glued to the top. It had been dressed in red and purple pajamas and had a crude smile painted on its face. It reminded [[Chloe Ashburt|Chloe]] of a clown. Lana believed that [[Chloe Ashburt|Chloe]] was not involved but said that someone who worked for [[Fanton's Department Store|Fanton's]] must be involved as they had avoided any security cameras.

Lana started a rotation for people who would close up the shop. [[Chloe Ashburt|Chloe]] noticed that those who closed up were jumpy the next day. After a few weeks, Lana had to close up herself: Jan had a doctor's appointment and Lee had a family emergency. [[Chloe Ashburt|Chloe]] convinced Lana to let her stay with her when she closed up to do some quick drawings and her manager agreed.

[[Chloe Ashburt|Chloe]] went to get her drawing equipment and realized she could not hear Lana. She went to draw the ring mistress, but was greeted with an empty display, bar a torn wedge of _papier-mache_ and a headless acrobat mannequin. She heard Lana calling to her in a way that made [[Chloe Ashburt|Chloe]] think she was being forced to do so. [[Chloe Ashburt|Chloe]] called 999 as she approached the store cupboard that Lana's voice was coming from. The lights did not work, and she could just see a tall, thin figure which moved jerkily towards her. [[Chloe Ashburt|Chloe]]'s muscles seized up, and she dropped her phone as the mannequin pressed a plastic finger to her lips, whispering "Sshhh."

The next thing [[Chloe Ashburt|Chloe]] knew the police arrived who had found Lana's body. CCTV exonerated [[Chloe Ashburt|Chloe]], and the only blood found on her was a single line traced down her lips. The police believed that a killer had been stalking [[Fanton's Department Store|Fanton's]] and fled when [[Chloe Ashburt|Chloe]] called the police.

# Post-Statement

[[Jonathan Sims|John]] is finding it difficult to follow up on this case without the resources of [[The Magnus Institute]]. Lana had been strangled and partially skinned in the store cupboard of [[Fanton's Department Store|Fanton's]]. [[Jonathan Sims|Jonathan]] was able to find a picture of the service entrance to the building, which was assumed to be used by the murderer, which showed a [[Breekon & Hope]] delivery van parked nearby.

[[Jonathan Sims|Jonathan]] was not able to find much on [[Chloe Ashburt|Chloe]], other than bringing an unfair dismissal case against a previous employer, claiming it was motivated by her dwarfism.

[[Jonathan Sims|Jonathan]] suspects that these mannequins are tied to [[The Stranger]] in some way and [[Breekon & Hope]] may be linked to it as well, bringing skin to a taxidermist in [[MAG 54 - Still Life]].

The ex-archivist is not sure who sent this statement: there are no revelations and he wonders if it is a message, that hiding will not bring any answers.
