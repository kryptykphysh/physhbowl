## Statement[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fthe-magnus-archives.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMAG_5%3A_Thrown_Away%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&uselang=en "Sign in to edit")

[[Kieran Woodward]] is a refuse collector for Waltham Forest Council, whose crew comprised [[David Atayah]], [[Matthew Wilkinson]] and [[Alan Parfitt]].

On an undisclosed date the binmen found a bag of assorted doll heads in a bin bag outside 93 Lancaster Road, Walthamstow. The heads appeared as though they had been dragged across rough concrete. [[Alan Parfitt]] was greatly amused by this and the group kept an eye out on the house afterwards.

A few months later they found another strange bag outside #93. This bag was full of long strips of paper, about 1 inch across, with the Lord's Prayer written on in Latin and singed slightly on the edges.

The third bag, a fortnight later, was full of thousands of teeth, all in various states of decay. The binmen called the police, who knocked on the door of #93 but the elderly couple there did not know anything about the bags.

After this last bag, [[Alan Parfitt|Alan]] seemed troubled, regularly tired and irritable. He revealed to [[Kieran Woodward|Kieran]] that he had been going to 93 Lancaster Road to watch out for whoever was leaving the bags. [[Kieran Woodward|Kieran]] admitted on the statement that he had thought about doing the same.

Eventually [[Alan Parfitt|Alan]] was fired after he fell asleep at the wheel and crashed the truck into a car. He was replaced with [[Guy Wardman]].

On 8th August 2008, at 02:09AM [[Kieran Woodward|Kieran]] received a text from [[Alan Parfitt|Alan]], saying only, "Found him". Keiran's replies received no response from [[Alan Parfitt|Alan]], but [[Kieran Woodward|Kieran]] knew where to look for him and went to 93 Lancaster Road.

He arrived at the scene to find another bag with a dark green bow. Unwrapping it, the bag was full of polystyrene packing peanuts and a bronze or copper anatomical heart with [[Alan Parfitt|Alan]]'s name engraved on it, which was as cold as if it had just come out of a freezer. He never saw [[Alan Parfitt|Alan]] again. He asked a friend to dispose of the heart in a medical waste incinerator.

There were no more strange bags at 93 Lancaster Road.

# Post-Statement

[[Martin Blackwood|Martin] did a follow up interview with [[Kieran Woodward|Kieran]] but there was nothing new to add to the statement and no new bags had arrived at 93 Lancaster Road.

[[Sasha James|Sasha] looked into the police reports. [[Alan Parfitt|Alan]] was reported as missing by his brother, [[Michael Parfitt]], on 20th August 2009 (actually 2008 but misspoken on the recording, [confirmed by Jonny on the forum](http://rustyquill.proboards.com/post/1852/thread)) and was never found. Officer Suresh and Altman were the two officers who dealt with the teeth case.

Through these police reports it was revealed that all the teeth from the third bag, all 2780 of them, were the same tooth in many different states of decay.

# Continuity
- **Related Entities:** As detailed in the season 4 Q&A, this episode's main [[The Entities|Entity]]]] is intended to be [[The Flesh]]]], as an early version of [[Tom Haan]].
    - It is based on notions of removal and transformation of body parts, surplus, and overproduction. However, due to the early writing and production of this episode, several thematic elements of this Entity had not yet been fully nailed down, resulting in the muddied perception of which power is behind this statement. While initially written as "Flesh-adjacent", the result is a complicated "grab bag of Fears". In its current form several entities could be reasonably interpreted from the contents of the bags, such as:
        - [[The Stranger]], for the [[MAG 24 - Strange Music|doll's heads]].
        - [[The Desolation]], for the burnt pages.
        - [[The Flesh]], as previously mentioned.
        - [[The Eye]], for the binmen's ritualistic observations and, especially [[Alan Parfitt|Alan]]]]'s, curiosity.
        - [[The Extinction]], for the post-human implications of garbage and old relics of humanity being discarded en masse.
- The house's location is close to the restaurant mentioned in [[MAG 72 - Takeaway]].
- Officer Altman could be the same person as [[Leo Altman]], a sectioned officer mentioned in [[MAG 73 - Police Lights]], or [[Harry Altman]], mentioned in [[MAG 43 - Section 31]]
