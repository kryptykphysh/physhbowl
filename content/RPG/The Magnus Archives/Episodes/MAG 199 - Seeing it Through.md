# Recording

[[Melanie King|Melanie]], [[Jonathan Sims|John]], [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]], [[Georgie Barker|Georgie]], and [[Basira Hussain|Basira]] are in the tunnels and discuss what to do next. [[Georgie Barker|Georgie]] sees three possible options and starts to lay them out.

The first one is [[Annabelle Cane|Annabelle]]'s plan of destroying the Panopticon and killing [[Jonah Magnus|Jonah]] to release the [[Fears]] into an unknown number of other realities. They do not know if this means just some realities or all of them and it is not something [[Jonathan Sims|John]] is able to Know.

Their second option is to keep the [[Fears]] contained and let [[Jonathan Sims|John]] take over from [[Jonah Magnus|Jonah]] so that he can try to make things more tolerable until the [[Fears]] and all of humanity dies. [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] strongly opposes this plan since it means losing [[Jonathan Sims|John]] but [[Jonathan Sims|John]] blames himself for the apocalypse and feels that he has a responsibility to try and make things better, even if it means sacrificing himself. [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] counters that they should all sacrifice themselves in that case since they all bear some responsibility for the apocalypse. [[Melanie King|Melanie]] protests that [[Georgie Barker|Georgie]] is blameless but [[Georgie Barker|Georgie]] thinks that she should have done more to help instead of staying out of everything.

Their third option is to try and adapt to the world as it is and simply do nothing as the [[Fears]] slowly burn themselves out.

They argue about whether they even have the right to make the choice when they are not the ones trapped in never-ending nightmares and [[Jonathan Sims|John]] is angry that they are considering dooming other realities to save their own. The others point out that, according to [[Annabelle Cane|Annabelle]], it would not be an automatic death sentence. The other realities would be like it was before the apocalypse with the [[Fears]] lurking on the edge of reality and in this reality it took millennia for someone to succeed at a ritual. 

[[Jonathan Sims|John]] thinks they should take the chance to destroy the [[Fears]] forever. The others raise the possibility that the [[Fears]] do not even originate in this reality, they may just be one of many realities that received the [[Fears]] from a situation just like this. They cannot know for sure either way and the others are not keen on dooming their reality on the mere chance that their [[Fears]] are unique.

[[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] does not want to watch the world slowly die on the off-chance that it might do some small good somewhere else. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] raises the possibility that if he takes [[Jonah Magnus|Jonah]]'s place, he might be able to speed it along and end humanity's suffering sooner but no one else wants to consider that as an option.

The others have made up their mind to try [[Annabelle Cane|Annabelle]]'s plan and [[Jonathan Sims|John]] unhappily concedes before leaving to have a smoke.

[[Georgie Barker|Georgie]] joins him and asks for a cigarette before they chat for a bit. [[Georgie Barker|Georgie]] asks if she can hang onto his [[Web Lighter|lighter]] after he jokes that he should quit smoking and [[Jonathan Sims|John]] absentmindedly agrees. She apologises for going with a plan he hates and heads off as [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] comes up the stairs

[[Jonathan Sims|John]] apologises for getting heated and they chat for a bit. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] imagines how it would have been if they had met and gotten together without anything supernatural going on and [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] points out that they would not have been together in such circumstances. They worked a normal job for almost a year and [[Jonathan Sims|John]] hated him. It took two years of crisis and trauma to get them together.

[[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] cannot imagine making a decision that would mean losing [[Jonathan Sims|John]] and is hurt that [[Jonathan Sims|John]] can make such a decision. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] becomes agitated and talks about causing the apocalypse. He cannot let anyone else feel the burden of that helpless guilt. He tears up and [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] holds him, trying to reassure him. They take a moment before heading back to the others.

Later, [[Melanie King|Melanie]] and [[Basira Hussain|Basira]] have done some scouting and they all formulate a plan. The gas main under the [[The Magnus Institute|Institute]] is guarded by old archivists that [[Melanie King|Melanie]] and [[Georgie Barker|Georgie]] will try to sneak past while [[Basira Hussain|Basira]] creates a diversion. [[Georgie Barker|Georgie]] will need a torch to navigate and if they spot her, she will also try to distract them while [[Melanie King|Melanie]] carries on alone. Once they find the gas main they will try to break it and back off once they smell gas, then set something burning and quickly get away. They do not know how big the explosion will be but hope that it will magically channel up into the tower instead of into the tunnels.

Meanwhile, [[Jonathan Sims|John]] and [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] will go up the tower to kill [[Jonah Magnus|Jonah]]. [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] insists that he be the one to kill him since he thinks [[Jonathan Sims|John]] would take [[Jonah Magnus|Jonah]]'s place if he did it. [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] is certain that he can kill [[Jonah Magnus|Jonah]] and he intends to make it painful.

They all agree that it is nice to have a plan for once and decide to enact it the next day once they have had a chance to rest and prepare themselves. As everyone heads off to do that, [[Basira Hussain|Basira]] takes a moment to thank [[Jonathan Sims|John]] for coming back for her and says that all they can do is try and do something worthwhile with the time they have left. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] agrees and they head off.
