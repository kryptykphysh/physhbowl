# Statement

[[Alison Killala]] loved [[Neil Lagorio]] or might have been in love with him, but it is difficult to say. One of her earliest memories is watching one of his movies, _Labyrinth of the Minotaur_. She has always had two passions: engineering and special effects. This led her towards working on animatronics. Nobody had made the animatronics move as well as [[Neil Lagorio|Neil]] did in his 1975 movie, _Agents of Orion_. She hunted the movie down and watched the scene with an animatronic robot over and over again. She was already obsessed with [[Neil Lagorio|Neil]]'s work by then, mostly with his sci-fi work from the late 70's— _Beyond Time_, _Under New York_, _The Crawling Ones_.

She had a fondness for his earlier works, but his animation and stop-motion work never grabbed her like his animatronics did. The way [[Neil Lagorio|Neil]] tells it, he split from his partner Gabe in 1972, and stop-motion did not really have the same charm after that. He always told her that, "He was a puppeteer at heart". [[Alison Killala|Alison]] could never figure out what performance school he went to, but he would twist his fingers into all sorts of intricate shapes until she could see strings flowing all over them.

Growing up as an 80s cinephile and a devotee to his art, [[Alison Killala|Alison]] had to learn to love horror. It was common knowledge that [[Neil Lagorio|Neil]] was deliberately seeking out darker and more grotesque works. _Dead Sky_, _The Nightmare Children_, and _Forty Winks_ all led to the culmination with _Toy Shop_. While it is considered a cult classic now, it was often deemed the end of [[Neil Lagorio|Neil]] Lagorio's career and was referred to as a video nasty.

[[Alison Killala|Alison]]'s own relationship with [[Neil Lagorio]] started in 1992, on the set of _Jewel of the Amazon_. The movie was a low-budget, rushed production that made [[Neil Lagorio|Neil]] seem miserable. She and [[Neil Lagorio|Neil]] became friends. She is not really sure how to describe [[Neil Lagorio]]: In many ways, he was his work, but she will say that there was no warmth to him. He was not unpleasant or cruel, but he might as well have been talking to an animatronic. [[Neil Lagorio|Neil]] would invite people [[Alison Killala|Alison]] was sure he hated to screenings of the original cuts of his movies. [[Alison Killala|Alison]] was quite jealous of this, but she now thinks it was for the best. Those invited never looked the same afterward.

She and [[Neil Lagorio|Neil]] stayed in touch and even worked together on _The Wire Runner_. He even kept in contact when she left to have her baby. It was not planned, and after finding childcare arrangements, she found herself unwelcome in the industry. It was not that people did not want to hire her, she was just not able to take part in the culture of drinking and networking alongside parenting. She only really heard about [[Neil Lagorio|Neil]]'s work from what she told him. He was disappointed by the animatronics in _Eagle Falls_ but was satisfied with his animatronics in _The Harvestmen_. It was around that time he was starting to experience symptoms. He later told her that his biggest regret was not being able to finish a movie titled _Dancer_.

[[Alison Killala|Alison]] worked for [[Neil Lagorio|Neil]] as a caretaker. [[Alison Killala|Alison]] is certain that [[Neil Lagorio|Neil]] did not have Parkinson's Disease. It was simply that over the years, he stopped being able to move on his own accord. The doctors were only able to name it as Parkinson's, but she knows that they are wrong. [[Neil Lagorio|Neil]] started throwing himself into a new project. He had devised a series of frames and pulleys to puppeteer him around his house. [[Alison Killala|Alison]] protested, but [[Neil Lagorio|Neil]] insisted that this was what he wanted, so [[Alison Killala|Alison]] obliged. The routine of her puppeteering him through a parody of domestic life continued. She barely even noticed when a harness was unnecessary to move him, and that the loops to move him were directly embedded into his body.

It was almost six months ago when the woman came to [[Neil Lagorio|Neil]]'s door. [[Alison Killala|Alison]] thought she was a film student at first, so she asked her to wait while she asked [[Neil Lagorio|Neil]] what he would like her to do, but then the woman turned her head to reveal a mass of white thread all over the side of her temple. The woman told [[Alison Killala|Alison]] to sit down, and she obliged. There was the sound of levers and pullies, indicating [[Neil Lagorio|Neil]] being walked down to the front door. She could not see his reaction, but she heard him call the woman [[Annabelle Cane|Annabelle]] without any tone of fear or despair. [[Annabelle Cane|Annabelle]] sent [[Alison Killala|Alison]] to the screening room, and the last thing she saw of [[Neil Lagorio|Neil]] was him dancing and weeping with tears of joy.

[[Alison Killala|Alison]] does not know how long she was watching the films for. According to her daughter, she had been missing for five months. When [[Annabelle Cane|Annabelle]] let her out, [[Alison Killala|Alison]] saw [[Neil Lagorio|Neil]] dead, wrapped in a white cocoon of thread. [[Annabelle Cane|Annabelle]] gave [[Alison Killala|Alison]] [[Neil Lagorio|Neil]]'s original cuts and told her to deliver them to [[The Magnus Institute]]. [[Alison Killala|Alison]] tried to resist, but she is done now. [[Annabelle Cane|Annabelle]] won and [[Alison Killala|Alison]] just wants to go home.
