# Pre-Statement

The episode begins with [[Elias Bouchard]] dialing the police to report a crime in progress. He identifies himself and asks to speak with a Chief Inspector, whereupon he informs the Inspector that [[Alice Daisy Tonner|Daisy Tonner]] will be coming to [[The Magnus Institute]] in the next 15 minutes to harm him. The police promise to send someone.

# Statement

Following this call, [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]] reads a letter written by [[Barnabas Bennett]] to [[Jonah Magnus]]. [[Barnabas Bennett|Barnabas]] is writing for help regarding a debt he has fallen into with [[Mordechai Lukas]]. [[Mordechai Lukas|Lukas]] demanded repayment of the debt, and promises [[Barnabas Bennett|Barnabas]] that the debt will be repaid "in kind".

Returning home, [[Barnabas Bennett|Barnabas]] seemed slightly disturbed that the coach he had taken seemed to have no driver. He reflects in this letter that he is usually glad that he has no one who he is close to apart from a brother, but he feels now lost in this loneliness and that events are occurring that he cannot explain. This letter was written in order to ask for help from [[Jonah Magnus|Magnus]], expressing a belief that [[Barnabas Bennett|Barnabas]] would not be left in that lonely place.

# Post-Statement

[[Elias Bouchard|Elias]] addresses [[Jonathan Sims]] directly in his reply, saying that [[Jonah Magnus|Magnus]] did leave [[Barnabas Bennett|Barnabas]] to fend for himself. It was within [[Jonah Magnus|Magnus]]' power to intervene, but he chose not to not out of malice or lack of caring, but because he was curious what would happen to [[Barnabas Bennett|Barnabas]] and wanted to observe events. [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]] affirms the point of [[The Magnus Institute]] is to watch and observe, which is something he claims [[Gertrude Robinson|Gertrude]] understood. He then says he is ready to begin as [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] enters to say that [[Jonathan Sims|Sims]] has returned to the [[The Magnus Institute|Institute]], along with [[Alice Daisy Tonnr|Daisy]] and [[Basira Hussain|Basira]].

[[Elias Bouchard|Elias]] asks for [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] to bring [[Melanie]](https://the-magnus-archives.fandom.com/wiki/Melanie_King "Melanie King") and [[Tim]](https://the-magnus-archives.fandom.com/wiki/Tim "Tim") to witness what is about to happen. He asks if John has a question for him, and John asks if [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]] killed Gertrude and [[Jurgen Leitner]](https://the-magnus-archives.fandom.com/wiki/Jurgen_Leitner "Jurgen Leitner"). [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]] revels in the feeling of being compelled to speak his story, and [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]] says he will answer this question of his own free will, but wants to wait for the other research assistants.

Once everyone gathers, [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]] affirms that all actions he has taken were under his own control and there was no compulsion involved. He admits to killing [[Gertrude Robinson]] to protect the Archives, and killed [[Jurgen Leitner]] to prevent him from telling [[Jonathan Sims|John]] too much too soon. When [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] asks about [[Sasha James|Sasha]], [[Jonathan Sims|John]] tells [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] that [[Sasha James|Sasha]] was replaced by [[Not-Sasha]] over a year ago - a fact that [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]] confirms. When questioned about [[The Distortion|Michael]], [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]] is dismissive and calls him an "irritant".

[[Alice Daisy Tonner|Daisy]] says she has had enough and tells everyone to back off, drawing a gun. A phone rings, and [[Rosie]](https://the-magnus-archives.fandom.com/wiki/Rosie_Zampano "Rosie Zampano") says that some police officers are here to see him. [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]] taunts [[Alice Daisy Tonner|Daisy]] saying she can arrest him now, before revealing that he has sent evidence to police headquarters exposing [[Alice Daisy Tonner|Daisy]]'s crimes. [[Alice Daisy Tonner|Daisy]] is prepared to go to jail in exchange for killing [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]], but [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]] says that in their effort to clean up her involvement they would kill anyone involved - including Basira.

As an alternative plan, [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]] presents an employment contract to [[Basira Hussain|Basira]] saying that if she signs this contract, he will allow [[Alice Daisy Tonner|Daisy]] to go. When asking how this is meant to prevent her from shooting [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]], [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]] says that he is the heart of the Institute and that if he dies, everyone employed by it will die as well. Unwilling to kill [[Basira Hussain|Basira]], [[Alice Daisy Tonner|Daisy]] backs down and [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]] says he will make use of [[Alice Daisy Tonner|Daisy]]'s services in the future.

Dismissing everyone else, [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]] congratulates [[Jonathan Sims|John]] on his work in the last few weeks. [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]] says he will not be giving any answers to him, that his job as [[The Archivist]] is to chronicle and experience events through the eyes of others. If [[Jonathan Sims|John]] merely got all the answers, it would not please [[The Eye|their master]].

[[Elias Bouchard|Elias]] confirms that he was the one sending the tapes to [[Jonathan Sims|John]], with the goal of preventing [[The Unknowing]]. [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]] states that it is a ritual being performed by [[The Stranger]] but again refuses to just tell [[Jonathan Sims|John]]. He claims that [[The Stranger]] is the antithesis to [[The Beholding]] and if [[Jonathan Sims|Jonathan]] does not strengthen his own abilities, he will not be strong enough to stop the ritual.

The last question [[Jonathan Sims|John]] has is if he is still human. [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]] asks what human is, but says that [[Jonathan Sims|John]] is still breathing, and still has most of his own will and is unlikely to end up like the creature [[MAG 53 - Crusader|found in Alexandria]].

The tape ends with [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]] welcoming [[Jonathan Sims|John]] back to work.
