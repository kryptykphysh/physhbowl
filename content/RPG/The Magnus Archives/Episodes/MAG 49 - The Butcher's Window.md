# Statement

[[Gregory Pryor]] is a private investigator who used to work for the police; he refuses to say why he left. In June 2007, [[[[Nicola Laredo|Nicola]] Laredo]] asked him to investigate her husband, [[Hector Laredo|Hector]]. She wanted a divorce, but due to their prenup, she wouldn't get anything out of the proceedings without evidence that he was cheating on her.

[[Hector Laredo|Hector]] worked for the IT department of a shipping and delivery company based in Liverpool Street. His wife didn't believe his recent claims of having to frequently work late and [[Gregory Pryor|Gregory]] decided to shadow him. [[Hector Laredo|Hector]] left work at 6pm and went to Barking, then south over the A13 and before arriving in an industrial estate. In one of the warehouses on River Road, he went into a warehouse marked 'For Sale'. A red SUV pulled up and two Ukrainians in tracksuits spoke to him. After a short exchange, they handed [[Hector Laredo|Hector]] a brown envelope obviously full of money, while [[Hector Laredo|Hector]] opened the briefcase to reveal a white package of what looked to be heroin.

Later, when [[Gregory Pryor|Gregory]] was still tailing [[Hector Laredo|Hector]], he lost the briefcase. He returned to the Ukrainians to explain, but they just gave him a note with an address and a message: "Ask for [[Jared Hopworth|Jared]]". The next day, [[Nicola Laredo|Nicola]] told [[Gregory Pryor|Gregory]] that [[Hector Laredo|Hector]] had not returned home last night and that she needed confirmation that he was dead, if he was. She agreed to pay [[Gregory Pryor|Gregory]] a large sum of money to get evidence of his death.

[[Gregory Pryor|Gregory]] went to the address from the note, a butcher's shop in Stockwell. Although meat dripped in the window, there was nothing inside. Past a thick, soundproof steel door was the backroom, inside which was a table with a tarpaulin over it. Removing the tarpaulin, [[Gregory Pryor|Gregory]] found [[Hector Laredo|Hector]], whose waxy skin suggested that he was dead. After taking a few photos he turned to leave, but saw a tall, bulky figure at the door to the building. He hid in a nearby locker and saw the 7-foot-tall man with too thick limbs walk in.

This man, most likely [[Jared Hopworth|Jared]], removed the tarpaulin from [[Hector Laredo|Hector]] before he reached inside him and pulled out a handful of ribs. As he did this, [[Hector Laredo|Hector]] awoke and screamed. [[Jared Hopworth|Jared]] twisted the ribs as though they were warm putty in his hand and crafted a braid. Seemingly dissatisfied, he lifted one of the floor tiles to reveal a glistening, teeth-lined hole into which he threw the braid.

[[Hector Laredo|Hector]] quickly lost consciousness, but for the next four hours [[Jared Hopworth|Jared]] removed his bones, changed their shape and threw them into the hole. He even removed [[Hector Laredo|Hector]]'s femur, fashioned it into a spiral and placed it in himself. [[Hector Laredo|Hector]] eventually died, and [[Jared Hopworth|Jared]] sighed in disappointment. He dismembered the body and threw it down into the hole, then sat down and closed his eyes.

After a few minutes, [[Gregory Pryor|Gregory]] crept out of the locker and headed for the door. As he opened the thick steel door, an ambulance drove past, sirens blaring. [[Jared Hopworth|Jared]] leapt up and charged towards him. [[Gregory Pryor|Gregory]] was able to close the door on [[Jared Hopworth|Jared]], but not before [[Jared Hopworth|Jared]] grabbed his arm, removing the bones therein.

The police didn't find [[Jared Hopworth|Jared]], but [[Nicola Laredo|Nicola]] was happy with the result. His arm was eventually amputated, although he sometimes feels like it's still somewhere, moving in someone else's arm.

# Post-Statement

[[Jonathan Sims]] believes that this is clearly the [[Jared Hopworth]] from [[MAG 17 - The Boneturner's Tale]]. Although there's no account of the [[The Boneturner's Tale|book]], it's possible that [[Jared Hopworth|Jared]] doesn't need it anymore. [[Jonathan Sims|Jonathan]] isn't sure what to make of the pit, but mentions that it puts him in mind of other meaty cases.

[[Gregory Pryor|Gregory]] emigrated to New Zealand in 2013 following a tax evasion scandal. [[Nicola Laredo|Nicola]] confirmed the details of the case, but [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] let slip some of the more gory details of [[Hector Laredo|Hector]]'s death to the widow and [[Jonathan Sims|Jonathan]] suspects another complaint might be on the cards soon.

[[Sasha James]] is unable to use her usual technical skills to gain access to the police reports, supposedly because her computer has broken down. [[Timothy Stoker|Tim]] used his connections (romantic involvements with two staff members in the police office: one of the young ladies in one shift and the man who manages the other one) to get a copy of the police records, which explains that clothing belonging to at least 14 people was recovered from the butcher's shop, including 5 missing persons. There was nothing under the floor except the body of [[Harry Gough]], the owner of the establishment who has been dead for 6 months. 

[[Jared Hopworth]] is still at large.
