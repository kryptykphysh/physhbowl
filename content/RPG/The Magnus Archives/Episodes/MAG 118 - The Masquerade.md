# Recording

[[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] is gleefully burning statements while pointedly talking to an empty room. After three statements, [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]] shows up and orders him to stop. [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] taunts him through the locked door and [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]] is forced to go look for the key.

He returns shortly and confronts [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] about his newfound interest in arson. They argue and [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]] realises that [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] is intentionally wasting his time. He resolves to deal with him in the same manner as [[Melanie King|Melanie]] and [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] steels himself as [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]] prepares to deliver the perfect bit of emotionally devastating information.

He talks of [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]]'s mother. How [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] spent years caring for her and yet she was the one that asked to be moved into a nursing home and now she refuses his visits. She simply hates him and [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] does not know why. Of course, it is not really his fault. He was only 8 or 9 years old when she became ill and his father left them. [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] does not even really remember what he looks like.

[[Martin Blackwood|Martin]]'s composure begins to falter as [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]] tells him, he need only look in a mirror to find out _exactly_ what his father looked like. His mother never recovered from being abandoned and all these years she has been seeing the face of a man she hates as [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] cared for her. [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]] forces the raw knowledge of how she feels into his mind and [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] is reduced to tears. [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]] simply orders him not to burn any more statements as he leaves.

[[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] barely manages to compose himself before Melanie shows up. She is distraught at the state [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]] has left him in but she got what she needed from his office while [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] was distracting him. Melanie wants to take another stab at killing [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]] but [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] convinces her to leave with him.

In the [[House of Wax Museum|House of Wax]], [[Jonathan Sims|John]], [[Alice Daisy Tonner|Daisy]], [[Timothy Stoker|Tim]], and [[Basira Hussain|Basira]] have been planting explosives. [[Alice Daisy Tonner|Daisy]] is setting up the last of them when music starts playing from the main room.

They realise that the wax figures around them are not made from wax but from meat and they risk a peek into the main room to see [[The Anglerfish]] and a choir of skinless people. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] had assumed it simply ate its victims after skinning them but, clearly, you do not need skin to sing.

[[Timothy Stoker|Tim]] wants to try and save the people in the meat choir but [[Jonathan Sims|John]] does not want to risk it. [[Alice Daisy Tonner|Daisy]] finishes setting the explosives while they argue and hands [[Jonathan Sims|John]] the detonator. Before they can make their next move, [[Nikola Orsinov]] calls for the audience to take their places and announces that the show has begun.

[[The Unknowing]] starts and the world goes wrong.
