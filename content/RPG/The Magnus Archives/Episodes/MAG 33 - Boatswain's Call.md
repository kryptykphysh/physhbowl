# Statement

[[Carlita Sloane]] was working on a ship that was stopped due to some discrepancy, meaning she had to find work on another ship to leave Porto do Itaqui, which was difficult because even though she was a part of Nautilus, a UK Ship Union, she didn't speak Spanish (the "lingua franca" of most South American ports).

Eventually she found a ship that was bound for England: the Tundra. She found [[Peter Lukas]], the captain, in a local bar drinking black coffee. When she approached him the bar was suddenly empty. [[Peter Lukas|Peter]] said they had one position left and that they were planning to leave tomorrow at dawn.

The next day [[Carlita Sloane|Carlita]] went aboard and met [[Tadeas Dahl]], the first mate who was heavy set with a thick black beard and wore a brass boatswain's call. They set sail immediately. Whilst on the ship [[Carlita Sloane|Carlita]] noticed that the crew was exceptionally quiet and didn't talk and hardly replied to questions. She didn't see the captain at all whilst on the boat, but met [[Sean Kelly]], a Scottish man.

One day whilst working above deck she noticed that the shipping containers were rusted together and shut, with a new layer of paint over them. One night she decided to open one of the containers and found it totally empty. Just after this, the first mate came around gathering the crew. They went into the lifeboats, which were regular rowing boats, and travelled away from the Tundra.

When at sea, [[Tadeas Dahl|Tadeas]] blew the [[Boatswain's Call (artefact)|boatswain's call]], a shrill, high and piercing but distant sound, as though it was far away. A thick sea smoke rolled in, totally hiding the Tundra. [[Carlita Sloane|Carlita]] realised that [[Sean Kelly|Sean]] was not on the boat. Eventually the smoke cleared and they returned to the ship. She didn't see [[Sean Kelly|Sean]] again. The crew became more talkative, but when asking about [[Sean Kelly|Sean]] she was told by [[Kim Dwong]] (the third mate) that "It hadn't been an easy choice".

When they arrived at Southampton, she returned home. A few days later her paycheck came through for £25,000, which was not bad for a few days work.

# Post-Statement

The Tundra is still active, working for [[Solus Shipping]] PLC, which is owned by [[Nathaniel Lukas]. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] notes that the [[Lukas Family]] provides funding for various academic and research organisations, including [[The Magnus Institute]]. He cannot look into this any further, even though there are similarities between this case and [[MAG 13 - Alone]], as [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]] does not like him looking into cases that might have "Funding repercussions".

The Tundra crew has stayed the same for the past 10 years, there is no record of cargo being loaded or unloaded in the UK.

[[Sean Kelly|Sean]] Kelly went missing from the port of Felixstowe in October 2010, his body was found on a beach in Morocco in April 2011. Although he was missing for 6 months, his body had only been in the water for 5 days.
