# Pre-Statement

[[Jonathan Sims|John]] encounters [[The Distortion|Helen]] at her [[Domains|domain]]. She comments on the fact that [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] is not with him as he did not want [[Jonathan Sims|John]] to be with him [[MAG 186 - Quiet|in his domain]]. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] reveals he does not actually want to kill [[The Distortion|Helen]] and does not want [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] to ask him to kill her. He enters into the domain as the only other path is, "Rather full of spiders".

[[The Distortion|Helen]] promises it will be fun but is upset that [[Jonathan Sims|John]] immediately Knows the way through. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] does not trust or like [[The Distortion|Helen]] and is still upset about her taking [[Helen Richardson]] even if it was to, "Save him," from Michael. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] says he liked [[Helen Richardson]] and felt solidarity towards her paranoia. He never got to know her enough to learn about the negative parts of her and though he feels he could have grown to dislike her, he never got the chance and is instead stuck disliking [[The Distortion|Helen]]. He asks her to show him the quickest way through the domain instead of trying to hide her victims or stalling to try and figure out if there is a way to destroy him.

He asks how long she has been working with [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]] and though she denies it, he does not trust her. She does not want him and [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] to find a way to reverse [[The Change]]. [[The Distortion|Helen]] has been, "Nice," to him and that has always made him distrust her even more because of the nature of [[The Spiral]]. He asks her to manifest a room that he can do a statement in and she obliges despite commenting that the room will be made of her.

# Statement

**Note:** This statement is voiced by both [[The Distortion|Helen]] and [[Jonathan Sims|John]]

A woman walks through the corridors of the hotel. She is looking for her son. The lady at reception had said to walk in a certain direction, but it led her to a dead end. She feels guilty that she has left her son all alone in this hotel, and is trying to get to him as fast as possible. She bangs on every door, finally noticing that she has blisters. How many doors has she knocked on?

She sees [[The Distortion|Helen]] up ahead, and asks her to help find her son. [[The Distortion|Helen]] says that it would be a pleasure, and would love nothing more to help them. Though she does hate to see guests leave. Her arm is bleeding but she does not remember cutting it. [[The Distortion|Helen]] bandages her arm and it starts to bleed more. There is something behind [[The Distortion|Helen]]'s smile that is not quite right, but she looks away. She becomes more disoriented, and continues to knock on doors. She tries to ignore the blood on the carpet, her haggard reflection in the mirrors, and the laughing and smiling and the chatter that has followed her since the reception. She then hears [[Jonathan Sims|John]] recording his statement, and heads toward him.

# Post-Statement

The woman stumbles upon [[Jonathan Sims|John]] and [[The Distortion|Helen]] and asks for help to find room 288, where she thinks her son is. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] tells her that there is no room 288 but [[The Distortion|Helen]] steps in to "help" her find it. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] tells her [[The Distortion|Helen]] is lying which causes the woman to realize that [[The Distortion|Helen]] is the one who has been tormenting her. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] and [[The Distortion|Helen]] argue while the woman continues to beg for help. She reaches out for [[Jonathan Sims|John]] but he yells at her not to touch him and pulls back, causing her fall to the floor crying. [[The Distortion|Helen]] comments on how monstrous he is acting right now and removes the woman after [[Jonathan Sims|John]] threatens to make her an [[Avatar]].

[[Jonathan Sims|John]] has come to a decision after seeing [[The Distortion|Helen]] interact with her victim and is going to destroy her. He was hoping he was wrong about her and that she genuinely cared but now realizes she is too dangerous to be kept alive. She convinced them that she was still a friend but it was just a distortion of the truth. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] says she is "the question of what lurks behind a smile." She hurt and helped them at the same time without ever seeming like she fully betrayed them, and does not even really have motive for it. She wants to stop [[Jonathan Sims|John]] from turning back the world since it is perfect for The Spiral and could be a serious threat to [[Jonathan Sims|John]] and [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]].

She says that she will not do anything but [[Jonathan Sims|John]] begins trying to destroy her. He tries several times but she keeps changing herself and her location to make it impossible to focus [[The Eye]] on her. [[The Distortion|Helen]] is actually scared but lies about it. She offers him a door to leave but he refuses to take it, instead grasping onto the lie like a string, finally managing to focus The Eye on her and unravels her entire being while she pleads for him to spare her. Destroying her unravels the domain as well since it was also [[The Distortion]], so [[Jonathan Sims|John]] leaves.

Outside he finds [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] and tiredly tells him that he killed [[The Distortion|Helen]]. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] asks about [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]]'s domain, and receives some vague comments about self reflection. [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] is somewhat upset about the death of [[The Distortion|Helen]] and takes some time to mourn, but understands that [[Jonathan Sims|John]] had reason to do it.
