# Statement

[[Sebastian Adekoya]] was a true bibliophile and started work in Chiswick Library in 1995.

In September 1996, he found himself checking in a book that was listed in the library system as _Trainspotting_ by Irvine Welsh but it was a thick black paperback titled _[[The Boneturner's Tale (artefact)|The BoneturnRr's Tale]]_ in a white serif font with no obvious author. The other librarian, [[Ruth Weaver]], had no recollection of it or how it had come to be in the collection - it had a library bookplate and date stamps going back many years. [[Sebastian Adekoya|Sebastian]] did a cursory search into the man who had returned it, one [[Michael Crew]], but found nothing amiss other than 3 weeks prior, he had checked out 4 other books, which he returned at the same time as this one.

It was at this point that [[Jared Hopworth]] showed up. He was a childhood friend and neighbour but had become something of a crook when [[Sebastian Adekoya|Sebastian]] went to university, [[Sebastian Adekoya|Sebastian]] theorises that [[Jared Hopworth|Jared]] may have saw his leaving to university as some sort of betrayal.

_At this point in the recording, [[Jonathan Sims|Sims]] was interrupted by [[Elias Bouchard]], saying that [[MAG 13 - Alone|Miss Herne]] had lodged a complaint. [[Jonathan Sims|Sims]] gets annoyed, suggesting she had wasted his time and that [[Rosie Zampano|Rosie]] didn't keep her equipment in good condition, which forced him to do the recording. [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]] warns him not to annoy anyone connected to the [[Lukas Family]] as they are patrons of the [[The Magnus Institute|Institute]]. He also asks where [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] is, [[Jonathan Sims|Sims]] says he's ill with stomach problems.

[[Jared Hopworth|Jared]] started harassing [[Sebastian Adekoya|Sebastian]]. His appearance at the library led to some petty torment before he abruptly left, taking _[[The Boneturner's Tale (artefact)|The Boneturner's Tale]]_ with him. [[Sebastian Adekoya|Sebastian]] noted something of a fearful look about his eyes.

That evening, as he was returning home, [[Sebastian Adekoya|Sebastian]] found a pet rat with a strangely flat back and awkward neck half-crawling down the street. This was seen in the orange glow of the streetlamp outside the Hopworth home.

In late October, [[Sebastian Adekoya|Sebastian]] had heard nothing more from [[Jared Hopworth|Jared]] which was slightly concerning until the day that [[Jared Hopworth|Jared]]'s mother came to the library. Her arm was in a sling and she was wearing a bulky coat as she threw _[[The Boneturner's Tale (artefact)|The Boneturner's Tale]]_ at him, telling him to stay away from [[Jared Hopworth|Jared]] and keep his books to himself. [[Sebastian Adekoya|Sebastian]] was careful not to touch the book when putting it away, somewhat disconcerted by the angle her arm had appeared to be at.

That night [[Sebastian Adekoya|Sebastian]] wasn't able to sleep for worry over the book. He returned to the library at 2am, only to find all the books on the cart surrounding _[[The Boneturner's Tale (artefact)|The Boneturner's Tale]]_ were bleeding. Carefully and with gloves on, [[Sebastian Adekoya|Sebastian]] started leafing through the book. It told a story with unsettling details that appeared to describe some of the pilgrims from Chaucer's _Canterbury Tales_. Desperate for more details as to how it ended up here, [[Sebastian Adekoya|Sebastian]] peeled back the library's lending label, finding a plaque from a library he assumed was Scandinavian.

Before he could read further, the sound of breaking glass brought a tall, cloaked stranger into the library, who demanded the book in a voice rather like [[Jared Hopworth|Jared]]'s. He had long pointed fingers and looked longer, more angular, than the [[Jared Hopworth|Jared]] he knew. [[Sebastian Adekoya|Sebastian]] punched him and noted that he felt like he was being drawn into [[Jared Hopworth|Jared]]'s chest by his sharp ribs. Screaming, he dropped the book at which [[Jared Hopworth|Jared]] released him, grabbed the book and fled, with [[Sebastian Adekoya|Sebastian]] noting a marked increase in the number of limbs that [[Jared Hopworth|Jared]] now had. The police showed up shortly after, alerted by his screams, and [[Sebastian Adekoya|Sebastian]] never saw [[Jared Hopworth|Jared]] or the book again.

# Post-Statement

This is the second book from the Library of [[Jurgen Leitner]] that has turned up. [[Jonathan Sims|Jonathan]] says that he'll talk to [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]] to see what they could do about it. [[Timothy Stoker|Tim]] and [[Sasha James|Sasha]] found police reports relating to [[Jared Hopworth]] and the break-in, though he was never found. The book appears to have vanished as well. [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] had a look for [[Sebastian Adekoya|Sebastian]], who was apparently killed in a hit-and-run on 17th April 2006 and although no blunt force trauma was recorded, the body had been found in a rather twisted pose.
