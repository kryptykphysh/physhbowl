# Pre-Statement

[[Jonathan Sims]] travels to Chicago in an attempt to track down the files [[Gertrude Robinson|Gertrude]] requested from the [[Pu Songling Research Centre]] in 2014. He finds no information there except that she had left a forwarding address to the [[Usher Foundation]] in Washington DC. He travels to Pittsburgh in the hopes of finding more information on [[Gerard Keay]], as according to reports he died of complications from his brain cancer in the city. He discovers that [[Gertrude Robinson|Gertrude]] was arrested for breaking into the morgue and doing something to [[Gerard Robinson|Gerard's]] body, but ultimately no charges were filed.

During this time [[Jonathan Sims|John]] grows increasingly ill, and he suspects a Chicago police officer is following him. [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]] sends him statement with a note attached reading, "To tide you over."

# Statement

[[Howard Ewing]], [[Kelly Dwyer]], and [[Vihan Prasad]] were cleaners working on Moorgate Station when a mysterious announcement came over unpowered loudspeakers: "This is a security announcement", then indistinct words that sounded as if they were coming from underwater. After a brief silence, there was a painfully loud shriek from the speakers. As his co-workers were recovering from the noise, [[Howard Ewing|Ewing]] noticed a foul smell coming from the tunnel at one end of the platform. He crawled down onto the tracks to see what the source of the smell was.

A short way down the tunnel, he beheld a burning train carriage, still full of screaming, writhing passengers. He fell to his knees at the sight, screaming. Over the loudspeakers, he heard a broadcaster calling for "Inspector Sands," a code used to inform station workers of a fire without alerting the public. [[Kelly Dwyer|Kelly]] attempted to enter the tunnel, while [[Vihan Prasad|Vihan]] shook [[Howard Ewing|Ewing]]'s shoulders and shouted at him to wake up. [[Howard Ewing|Ewing]] firmly believed that if he touched either of them he would burn them.

Both [[Kelly Dwyer|Kelly]] and [[Vihan Prasad|Vihan]] were found burned to death in the station, with no sign of the burning train, and [[Howard Ewing|Ewing]] was arrested and taken to a police station. The room became very hot while he waited to be questioned, and a man dressed as a chief inspector of the transit police entered the room to question him about the incident.

After he explained his story, the inspector asked [[Howard Ewing|Ewing]], "What do you love most in the world?" Against his will, [[Howard Ewing|Ewing]] says he loves his father more than anything. The inspector then asked, "Who am I?" but [[Howard Ewing|Ewing]] refused to answer.

[[Howard Ewing|Ewing]] was eventually released, but shortly after this his father died of a sudden heart attack. At the funeral, against [[Howard Ewing|Ewing]]'s wishes, the body was cremated. He believes he will see the inspector again, and will again be asked the same two questions. [[Howard Ewing|Ewing]] specifically refuses to name the inspector, because he "doesn't want to cause any panic"; implying that the policeman in question is in fact Inspector Sands.

# Post-Statement

[[Jonathan Sims|John]] feels refreshed after reading the statement, and while there may be a perfectly mundane explanation, he admits it is more likely that he physically needs to read statements to remain healthy. He resents that [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]] once again did not see fit to tell him about this directly or beforehand.

He departs on a bus to Washington, D.C., but during a comfort break he is accosted by an English woman who forces him into her car. She tells him that he has attracted the attention of something she'd been following. In the car, [[Jonathan Sims|John]] realizes the woman is [[Julia Montauk]], but they do not have much time to talk before they are pulled over by the police officer who was following [[Jonathan Sims|John]] in Chicago and Pittsburgh.

The officer, identified as [[Max Mustermann]], asks [[Julia Montauk|Julia]] and [[Jonathan Sims|John]] to step out of the car and open the trunk. He is immediately shot by [[Trevor Herbert]], who was hiding in the boot with a gun. This does not immediately kill [[Max Mustermann|Mustermann]], so Trevor asks [[Jonathan Sims|John]] to get an ax out of the car.
