# Statement

[[Mark Bilham]] does not believe in the supernatural and does not think what he experienced is esoteric or arcane. Indeed, he only made the statement to [[The Magnus Institute]] because his girlfriend, [[Katherine Harper]], suggested it.

[[Katherine Harper|Kathy]] graduated from university in 2014 and moved in with [[Natalie Ennis]], a friend from outside of university. [[Natalie Ennis|Natalie]] was quiet, nice and extremely religious. She and [[Mark Bilham|Mark]] did not get along, as they had nothing in common.

In October, [[Natalie Ennis|Natalie]]'s mum passed away and she became very introverted, and lost her faith. A few months after, [[Natalie Ennis|Natalie]] explained to [[Katherine Harper|Kathy]] that she had found a new congregation and became more introverted and ignored [[Katherine Harper|Kathy]] and [[Mark Bilham|Mark]] for the most part. She started to spend more time with her new church, spending most nights there, and the few days she spent at home she sang in a foreign language all night. [[Katherine Harper|Kathy]] started to hear movement in [[Natalie Ennis|Natalie]]'s room when she was not in.

The light bulbs kept breaking in the house, and on closer inspection [[Mark Bilham|Mark]] discovered that [[Natalie Ennis|Natalie]] had been unscrewing the light bulbs all throughout the house.

One night, [[Katherine Harper|Kathy]] arrived at [[Mark Bilham|Mark]]'s house in tears. [[Natalie Ennis|Natalie]] had asked her to dinner, and brought her into the kitchen, where a bowl of something like cold overcooked spinach lay waiting for her. [[Katherine Harper|Kathy]] took a few mouthfuls of it before she could not stomach it anymore. [[Natalie Ennis|Natalie]] said that she was going to move out, saying that she was going to a new home and a new family. She said that 300 years was a long time to wait, but she was lucky to find them so close to the end and that they were going to be collected by Mr. Pitch.

[[Natalie Ennis|Natalie]] offered [[Katherine Harper|Kathy]] a place with them, but [[Katherine Harper|Kathy]] said that she was not one for Christian worship, to which [[Natalie Ennis|Natalie]] laughed, saying "no, but you're a natural for them. You're worshipping as we speak." [[Katherine Harper|Kathy]] fled to [[Mark Bilham|Mark]]'s house.

[[Mark Bilham|Mark]] resolved to approach [[Natalie Ennis|Natalie]] about this, he drove over to the house and went into [[Natalie Ennis|Natalie]]'s room. In there, all the furniture, wallpaper and carpets had been removed and attached to the window, effectively blanking out any light coming into the room. He found a piece of paper in the room. On one side it said "Hither Green Dissenters", and the other side it had a curved line with four straight lines underneath it, like a stylized closed eye.

[[Mark Bilham|Mark]] found that there was a chapel called Hither Green Chapel in a graveyard in Lewisham. He drove there and found a run down chapel with boarded up windows and open doors. Inside he shone his light around and saw broken pews and general detritus. His torch failed and he removed the batteries to check them and dropped them.

As he felt around for the batteries, he realized that the floor was cold and smooth, like marble, without any of the rubbish there had been before. A foreign singing started, as though from different throats, but they all came together something that sounded like "Ny-Ålesund". He walked forwards, looking for a wall, but although the chapel was small he walked for a full minute before finding anything. It was a metal grate, rusted metal in a criss-cross pattern, as he removed his hand he felt leathery fingers grabbing for him. He fell back and remembered his phone, as the singing crescendoed he lit the light on the phone and saw that he was back in the chapel.

He called the police and filed a missing person's report for [[Natalie Ennis|Natalie]].

# Post-Statement

[[Sasha James|Sasha]] confirmed that [[Natalie Ennis|Natalie]] was reported as missing by [[Mark Bilham|Mark]] on 11th March 2015, but there was no trace of her, the church or any cult at the chapel. Neither [[Mark Bilham|Mark]] nor [[Katherine Harper|Kathy]] have heard from [[Natalie Ennis|Natalie]] since.

The stylised closed eye sounds similar to [[The People's Church of the Divine Host]], leading [[Jonathan Sims]] to think the church may still be in existence.

The word may have been "[[Ny-Ålesund]]", which is the northernmost major settlement in the world and is owned by [[Outer Bay Shipping]]. Winter - and nighttime - lasts for a long time that far north.

[[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] found another police report a month after this statement. On 15th May 2015 police were called to the chapel again as there were screams heard after 11pm, although no cause of the noise was found. This concerns [[Jonathan Sims]] because this is the same day that [[Gertrude Robinson]] passed away.
