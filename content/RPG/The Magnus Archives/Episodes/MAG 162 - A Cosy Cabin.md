# Pre-Statement

[[Gertrude Robinson|Gertrude]] catches [[Gerard Keay]] snooping through her stationery cupboard. They chat for a bit before he asks her about a book he only refers to as _The Travels_ (likely _The Travels of Marco Polo_), [[Gertrude Robinson|Gertrude]] figures it will have nothing to offer them that is worth the danger and asks him to burn it. She chides him when it becomes apparent that he has brought the book into the Archives and tells him to go burn it in the back courtyard.

[[Gerard Keay|Gerard]] asks her what happens if they fail to stop a [[ritual]](https://the-magnus-archives.fandom.com/wiki/Rituals "Rituals"). [[Gertrude Robinson|Gertrude]] thinks the best they can hope for is to die in their attempt to stop it. She suspects that if an [[Entity]](https://the-magnus-archives.fandom.com/wiki/The_Entities "The Entities") were to fully manifest, it could fully rewrite the laws of nature. It could possibly even stop death entirely, keeping people alive and afraid forever. Beyond that, the state of the world would vary greatly depending on which Entity is manifested.

[[Gerard Keay|Gerard]] raises another hypothetical, if a ritual were to succeed, could it be undone? [[Gertrude Robinson|Gertrude]] does not think so.

In the present, [[Jonathan Sims]] rewinds the tape several times to listen to her say, "No. I don’t think so," before he finishes the tape and starts another one.

Still in the Archives, shortly after [[Jonathan Sims|John]]'s promotion to head archivist. [[Sasha James|Sasha]] ropes [[Timothy Stoker|Tim]] into helping her staple statements and they gossip about their new boss. [[Timothy Stoker|Tim]] insists that [[Sasha James|Sasha]] is more qualified and should have gotten the job instead, he is certain [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]] only promoted [[Jonathan Sims|John]] instead because of sexist prejudice and urges her to say something about it. [[Sasha James|Sasha]] is less confrontational, she figures that she will only get blacklisted if she makes a fuss about it. She might just look for a new job instead.

[[Timothy Stoker|Tim]] mentions their past hookup and they joke around for a bit. On a more serious note, she knows [[Timothy Stoker|Tim]] will not leave because of his brother and [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] cannot go anywhere that will look too closely at his CV, but she has nothing keeping her in the Archives.

The topic turns to [[Gertrude Robinson|Gertrude]] and [[Timothy Stoker|Tim]] thinks the Archives are a mess because she was just old and starting to lose it. [[Sasha James|Sasha]], who has actually talked to [[Gertrude Robinson|Gertrude]], knows she was still sharp as a knife and the messy state of the Archives must be intentional, but she cannot figure out why.

# Statement

In the present, the tape clicks off. A static rises in the background and [[Jonathan Sims|John]] begins to speak as if he is reading a statement. He speaks of the cabin they reside in and its false promises of comfort and relief. The land outside is twisted and torn but inside, he can ignore the screams that linger on the breeze. Inside, there is safety.

The cabin urges them to stay, to cling to their love so tightly that it withers and warps as they live in shaking fear of the things that seek to take it from them. The cabin wishes to be their tomb as they putrefy in body and soul, but [[The Eye]] does not wish it so.

"No," [[Jonathan Sims|John]] says, "the Eye wishes instead that it be my chrysalis. It is time that I emerge."

# Post-Statement

[[Jonathan Sims|John]] comes back to his usual self with a start and [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] comes in to see what the commotion is about. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] tries to explain what just happened: As he was listening, he was filled with hatred and the urge to leave and hunt down [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]]. Then he felt how the cabin did not want them to leave and it all came pouring out of him, into the tape, and it felt _right_.

To [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]]'s surprise [[Jonathan Sims|John]] wants to leave and head back to the Archives. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] cautions him that it will be a hard journey but [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] is already rambling about all the things he has packed, he has been ready for a while.

They do not know if there is any way to undo [[The Change]], but they are determined to find out for themselves.
