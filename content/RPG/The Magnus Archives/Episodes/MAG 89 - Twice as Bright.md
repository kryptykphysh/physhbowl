# Pre-Statement

[[Jonathan Sims|Jonathan]] refused to shake [[Jude Perry]]'s hand when he found her. She confirms that she and others burnt down [[Gwydir Forest]] because [[Nikola Orsinov]] asked her to. She explains that she could reach into [[Jonathan Sims|John's]] chest and burn his heart, but decides not to.

[[Jonathan Sims|Jonathan]] says that he believes he serves [[The Eye|Beholding]], as [[Jude Perry|Jude]] serves [[The Lightless Flame]], which she calls [[The Desolation]]. She says she will not kill him as a favour to [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]], as repayment for him killing [[Gertrude Robinson]]. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] starts to compel [[Jude Perry|Jude]] to explain why she wanted [[Gertrude Robinson|Gertrude]] dead, but she burns him, telling him to stop. Regardless, she decides to give him some advice.
# Statement

[[Jude Perry|Jude]] explains that flames must be fed or they go out. She used to be a stockbroker at a bank that has gone out of business due to frequent fires (it is implied that [[Jude Perry|Jude]] set them). When working at the bank, she became depressed, or "burnt out" before she met [[Agnes Montague]]. The two talked about sacrifice and power, as they did, strangers gathered around them. One of them, Sandy, was dismissive of [[Jude Perry|Jude]]. When Sandy squatted down to stare at [[Jude Perry|Jude]] and said, "I don't think so," to [[Agnes Montague|Agnes]], [[Jude Perry|Jude]] pushed her away but her hand sank into her waxlike flesh. After [[Jude Perry|Jude]] removed her hand from Sandy's now-misshapen head, Sandy stood up, reshaped it, and nodded her approval to [[Agnes Montague|Agnes]].

[[Jude Perry|Jude]] knew she had to make a sacrifice to [[The Desolation]], someone who had value. She chose her colleague and friend [[Nicolas Trikenzo]], whose wife, [[Julie Trikenzo|Julie]], had just given birth to their first child: [[Desmond Trikenzo|Desmond]]. She got him drunk and stabbed him to death. After she did, his body burnt and she felt her senses sharpen as she felt the warmth of [[The Desolation]]. The death wasn't the thing that it craved, but the destruction of his life. She also burnt down his new house and forged his signature to have his wife's assets seized. She still keeps an eye on Desmond to see if there is anything worth taking from him.

[[Jude Perry|Jude]] continued to burn people around her but, thanks to help from other members of [[The Cult of the Lightless Flame]], she was never caught. She was living for the pain that she caused and for [[Agnes Montague|Agnes]]. 

[[Jude Perry|Jude]] doused herself in petrol in front of Gretchen, her girlfriend, and immolated herself, feeling her bones crack and pop.

# Post-Statement

[[Jonathan Sims|John]] starts to ask more questions, but [[Jude Perry|Jude]] says that she will kill him if he compels her. She confirms that she met [[Agnes Montague|Agnes]] in 1989 and completed her 'transformation' in 1991, putting her in her 50s. [[Jonathan Sims|Jonathan]] notes that she does not look 50 and she replies, "Wax is easy to mould", changing her face.

[[Jonathan Sims|John]] asks what he can feed the [[The Eye|Beholding]], but she does not know. He starts to leave, muttering about dead ends, but [[Jude Perry|Jude]] stops him, saying she knows where [[Michael Crew]] is and that he hangs around with [[Fairchild Family|the Fairchilds]], stating he is closer to "your lot than mine". She says the price for this information is [[Jonathan Sims|John]] shaking her hand, which he does, screaming in pain as she burns his hand.
