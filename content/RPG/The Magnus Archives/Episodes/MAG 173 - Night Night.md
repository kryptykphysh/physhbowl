# Pre-Statement

[[Jonathan Sims|John]] and [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] are walking down a dark street; a [[Domains|domain]] of [[The Dark]]. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] is unsettled and would rather leave, and when [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] asks why he explains that there are children in this domain, as they did not simply go away when [[The Change]] happened. [[The Eye]] prefers the more complex neuroses and disquiets of a fully developed mind. So children are allowed to age and are placed into domains where their fears can mature.

[[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] asks him to kill whoever is tormenting the children, but [[Jonathan Sims|John]] says that it is more complicated than that. He approaches a house and knocks on the door.

The door is opened by [[Callum Brodie]]; the child kidnapped by [[Maxwell Rayner]] in 2017. [[Callum Brodie|Callum]] recognises [[Jonathan Sims|John]] as '[[The Eye]] guy', and it is shown that [[Callum Brodie|Callum]] is now an [[Avatar]] of [[The Dark]]. He states that everyone is afraid of him now and that his parents are either dead or absent. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] and [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] leave his house. They discuss [[Callum Brodie|Callum]]; [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] wants [[Jonathan Sims|John]] to use his powers to "make things better," but [[Jonathan Sims|John]] states that there is no "better." He asks what [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] wants him to do and [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] asks him to give him a statement. Both [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] and [[Jonathan Sims|John]] are uncertain of this, but at [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]]'s insistence [[Jonathan Sims|John]] does so.

# Statement

The statement follows a child, Jack, as he runs through the darkness of Night Street, trying to escape monsters that he cannot see but knows are there. He runs into the dark because everywhere is dark, and because if he runs then the monsters cannot get him. Jack encounters [[Callum Brodie|Callum]], who tells him about a new monster. Every time [[Callum Brodie|Callum]] tells Jack about a new monster, Jack must run from this monster too, though he still cannot see it. Jack doesn't want to hear about the new monster, but [[Callum Brodie|Callum]] will "put him in a hole for a very long time" if he doesn't listen, and "friends don't tell each other to be quiet." The only way to stop the new monster is with a torch, but there are no torches here. Jack continues to run.

The statement then changes to follow Kaitlyn, who is hiding in her wardrobe as monsters search through her room for her. She cannot see them but she can hear them, and knows that they look like the deep-sea fish she saw once in a picture book that gave her nightmares for weeks. Her mother is downstairs, laughing at the TV, and does not like it when Kaitlyn is awake, so Kaitlyn is quiet. She finds [[Callum Brodie|Callum]] hiding in the wardrobe with her and tells him to go away because she is trying to hide; [[Callum Brodie|Callum]] shouts to draw the monsters to her, so Kaitlyn leaves the wardrobe and runs to find another hiding place.

# Post-Statement

[[Jonathan Sims|John]] asks if [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] would like to hear more, and begins to tell the story of Luka who sleeps, but [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] cuts off the new statement. He says that it did not help, and [[Jonathan Sims|John]] apologises. [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] says that they just need to get to the Archives to stop this. They resume walking.
