# Pre-Statement

[[Basira Hussain|Basira]], [[Jonathan Sims|John]], and [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] are walking through a domain of [[The Spiral]], which appears as a mental health facility called [[Wonderland House]]. [[Basira Hussain|Basira]] is eager to find [[Alice Daisy Tonner|Daisy]] but [[Jonathan Sims|John]] states that she has already moved on from this place. They stop to have a conversation, during which [[Jonathan Sims|John]] shares that he inadvertently caused the apocalypse and that [[Basira Hussain|Basira]] would not be able to kill him now (and doing so would not fix the world). [[Basira Hussain|Basira]] explains that when [[Trevor Herbert|Trevor]] and [[Julia Montauk|Julia]] attacked the [[The Magnus Institute|Institute]], she lost track of [[Alice Daisy Tonner|Daisy]] and [[Julia Montauk|Julia]]; [[Jonathan Sims|John]] shares that [[Alice Daisy Tonner|Daisy]] killed [[Julia Montauk|Julia]], and [[Trevor Herbert|Trevor]] had been hunting [[Alice Daisy Tonner|Daisy]] since.

[[Basira Hussain|Basira]] was still in the [[The Magnus Institute|Institute]] when the apocalypse occurred, which she thinks protected her from the initial effects. Since then, she has been following [[Trevor Herbert|Trevor]], thinking he would lead her to [[Alice Daisy Tonner|Daisy]] so she could keep her promise to her. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] also apologizes to [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] for using him as bait for [[Trevor Herbert|Trevor]].

[[Jonathan Sims|John]] reveals to [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] that they are located in Wonderland House, a mental health facility. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] takes a moment to Know [[Alice Daisy Tonner|Daisy]]’s location, and [[Basira Hussain|Basira]] and [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] talk about what [[Jonathan Sims|John]] and [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] have seen, their plan to kill [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]], and that [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] trusts [[Jonathan Sims|John]] not to Know things about him. They set off on the correct route, and [[Jonathan Sims|John]] stops, needing to make a statement. [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] explains the process to [[Basira Hussain|Basira]] and then states he will keep lookout, though [[Basira Hussain|Basira]] wants to listen in.

# Statement

The statement is presented as a series of three second-person conversations, where a figure known as [[Dr. David]] speaks to a patient at [[Wonderland House]]. These conversations all contain similar themes, in which [[Dr. David]] overrides previous diagnoses and prescriptions, tells the patient that they are lying about their illnesses, belittles them, and gaslights them about their previous conversations. Only [[Dr. David]]’s portion of each conversation is heard but it appears that the patient attempts to protest, both verbally and physically. The overall theme of each conversation is [[Dr. David]] attempting to convince the patient that their mental health problems are not actually real.

# Post-Statement

After the statement ends, the three find a body, killed by [[Alice Daisy Tonner|Daisy]] – a figure [[Basira Hussain|Basira]] recognizes, a criminal named [[Noah Thompson]] who was arrested by [[Alice Daisy Tonner|Daisy]], and dodged a criminal charge for blinding another man. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] tells them that [[Noah Thompson|Noah]] blinded the other man in a panic when he was running away from [[Alice Daisy Tonner|Daisy]]; the court believed him, but [[Basira Hussain|Basira]] believed [[Alice Daisy Tonner|Daisy]].

[[The Distortion|Helen]] appears and offers [[Basira Hussain|Basira]] a shortcut to [[Alice Daisy Tonner|Daisy]] through her hallways. [[Basira Hussain|Basira]] declines, stating that she will stay with [[Jonathan Sims|John]] and [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]]. [[The Distortion|Helen]] is disappointed but bids them farewell. [[Jonathan Sims|John]], [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]], and [[Basira Hussain|Basira]] continue on.
