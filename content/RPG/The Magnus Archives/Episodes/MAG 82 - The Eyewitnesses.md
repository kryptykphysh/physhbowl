# Pre-Statement

[[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] starts to give a statement to [[Alice Daisy Tonner|Daisy Tonner]], but she interrupts him, saying that she just wants [[Martin blackwood|Martin]] to tell her where [[Jonathan Sims|Jonathan]] is. She also reveals that [[Basira Hussain|Basira]] is no longer a police officer. Although she listened to the tape with [[Jurgen Leitner|Leitner's]] murder, she is dubious, and threatens to pin the crime on [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]]. He explains that he does not know where [[Jonathan Sims|Jonathan]] is or where he could be. [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] asks about [[Sasha James|Sasha]], but [[Alice Daisy Tonner|Daisy]] does not give him any information.

[[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] goes to get [[Timothy Stoker|Tim]] under [[Alice Daisy Tonner|Daisy]]'s instructions and she turns off the recorder, which turns itself back on. Tim explains that he does not know where [[Jonathan Sims|Jonathan]] or [[Sasha James|Sasha]] are. After a brief exchange, [[Alice Daisy Tonner|Daisy]] asks Tim to send in [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]].

[[Alice Daisy Tonner|Daisy]] asks [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]] how to turn the tape recorder off, but he says that [[Jonathan Sims|Jonathan]] will want to review the tapes when he gets back. [[Alice Daisy Tonner|Daisy]] confesses that they do not know the identity of the body in the Archives. When asked, [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]] explains that he does know where [[Jonathan Sims|John]] is, but won't tell the detective. She threatens to take him to the station, but [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]] explains that she has become sloppy, not following up on leads or taking evidence and that she is just hunting for [[Jonathan Sims|John]] with her own agenda. When [[Alice Daisy Tonner|Daisy]] threatens to harm [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]], he explains that she will not because of Calvin Benchley. He explains that he knows what she did and he is going to make her statement to prove it, and so [[Jonathan Sims|John]] can hear it one day. He also threatens to go public with this.

# Statement

[[Elias Bouchard|Elias]] speaks in first person as [[Alice Daisy Tonner|Daisy]]. She is called "[[Alice Daisy Tonner|Daisy]]" instead of "Alice" because she has a scar on her back which the doctor described as a daisy. She likes to be called that because it sounds soft, while the scar proves she is strong.

When she was 11 she was friends with Calvin and they would play in a nearby park and old building site. One day, she dared him to look in the building site after they heard a commotion. He did and when he had not returned after 15 minutes, she followed him. In the building she saw her first two dead bodies, which had apparently killed each other, and she felt both afraid and excited. Behind them on top a staircase was Calvin, looking blank. Behind him was a naked, hunched creature covered in bloodless open wounds. Its mouth moved quickly, speaking silently into Calvin's ear, with its a black tongue flicking into his ear.

Calvin ran at [[Alice Daisy Tonner|Daisy]], slamming her into a fence, causing the metal to stab into her back. She lost consciousness as Calvin stood proudly over her. She later awoke in a hospital. Calvin said that she had fallen. At school, he became more moody, got into more fights, and enjoyed scaring people; 6 children were killed or seriously injured by the time he left for university. He was arrested once but nothing stuck.

Six years ago, [[Alice Daisy Tonner|Daisy]] beat him up, took him to her special spot and shot him five times. It was harder than dealing with [[Vampires|vampires]]. He was the first human she killed, but not the last.

# Post-Statement

[[Alice Daisy Tonner|Daisy]] has repeatedly implored [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]] to stop during the statement. [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]] tells [[Alice Daisy Tonner|Daisy]] to leave. He is not going to tell her where [[Jonathan Sims|John]] is but he will not stop her hunting him, and suggests that if she cannot leave well enough alone, she should kill him quickly, as "there's no telling" what [[The Archivist|the archivist]] might be capable of. [[Alice Daisy Tonner|Daisy]] leaves.

Outside, [[Timothy Stoker|Tim]] tells [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] he believes that [[Jonathan Sims|John]] killed [[Sasha James|Sasha]], and if it was not him it must have been [[The Distortion|Michael]].
