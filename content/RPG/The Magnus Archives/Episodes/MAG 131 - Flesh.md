# Pre-Statement

[[Jonathan Sims|John]] is attempting and failing to cut off his finger. [[Melanie King|Melanie]] enters and asks [[Jonathan Sims|John]] what he is doing and he claims to be trying to save [[Alice Daisy Tonner|Daisy]]. [[Melanie King|Melanie]] is unimpressed with [[Jonathan Sims|John]] and bitterly angry with him and [[Basira Hussain|Basira]] after they performed surgery to remove the bullet from her leg without her consent.

[[Melanie King|Melanie]] tells [[Jonathan Sims|John]] how she has regular nightmares about the experience and that her already-fragile sense of safety has been thoroughly destroyed. When [[Jonathan Sims|John]] tries to downplay the severity of the violation and of [[Melanie King|Melanie]]'s anger toward the world, she becomes extremely distraught. Composing herself, she explains that while the bullet affirmed her anger and allowed her to create an identity out of it, it did not create her rage or desire for violence, and its continued presence in her body was because she allowed it to stay.

[[Melanie King|Melanie]] abruptly changes the subject and asks [[Jonathan Sims|John]] why he is trying to cut off his finger. He explains that he needs an anchor for when he goes into [[The Coffin]] and thinks one of body parts would work best, but when he tries to cut off his finger the wound heals as soon as the blade is removed. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] dryly jokes about needing [[Jared Hopworth]], The Boneturner, to take a rib for him. After a pause, [[Melanie King|Melanie]] asks him to come with her.

They go downstairs and find one of [[The Distortion|Helen's]] doors. [[Melanie King|Melanie]] knocks on the door and says that [[The Distortion|Helen]] has been helping them, but [[Jonathan Sims|John]] is suspicious of her motivations. [[The Distortion|Helen]] answers, and she and [[Melanie King|Melanie]] explain that [[The Distortion|Helen]] helped dispose of various agents of [[The Flesh]] after [[Melanie King|Melanie]] killed them during their attack on [[The Magnus Institute]] some months prior. [[Jared Hopworth|Jared]] fled through one of [[The Distortion|Helen]]'s doors after [[Melanie King|Melanie]] attacked him and has been trapped in [[The Distortion|Helen]]'s corridors ever since. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] decides to go in and offer [[Jared Hopworth|Jared]] freedom in exchange for his services.

[[Jonathan Sims|John]] quickly finds [[Jared Hopworth|Jared]] in the corridors. He still wants to kill [[Jonathan Sims|John]], but knows he will never escape the corridors if he does. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] asks [[Jared Hopworth|Jared]] to take out one of his ribs, which he readily agrees to. When [[Jonathan Sims|John]] tries to compel [[Jared Hopworth|Jared]] to explain why he attacked the [[The Magnus Institute|Institute]], he firmly states that a statement will cost an additional rib, a condition [[Jonathan Sims|John]] reluctantly agrees to.

# Statement

Growing up, [[Jared Hopworth|Jared]]'s parents were bitter and he was often in trouble in school. He had little hope for his future until 1996, when a childhood friend of his, [[Sebastian Adekoya]], gave him a book: _[[The Boneturner's Tale]]_. [[Jared Hopworth|Jared]] was confused with the book at first, but the way it talked about the body deeply resonated with him. His first attempt at what the book had taught him was messy and unappreciated; his short father did not enjoy being made tall and his scowling mother did not like the smile she was given. [[Jared Hopworth|Jared]] left home shortly after.

He wandered for a period of time and found work as a contract killer. He met others who followed [[The Flesh]] and took some on as companions after altering their forms to match their, "Proper bodies". Some of the people he met told him about [[The Entities]] and [[Rituals]], and while some of his companions were swayed, [[Jared Hopworth|Jared]] already enjoyed the fear he could inspire in people and did not feel the world needed to change.

[[Jared Hopworth|Jared]] started receiving unsigned letters in 2016 that gave simple instructions to locate a person. He ignored the first few letters, but became curious after receiving several more. He followed one letter's instructions and found Regan Hasnain, a woman who had highly malleable bones. The letters continued to come and consistently led him to new victims, companions, and high quality bones, and eventually [[Jared Hopworth|Jared]] followed them without question.

In 2017, [[Jared Hopworth|Jared]] received a letter that directed him to the archives of The Magnus Institute to kill [[Jonathan Sims|John]]. He and some of his companions entered the archives through the pipes and came up through the floor. Their ambush caused [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] and [[Melanie King|Melanie]] to flee, leaving only [[Basira Hussain|Basira]]. [[Jared Hopworth|Jared]] noticed that [[Jonathan Sims|John]] was not present in the archives but continued the attack, intending to simply take the most desirable parts of the archival assistant's bodies, but [[Melanie King|Melanie]] returned with a knife and killed his companions. [[Jared Hopworth|Jared]] was unable to defend himself and grew frightened, fleeing into one of [[The Distortion|Helen]]'s doors and becoming trapped in her corridors.

At present, [[Jared Hopworth|Jared]] muses about the origin of the letters and wonders if they were sent by [[Jonathan Sims|John]] with the intention of trapping him in the corridors. He concludes that his theory isn't likely, as it seems like an unreasonable amount of work just for one rib.

# Post-Statement

After a brief exchange, [[Jared Hopworth|Jared]] removes two of [[Jonathan Sims|John]]'s ribs. [[Jared Hopworth|Jared]] incorporates the rib he received as payment into his own body and remarks that, while the bone is strange, it belongs to him now. [[Jared Hopworth|Jared]] goes to find an exit from the corridors and [[Jonathan Sims|John]] passes out.

[[Melanie King|Melanie]] and [[The Distortion|Helen]] find [[Jonathan Sims|John]] outside the corridors holding his newly-removed rib, unconscious. [[Melanie King|Melanie]] asks where [[Jared Hopworth|Jared]]'s door led to, and [[The Distortion|Helen]] happily replies that he came out some distance above a river. As [[Jonathan Sims|John]] regains consciousness he thanks [[The Distortion|Helen]] for her help, and [[The Distortion|Helen]] states that she supports what the Institute is doing. [[Melanie King|Melanie]] thinks [[Basira Hussain|Basira]] will be upset that [[Jared Hopworth|Jared]] is free, but [[Jonathan Sims|John]] assures her that [[Basira Hussain|Basira]] will consider [[Alice Daisy Tonner|Daisy]] escaping [[The Coffin]] more important.
