# Pre-Statement

[[Jonathan Sims]] is given the [[Catalogue of the Trapped Dead]] by [[Julia Montauk]] and [[Trevor Herbert]] in which he is instructed to read the last page. The pair leaves the room and [[Jonathan Sims|John]] starts to read the last entry, an account of the death of [[Gerard Keay]].

Once finished [[Gerard Keay|Gerard]] appears, summoned by [[Jonathan Sims|John]]. At first, [[Gerard Keay|Gerard]] is uninterested in talking to [[Jonathan Sims|John]]. He has been used repeatedly by [[Julia Montauk|Julia]] and [[Trevor Herbert|Trevor]] as a 'bloody monster manual.' [[Jonathan Sims|John]] introduces himself and [[Gerard Keay|Gerard]] asks when [[Gertrude Robinson|Gertrude]] died. He is pleased that her ending was not peaceful as she supposedly would not have wanted to go quietly. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] then asks [[Gerard Keay|Gerard]] to help him stop [[The Unknowing]] but [[Gerard Keay|Gerard]] initially refuses. He has become increasingly frustrated and depressed with his new existence as a book, as undeath is existentially painful, and has no interest in helping [[Jonathan Sims|John]].

[[Jonathan Sims|John]] asks him what it is he wants. [[Gerard Keay|Gerard]] bargains with [[Jonathan Sims|John]], saying that if he takes his page from the book and destroys it, thus permanently ending his 'life,' then he will give [[Jonathan Sims|John]] everything he knows about what [[Gertrude Robinson|Gertrude]] had been planning and about [[The Unknowing]]. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] agrees and tears out the page.

[[Gerard Keay|Gerard]] begins to recount everything he knows about [[The Unknowing]]. Apparently, whilst the [[Rituals|ritual]] could be delayed, there was no way to completely stop it as everything could be replaced. But he and [[Gertrude Robinson|Gertrude]] believed that, once the [[Rituals|ritual]] started, it might be vulnerable — it could be disrupted and thus stopped. Whilst [[Gerard Keay|Gerard]] does not know what it could be vulnerable to, he is able to tell [[Jonathan Sims|John]] about a storage unit where [[Gertrude Robinson|Gertrude]] has hidden something that she thought could do it.

The pair discusses [[Gertrude Robinson|Gertrude]] and the abilities both she and [[Jonathan Sims|John]] display from being [[The Archivist|Archivists]], though [[Gerard Keay|Gerard]] notes [[Gertrude Robinson|Gertrude]] seemed reluctant to use hers. She would also occasionally read statements aloud when she was "getting shaky", though she did not record them.

The conversation moves onto [[Gerard Keay|Gerard]]'s mother, [[Mary Keay]], and [[Gerard Keay|Gerard]] talks about how she was driven, wanting to start a mystic dynasty, a family to worship the occult. He mentions how the [[Lukas Family]] is an example of one such dynasty, a family which worships [[The Lonely]], but not [[Fairchild Family|the Fairchilds]] who just share a pseudonym but are not blood related.

As the conversation focuses more on [[Gerard Keay|Gerard]] and his relationship with his mother, [[Jonathan Sims|John]] asks if he would be willing to give a statement.

# Statement

[[Mary Keay]] believed she had been cheated out of some grand mystical legacy from her Von Closen relatives, and she aspired to use the power of [[The Entities]] without being consumed by any of them. She greatly admired [[Jurgen Leitner]], and put a lot of effort into tracking down books from his collection.

[[Eric Delano|Gerard's father]] had worked in the Archives of [[The Magnus Institute]], quitting shortly after [[Gerard Keay|Gerard]] was born. However, [[Mary Keay|Mary]] killed him in his sleep soon after and presumably bookbound him, although when [[Gerard Keay|Gerard]] eventually checked the book his page was not in it. All through his childhood, [[Gerard Keay|Gerard]] was tutored in occult secrets that even adults would have struggled with, and they travelled extensively, meeting "things" that terrified [[Gerard Keay|Gerard]] but greeted [[Mary Keay|Mary]] with affection.

As a youth, [[Gerard Keay|Gerard]] tried to run away, but he found mundane life terribly ignorant. Instead he resigned himself to being part of his mother's obsession. However, as he got older, he came to realize how misguided her attempts to control the Entities were, and their relationship deteriorated badly. He could not, however, bring himself to cut her off entirely.

[[Gerard Keay|Gerard]] came up to the bookshop one day to find [[Mary Keay|Mary]] had partially skinned herself as part of a ritual to take full control of the [[[Catalogue of the Trapped Dead]]. She begged him to finish it, as she was about to faint from blood loss, but [[Gerard Keay|Gerard]] panicked and ran out of the shop. Due to the blood on his clothes and the extent of [[Mary Keay|Mary]]'s wounds, [[Gerard Keay|Gerard]] was charged with [[Mary Keay|Mary]]'s murder. However, even the incomplete ritual allowed [[Mary Keay|Mary]] to manifest herself physically again, and she somehow tampered with the evidence before [[Gerard Keay|Gerard]] could be brought to trial, and there was not enough evidence for a second trial as several eyewitnesses had withdrawn their testimony.

For the next five years, [[Gerard Keay|Gerard]] was haunted by his mother, whom he describes as "damaged" by the botched ritual, powerful but erratic. She sometimes faded out of existence, during which time he occupied himself destroying [[List of Books|Leitners]] and trying to evade her power. Eventually, [[Gertrude Robinson]] offered to destroy the book and thus set [[Gerard Keay|Gerard]] free. He did not realize that she had learned how to make pages out of people before she did so.

# Statement Ends

[[Gerard Keay|Gerard]] explains that [[Gertrude Robinson|Gertrude]] was trying to stop not just [[The Unknowing]], but all the various [[Rituals]] that would allow one of [[The Entities]] to manifest in the world. He and [[Jonathan Sims|John]] discuss [[The Entities]], including [[Robert Smirke's]] list of fourteen, and why anyone would help such beings transform the world. [[Gerard Keay|Gerard]] mentions tracking [[Jurgen Leitner]] and meeting "a pathetic old man" who could not possibly have been him, and [[Jonathan Sims|John]] does not correct him.

In the end, [[Gerard Keay|Gerard]] asks [[Jonathan Sims|John]] to call him, "Gerry." [[Jonathan Sims|John]] then dismisses him. He thanks [[Trevor Herbert|Trevor]] and [[Julia Montauk|Julia]] for their help, telling them his next step is returning to London, and they thank him in return for "bringing them Max Mustermann's head," which they will have fun with.
