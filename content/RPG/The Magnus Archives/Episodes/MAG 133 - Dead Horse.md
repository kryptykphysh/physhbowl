# Statement

[[Percy Fawcett]] says that it is imperative that nobody tells anybody that he is alive. He simply wants to live the rest of life in obscurity and anonymity.

He suggests that the [[The Magnus Institute|Institute]] might have heard reports of his disappearance or death, with theories of him being attacked by Kalapolos tribesmen or lacking adequate supplies, but he only wishes that it was that mundane. He had been searching for the Lost City of Z. He had dreamed of it for decades since reading about it.

[[Percy Fawcett|Percy]]'s first expedition to find the city was alone, save for a few indigenous guides. He thought himself prepared, but the realities of the jungle were more than he could have foreseen. When it finally ended, he named the spot, the furthest they could reach into the jungle, Dead Horse Camp, as it was here when his horse finally died. He retreated in defeat, resolving to return to Dead Horse Camp when he was finally ready.

Five years later, in 1925, [[Percy Fawcett|Percy]] believed he would find the Lost City of Z. He was more prepared and crucially, not alone. Accompanying [[Percy Fawcett|Percy]] was his son, Jack. Jack requested that his friend, Raleigh Rimmell, was included, and [[Percy Fawcett|Percy]] accepted. [[Percy Fawcett|Percy]] never liked the look of Raleigh but had never had any reason to doubt his son's judgement. Raleigh suggested that himself, Jack, and [[Percy Fawcett|Percy]] continued on from Dead Horse Camp alone, abandoning the animals and locals who agreed to act as guides that travelled with them. Raleigh told his group that if they were going to find what they were looking for, they would have to travel quick. [[Percy Fawcett|Percy]] argued that a more methodical search would be better, but after Raleigh's persistence, he relented.

After travelling for a while, the group found a half-buried stone covered in hieroglyphics, like those described by a manuscript that spoke of the Lost City of Z. Raleigh lowered his face down to the stone and then tasted the air around it before taking off into the jungle at a dead run, without a word. Jack and [[Percy Fawcett|Percy]] quickly followed behind him. They eventually caught up to him and [[Percy Fawcett|Percy]] asked what in God's name Raleigh thought he was doing. Raleigh responded, saying that they were close and he would not let it get away again and that he could smell it. [[Percy Fawcett|Percy]] told him that they were looking for a lost city, that it could not run away, but Raleigh just repeated himself, saying he could smell it.

[[Percy Fawcett|Percy]] kept a journal, and after this point, his memories become fragmented and his journal entries become more sporadic. The dates no longer made any sense. At some point, he realised that there was no longer any animals around them and that the jungle had become strangely quiet, but he does not know if this was before or after he found a pile of dead birds in Raleigh's tent. Jack had taken over navigation at this point, so [[Percy Fawcett|Percy]] had little idea where they were going. There were other animals, but [[Percy Fawcett|Percy]] could not see any more than a glimpse of them. They were not normal animals, as they were far too sharp for that, and they were definitely following the group.

They met a second expedition, who donned cold-weather outfits despite the humid heat of the jungle, and [[Percy Fawcett|Percy]] seemed to be the only one who was surprised. The leader of the expedition was [[John Franklin]], a famed explorer who trapped in ice and lost in northern Canada, but the expedition had taken place almost 100 years ago. They were briefly a group of fourteen but the animals that were following them attacked. It was only through [[Percy Fawcett|Percy]]'s paranoia that led to him keeping his revolver close enough to him to survive the attack. There was six of them left afterward. [[Percy Fawcett|Percy]] felt like the safest option was to feign the same obsession that had gripped Raleigh and Jack. Although they seemed to have a better grasp of the situation, they did not question [[Percy Fawcett|Percy]] too closely if he seemed to share the fervor.

The group ran into Eduard von Toll a few days later, an explorer whose ship disappeared during a search for [[Sannikov Land]]. He and his crew were pinning [[Vampires]] to trees with long iron spikes, as well as two ordinary people, an act which Raleigh approves of. They continued on their way with the group, but supplies had run out days ago. One or two of the crew members began to falter or fall from exhaustion or hunger, but they were simply left behind. The worst part was when Jack talked to [[Percy Fawcett|Percy]] about what they would see when they finally found "it", or caught "it", or killed "it". [[Percy Fawcett|Percy]]'s body finally gave out, and he fell and was left behind. He awoke back at Dead Horse Camp. Some Kalabolos had found him and taken pity on him. After leaving the jungle, he had made sure that no one had known he was alive and that he intended to keep it that way.

[[Percy Fawcett|Percy]] knew that whatever Raleigh sought in the jungle, he would never find it, that whatever those people pursued did not exist. The sooner the world forgets them, the better, but [[Percy Fawcett|Percy]] wished he did not have to lose his son to learn that lesson.

# Post-Statement

This statement is the closest [[Jonathan Sims|John]] has gotten to finding a [[Rituals|ritual]] of [[The Hunt]]. [[Alice Daisy Tonner|Daisy]] thinks it could have been one. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] does not understand why the ritual did not work, even without any outside interference, but [[Alice Daisy Tonner|Daisy]] does not think it was about it working. She explains that during a case, her least favourite part was arresting the culprit because it meant that the hunt was over. She does not think that [[The Hunt]] would let its ritual end. A part of [[Gertrude Robinson|Gertrude's]] notes references something called the [[Everchase]].

[[Basira Hussain|Basira]] comes in, asking [[Alice Daisy Tonner|Daisy]] why she was not doing her exercises, and [[Alice Daisy Tonner|Daisy]] explains that she, [[Basira Hussain|Basira]], was out. [[Basira Hussain|Basira]] asks her to leave, and after she does, [[Basira Hussain|Basira]] asks [[Jonathan Sims|John]] if that was really [[Alice Daisy Tonner|Daisy]]. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] says that it is, but [[Basira Hussain|Basira]] says that [[Alice Daisy Tonner|Daisy]] just seems lost. [[Basira Hussain|Basira]] acknowledges that they have all changed, but she did not realise [[Alice Daisy Tonner|Daisy]] would change into someone who cannot look after herself, even without the muscle atrophy. [[Basira Hussain|Basira]] is happy to have [[Alice Daisy Tonner|Daisy]] back, but she is dead weight right now, and [[Basira Hussain|Basira]] needs to travel light.
