# Statement

[[Lawrence Moore]] used to have a cousin named Carl who lived in Peterborough. [[Lawrence Moore|Lawrence]] and his parents would go over to see Carl and his family, but they would rarely return visits to the Moore household. As they grew up they grew apart and did not keep in touch even when they accidentally met in Covent Garden in London.

When [[Lawrence Moore|Lawrence]] went to his brother's wedding, he met [[NotThem|a man who was not Carl]]. Everyone else said this man was Carl, including his humourless father. [[Lawrence Moore|Lawrence]] left and drove back to London, wondering if he was having some sort of episode after his recent divorce.

Later, he went into his attic and found countless pictures of this [[NotThem|NotCarl]] that matched the man he saw at the wedding. The only exception were two Polaroid photos which showed the original Carl.

There was a knock at his door. [[NotThem|NotCarl]] stood on his doorstep and said that he was sad to see [[Lawrence Moore|Lawrence]] leave so abruptly. The two of them sat, [[NotThem|NotCarl]] almost too still with a mad glint in his eye. [[Lawrence Moore|Lawrence]] tried to focus on him, but had difficulty, at one time seeing his neck longer than normal. They sat in silence for a long time before [[NotThem|NotCarl]] stood up, saying they should do this again soon, and left. [[Lawrence Moore|Lawrence]] wept.

Later that evening, there was another knock on the door. He found a black, grey haired man with a crisp white shirt and a thin necktie. He was thin and short and had an imposing presence. The man asked if [[Lawrence Moore|Lawrence]] knew the person who came to his house, and when [[Lawrence Moore|Lawrence]] said no, the man became very excited. 

He asked [[Lawrence Moore|Lawrence]] to get any photos that had not changed, and he brought them down from the loft. By this time the man was sitting in the place where [[NotThem|NotCarl]] had been sitting, and introduced himself as [[Adelard Dekker]], saying he was an exorcist of sorts. [[Adelard Dekker|Adelard]] fished out the photos that had not changed and asked [[Lawrence Moore|Lawrence]] to follow him. He did, and they brought a large wooden box into [[Lawrence Moore|Lawrence]]'s house. When it was inside, [[Lawrence Moore|Lawrence]] tried to look inside the box, but [[Adelard Dekker|Adelard]] slammed it closed and said it was not for him. The grey haired man told [[Lawrence Moore|Lawrence]] to go to his room and stay there. He said that his cousin was dead, and that he should stay out of the rest of the house lest he suffer the same fate.

[[Lawrence Moore|Lawrence]] slept fitfully and stayed in his room until the afternoon of the next day. There was a knock at the door at the same time as [[NotThem|NotCarl]] had come the day before, and the air grew close and heavy. After a tense hour, there was an intense, rage-filled scream. [[Lawrence Moore|Lawrence]] bolted out of the bedroom towards the front door, glancing into the living room as he fled. In it he saw [[Adelard Dekker|Adelard]] mouthing wordlessly in front of [[Web Table|a table with an intricate pattern on it]]. [[NotThem|NotCarl]] stood, limbs stretched and distorted, with spider webs attaching the creature to the table. [[Lawrence Moore|Lawrence]] fled.

When he returned home the next day, he found a [[Breekon & Hope]] van outside and watched the two burly men take the table away. Carl was reported missing by his parents but was never found.

# Post-Statement

[[Jonathan Sims|John]] found this tape in the case marked #9910602, where [[Gertrude Robinson|Gertrude's]] previous tape indicated [[Adelard Dekker|Adelard]]'s file would be. He deduces that [[Not-Sasha]] must be tied to the table. He found the missing tapes with [[Sasha James|Sasha's]] voice on them and plays them.

[[Jonathan Sims|John]] tells [[Timothy Stoker|Tim]] and [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] that they should take the next few days off as he is feeling ill. He starts to make his subtle goodbyes. [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] is concerned, but [[Timothy Stoker|Tim]] forces [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] to make a swift exit and is flippant towards [[Jonathan Sims|John]].

Later, [[Jonathan Sims|John]] goes to artefact storage with an axe he has bought. He destroys the table to find that it is hollow and full of cobwebs and dust. [[The Distortion|Michael]] appears with a laugh, telling [[Jonathan Sims|John]] that what he did was very stupid, and that he won't be able to escape the [[NotThem]]. It explains that the table was binding [[Not-Sasha]] effectively, and that [[Jonathan Sims|John]] will probably not survive her attack despite the protections around him. [[Not-Sasha]] calls after [[Jonathan Sims|John]] in a strange, distorted voice as [[The Distortion|Michael]] provides him with a door out.
