# Statement

[[Darren Harlow]] was a cleaner for the University of Surrey. He got on well with the scientists at the Psychology Department so he knew about most of the ongoing experiments. [[Dr. Elizabeth Bates]] explained about her post-graduate's latest study, which was remarkably similar to ESP research: if the feelings of the people in one room affect the experience of someone else in a nearby room.

In the experiment, they would place [[Annabelle Cane]] in a room, and an arachnophobe, referred to as a projector, in the next room, watching videos of spiders. The arachnophobe were to direct their feelings through the one-way mirror to [[Annabelle Cane|Annabelle]].

[[Darren Harlow|Darren]] does not know exactly what happened during the tests, but other scientist said that Annabelle was experiencing heightened stress and unease. As the trials continued, [[Darren Harlow|Darren]] ended up cleaning more cobwebs than normal, although he only saw glimpses of the spiders.

[[Annabelle Cane|Annabelle]] started to have nightmares about spiders, although she was not informed of the fear of those on the other side of the mirror. The post-grads started adding more arachnophobes to the group, and although this improved the results initially, eventually the results changed drastically, and were no longer showing fear.

[[Darren Harlow|Darren]] bumped into [[Annabelle Cane|Annabelle]] one afternoon. She walked strangely as though her clothes did not fit, her knees bent in strange ways and she scuttled her fingers along the wall.

A few days later, the post-grads tried with a group of 19 projectors. When [[Darren Harlow|Darren]] came to clean at 9pm they were still there. The cleaner went to see what was happening. He found them in two circles, one inside the other, with interlocking arms in a web-like structure. They slowly rotated. [[Mark Voight|Mark]], one of the post-grads, stood in the corner as though in a trance. 

[[Annabelle Cane|Annabelle]] was in the room opposite, staring at them, her body hunched and contorted, looking for a moment like she had more than two eyes. As [[Darren Harlow|Darren]] entered, all the projectors dropped their arms and turned to him. Then they turned to [[Annabelle Cane|Annabelle]], and smashed their heads against the one-way mirrors to allow [[Annabelle Cane|Annabelle]] to crawl out the room. When she was a foot away from [[Darren Harlow|Darren]], she stood over him, much taller than she used to be. When she looked into his eyes, he felt spiders crawling in his head and his hands crept up his body and started to strangle himself.

As [[Darren Harlow|Darren]] started to suffocate, [[Mark Voight|Mark]] charged into [[Annabelle Cane|Annabelle]], causing her head to crash into the wall. As she hit her head, the projectors and [[Darren Harlow|Darren]]'s hands fell, but he looked on in horror as [[Annabelle Cane|Annabelle]] started to stand, a hole in her head showing a skull full of webs. [[Darren Harlow|Darren]] fled. He never saw any of them again.

The university contacted [[Darren Harlow|Darren]] to say that, if he valued his job, he was not in the building, which [[Darren Harlow|Darren]] conveyed to the police. The official line was that [[Annabelle Cane|Annabelle]] had suffered a psychotic break, broken the window, beaten [[Mark Voight|Mark]] and fled. [[Mark Voight|Mark]] and [[Dr. Elizabeth Bates|Elizabeth]] have not returned to the University and [[Annabelle Cane|Annabelle]] seems to be on the run still.

# Post-Statement

[[Darren Harlow|Darren]]'s statement was corroborated by various sources. [[Mark Voight]]'s testimony was incomprehensible and [[Dr. Elizabeth Bates|Elizabeth Bates]] refused to give a statement. [[Mark Voight|Mark]] was able to confirm [[Darren Harlow|Darren]]'s presence, however.

[[Annabelle Cane|Annabelle]] was never found, and each of the projectors have disappeared.
