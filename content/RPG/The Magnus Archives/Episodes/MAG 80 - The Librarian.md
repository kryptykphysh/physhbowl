# Synopsis

Following the apparent banishment of [[Not-Sasha]] at the end of the previous episode, [[Jonathan Sims]] is left with his apparent saviour. He has revealed himself to be [[Jurgen Leitner]] as well as the mysterious figure who had been previously seen living in the tunnels and stealing from the Archives.

He tells [[Jonathan Sims|John]] that he trapped [[Not-Sasha]] using 'an unexpurgated copy' of Ruskin's _[[The Seven Lamps of Architecture]]_. He claims to be able to use it to manipulate [[Robert Smirke]]'s architecture, allowing him to capture [[Not-Sasha]] as well as reorient the tunnels to hide from [[Jonathan Sims|John]]. He also reveals that he has been using his copy of _[[A Disappearance]]_ to hide from [[Jonathan Sims|John]]'s 'master'([[Elias Bouchard|Elias]]).

[[Jurgen Leitner|Leitner]] explains that he has been in hiding since the destruction of his library in 1994, on the run from creatures and people who are hunting for him.

He then explains why he started his library. As the scion of a wealthy manufacturing family, [[Jurgen Leitner|Leitner]] lived a youth without struggle or need, but he always felt a calling to make something of himself. His one talent was finding and purchasing rare and valuable objects. Soon enough, he encountered his first of the books, _[[The Stalwart Hunters' Almanac]],_ following the violent death of its previous owner. Shortly afterwards, he hired his first assistant, [[Albert Stross]], who barely lasted a fortnight.

[[Jurgen Leitner|Leitner]]'s collection of these books continued to expand and he found that finding them was surprisingly easy. When he began encountering problems with storing certain books in close proximity to one another, he looked to the architecture of [[Robert Smirke]] for a solution and commissioned a house to be built upon his principles to allow for safe storage. At its height, his library held 978 books of varying types in the building's complex and bizarre architecture.

A few years later his library was assaulted. It began with strange people visiting and asking to see his collections. Soon after, the library was attacked by various entities, forcing [[Jurgen Leitner|Leitner]] to flee and resulting in the library being burned to the ground. Ever since, he has been on the run.

[[Jurgen Leitner|Leitner]] goes on to explain his understanding of [[The Entities]] behind the books, telling [[Jonathan Sims|John]] how he believes the books (and creatures such as [[The Distortion|Michael]] and the [[NotThem]]) to be manifestations of vast, powerful beings that reside outside our normal perception. He also tells [[Jonathan Sims|John]] that he believes [[[[Elias Bouchard|Elias]] Bouchard|[[Elias Bouchard|Elias]]]] killed [[Gertrude Robinson|Gertrude]], who he had been working with in the hopes of destroying the Archives, and that [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]] took the files related to their plans. [[Jurgen Leitner|Leitner]] reveals that the [[The Magnus Institute|Institute]] is a place of power for [[The Entities|the entity]] known as '[[The Eye|Beholding]]', or '[[The Eye]]' as named by [[Jonathan Sims|John]], and that [[Jonathan Sims|John]] belongs to it as well.

[[Jonathan Sims|John]], overwhelmed, leaves to have a cigarette, at which point [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]] enters the room, surprised and pleased to discover who [[Gertrude Robinson|Gertrude]] had been working with. [[Jurgen Leitner|Leitner]] tries to get the files from [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]], which he claims hold everything [[Gertrude Robinson|Gertrude]] found on stopping [[The Stranger]] and [[The Unknowing]]. [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]], however, brutally murders [[Jurgen Leitner|Leitner]] with a pipe and leaves. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] returns, but flees upon seeing [[Jurgen Leitner|Leitner]]'s body. [[Timothy Stoker|Tim]] and [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]], who have managed to escape [[The Distortion|Michael's]] corridors, discover the body, and suspect [[Jonathan Sims|John]] as the murderer.

# Continuity

- When [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] and [[Timothy Stoker|Tim]] emerge from [[The Distortion|Michael]]'s labyrinth, [[Timothy Stoker|Tim]] asks if there's, "Any sign of the woman," and [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] insists that they, "Should have helped her." They may be referring to [[Helen Richardson]], who was kidnapped by [[The Distortion|Michael]] in [[MAG 47 - The New Door]].
- [[Jurgen Leitner]] was previously mentioned in [[MAG 4 - Page Turner]], [[MAG 17 - The Boneturner's Tale]], [[MAG 35 - Old Passages]], [[MAG 46 - Literary Heights]], and [[MAG 62 - First Edition]].
- [[Robert Smirke]] was previously mentioned in [[MAG 26 - A Distortion]], [[MAG 35 - Old Passages]], [[MAG 41 - Too Deep]], [[MAG 50 - Foundations]], and [[MAG 63 - The End of the Tunnel]].
- [[Gerard Keay]] was previously mentioned in [[MAG 4 - Page Turner]], [[MAG 12 - First Aid]], [[MAG 35 - Old Passages]], and [[MAG 48 - Lost in the Crowd]].
- **Related Entity:** Numerous, [[Jurgen Leitner|Leitner]]'s account of the attack on his library features various familiar sounding entities. The fact that they all serve various different [[The Entities|Entities]], some diametrically opposed, is a testament to how much they hated [[Jurgen Leitner|Leitner]]'s work.
    - 'Something with too many teeth and limbs like knives': A creature of [[The Hunt]] similar to the "werewolf" in [[MAG 31 - First Hunt]]. It was later stated to be a separate but closely related creature in the Season 2 Q&A.
    - 'A cavernous maw opening beneath [[an assistant]]': An unclear manifestation. Thematically, it could be tied to [[The Buried]], but by description it could also be [[The Flesh]], as with its manifestation in [[MAG 49 - The Butcher’s Window]].
    - 'A door that shouldn't have been there': A manifestation of [[The Spiral]], likely [[The Distortion]] due to the similarity of its appearances in [[MAG 47 - The New Door]] and [[MAG 78 - Distant Cousin]].
    - 'A great hand [[that]] reached down through the roof and plucked away [[an assistant]]': Possibly a creature of [[The Vast]], such as that seen in [[MAG 51 - High Pressure]].
    - 'A great, pulsating pile of meat': A creature or manifestation of [[The Flesh]], similar to and possibly the same as that in [[MAG 18 - The Man Upstairs]].
    - 'Rooms that had fallen into darkness': Manifestations of [[The Dark]] akin to those seen in [[MAG 9 - A Father's Love]], [[MAG 52 - Exceptional Risk]], and [[MAG 73 - Police Lights]]. By their very nature, it is impossible to know whether anything lurked within them.
    - 'Rooms that [[...]] burned with a fire that seemed to leave the books untouched': Manifestations of [[The Desolation]].
- 'An angry Goth', presumed by [[Jonathan Sims|John]] to be [[Gerard Keay]], apparently once almost beat [[Jurgen Leitner|Leitner]] to death. The incident is later mentioned by [[Gerard Keay|Gerard]] himself in [[MAG 111 - Family Business]].
- This statement seems to suggest that [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]] and [[Jurgen Leitner|Leitner]] are both seeking to fight [[The Stranger]], despite their animosity towards each other.
- [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]] likely continued to hide [[Gertrude Robinson|Gertrude]]’s notes in order to ensure [[Jonathan Sims|John]] would be present at [[The Unknowing]], which he knew would fail and which was essential to his own ritual, as revealed in [[MAG 160 - The Eye Opens]].
- There is a timeline discrepancy in this episode: [[Jurgen Leitner|Leitner]] claims to have first met [[Gertrude Robinson|Gertrude]] around 2011 and during their partnership he had her acquire certain books including _[[The Key of Solomon]]_, however according to [[MAG 4 - Page Turner]] [[Gertrude Robinson|Gertrude]] purchased _[[The Key of Solomon]]_ in 2007. This may be explained by [[Jurgen Leitner|Leitner]] simply misremembering how long he has known [[Gertrude Robinson|Gertrude]] for.
