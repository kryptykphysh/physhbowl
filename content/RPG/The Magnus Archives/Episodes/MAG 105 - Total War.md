# Pre-Statement

[[Jonathan Sims]] visits the [[Pu Songling Research Centre]] in Beijing, as [[Gertrude Robinson]] had gone there in 2014 before her [[MAG 103 - Cruelty Free|trip to New Zealand]]. He speaks with the head librarian there, [[Zhang Xiaoling]], and does not seem to notice when she occasionally switches into Mandarin. She gives [[Jonathan Sims|John]] a statement that she believes is the one [[Gertrude Robinson|Gertrude]] was looking at, as it is the only one written in English from the period of the Taiping Rebellion.

# Statement

Second Lieutenant [[Charles Fleming]] served on the East India Company ship _[[Nemesis]]_ during the First Opium War. The captain and crew committed many atrocities against the Chinese during that time, including drowning prisoners out of pure cruelty. [[Charles Fleming|Fleming]] notes that he began to see the faces of the dead following him in his reflection, and hear them singing at night when he tried to sleep.

The _[[Nemesis]]_ eventually sank into the Canton River, and [[Charles Fleming|Fleming]] was the only survivor. When he escaped the ship, he saw it sink into dark water far deeper than the river should have been, and the water was full of corpses.

[[Charles Fleming|Fleming]] remained in China instead of trying to return to England, and became an opium addict. When the Taiping Rebellion began, he was trapped in Anqing and resorted to eating human flesh to survive the siege. He was eventually able to escape by dint of his British uniform, while the rest of the city's population was put to death by the conquering army.

[[Charles Fleming|Fleming]] resorted to wandering China, which was by then devastated by the Taiping Rebellion. He felt the ghosts of all those he had ever killed following him. At one point, he was captured by some thieves who hoped to ransom a foreign officer. Before the thieves could agree on which side would pay the better ransom, the ghosts tore them to pieces, but left [[Charles Fleming|Fleming]] alive.

During his travels, he passed Hangzhou, where the entire population had flung themselves into a lake rather than wait to be executed by the occupying army. [[Charles Fleming|Fleming]] claims he was able to walk three hundred yards from the shore on the backs of the tightly-packed bodies.

Eventually, [[Charles Fleming|Fleming]] found a shrine where the names of the ghosts who followed him were engraved on the walls. He stopped to write out his statement, and left it there, hoping that when he left the shrine the ghosts would finally kill him.

# Post-Statement

[[Jonathan Sims|John]] is shaken by the brutality of the statement, and hopes out loud that the most horrifying elements were just supernatural hallucinations. Still, [[Charles Fleming|Fleming]]'s account does not seem related to [[The Stranger]] or [[Circus of the Other|The Circus]], and he almost gives up the [[Pu Songling Research Centre]] as a dead end before he notices that [[Zhang Xiaoling|Xiaoling]] has given him the wrong file.

Upon checking the records again, [[Zhang Xiaoling|Xiaoling]] confirms that [[Gertrude Robinson|Gertrude]] had read [[Charles Fleming|Fleming]]'s statement in 2004. The two files [[Gertrude Robinson|Gertrude]] accessed in 2014 had been forwarded to an American address and never returned to Beijing. She gives [[Jonathan Sims|John]] the address, and he thanks her profusely.

# Notes

In the real world, the _[[Nemesis]]_ survived the Opium Wars and was ultimately sold for scrap in Kolkata in 1855.
