# Pre-Statement

[[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] asks him not to look inside his head; he does not want to keep secrets from [[Jonathan Sims|John]] but he thinks he should have the option at least. He finds it weird that [[Jonathan Sims|John]] can just Know everything he thinks and feels, especially when [[Jonathan Sims|John]] is not the most emotionally open person. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] protests this description so [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] asks him to describe his current emotional state. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] concedes the point after expressing that he is “Very sad" about everything that is going on. He agrees not to Know anything about [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] unless he is in physical danger.

[[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] tells him that [[Annabelle Crane|Annabelle]] did not say what she wanted to help them with. She just asked where they were going, but she already seemed to have an idea of the answer. [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] does not think she was influencing his mind but it is hard to know for sure. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] still cannot see where [[Annabelle Crane|Annabelle]] is and he is frustrated with his powers; he feels like he has the power to drink the whole ocean but he has to do it through a straw. [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] worries about him and the way he is doing statements now.

It will be a while before they will reach the next [[Domains|domain]] so they take a break at [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]]'s behest. He bemoans their fate and figures that [[Gertrude Robinson|Gertrude]] would have been better suited to dealing with these hellscapes, but [[Jonathan Sims|John]] disagrees. [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] asks if he Knows this and [[Jonathan Sims|John]] pauses for a moment before he begins to speak of [[Gertrude Robinson|Gertrude]] Robinson.

# Statement

In her own way, [[Gertrude Robinson|Gertrude]] was close to many people, but she reeked of [[The Lonely]]. When she first joined the Archives, she replaced previous head archivist Angus Stacey after he was killed by a creature of masks and smiles that [[Gertrude Robinson|Gertrude]] thought of as The Grinning Wheel. She used fire to kill it and it was one of the first things to fall at her hands. She knew some of the truth when she first took the position, enough to be dangerous and cautious. This kept her alive for the first few years.

[[Angus Stacey]] had been a disappointment to [[Jonah Magnus]], then wearing the body of Director [[Richard Mendelson]]. [[Angus Stacey|Angus]] had been too keen to learn, and had grand plans to revise [[Robert Smirke|Smirke's]] list of [[The Entities|Fourteen]]. Ultimately, he burned through his resources, his luck, and all but one of his assistants: [[Fiona Law]].

[[Fiona Law|Fiona]] possessed both curiosity and cowardice, and the single thing that had allowed her to survive what had killed braver people was that she would faint when she was terrfied. Most dark things get no real satisfication from killing someone not awake enough to know what is happening. Having survived Angus Stacey's tenure, [[Fiona Law|Fiona]] had a chance to leave her job. However, she stayed because her burning curiosity had not been sated.

Alongside [[Fiona Law|Fiona]], [[Gertrude Robinson|Gertrude]] added two new assistants: [[Eric Delano]] and [[Emma Harvey]]. [[Emma Harvey|Emma]] in particular was [[Gertrude Robinson|Gertrude]]'s confidant, she trusted her and she was the only member of the Institute who knew of the bond between [[Gertrude Robinson|Gertrude]] and [[Agnes Montague]]. Like [[Fiona Law|Fiona]], [[Emma Harvey|Emma]] was plagued by curiosity but unlike her, [[Emma Harvey|Emma]] recognised the danger in such an inclination and after a few years she had settled on a question: Why was [[Fiona Law|Fiona]] not dead yet?

She began experimenting. She would volunteer herself and [[Fiona Law|Fiona]] to investigate statements with a possibility for danger, and would intentionally expose [[Fiona Law|Fiona]] to danger while observing from a safe distance, taking notes before retrieving the often-unconscious assistant. [[Emma Harvey|Emma]] watched her stumbled through a maze of whispering grubs, timed the intervals at which [[Fiona Law|Fiona]] emerged from a hungry fog, and recorded her barely escaping the [[Sandman]]([[MAG 98 - Lights Out]]) who came to take her eyes. [[Fiona Law|Fiona]] never suspected a thing and [[Gertrude Robinson|Gertrude]] was preoccupied with her own work, trusting [[Emma Harvey|Emma]] to manage things. This went on for decades until they encountered a [[The Coffin|coffin]] that could not be escaped by fainting. When [[Emma Harvey|Emma]] came to tell [[Gertrude Robinson|Gertrude]] what had happened, she found cobwebs in her hair. She would wash new cobwebs away every morning for the rest of her life. [[Gertrude Robinson|Gertrude]] mourned the first of many losses, and did not suspect the truth.

[[Emma Harvey|Emma]] never touched [[Eric Delano|Eric]] as she was not eager to cross [[Mary Keay]], and [[Fiona Law|Fiona]] was eventually replaced by [[Michael Shelley]], who was far too young for the job. He was innocent and naive, and [[Emma Harvey|Emma]] decided to test him in a different way, by seeing how long she could keep him in the dark about the truth regarding the paranormal; the answer turned out to be his entire life.

This time [[Gertrude Robinson|Gertrude]] had some idea about what was going on but she was also preoccupied with her own escalating conflicts and recognised the potential in a truly ignorant assistant. Eventually, [[Eric Delano|Eric]] disappeared and [[Gertrude Robinson|Gertrude]] scoured the dark corners of London for him. She never found him and she took [[Mary Keay|Mary]] at her word when she said she had not seen him.

[[Emma Harvey|Emma]] knew what had happened, but had no interest in sharing, and [[Eric Delano|Eric]] was eventually replaced by [[Sarah Carpenter]]. She was not as young as [[Michael Shelley|Michael]] but there was a fire to [[Sarah Carpenter|Sarah]] that reignited [[Emma Harvey|Emma]]'s curiosity. She would find out exactly what it would take to break this brave new assistant. [[Gertrude Robinson|Gertrude]] now paid little attention to her assistants as the genuine encounters grew in frequency and the season of rituals drew close.

[[Emma Harvey|Emma]] began to test [[Sarah Carpenter|Sarah]], sending her into a cave of deepest darkness, asking her to chart the stars with a strange book of astronomy, even convincing her to stay inside [[MAG 27 - A Sturdy Lock|a house haunted by a hungry door]]. But throughout, [[Sarah Carpenter|Sarah]] remained unbroken and [[Gertrude Robinson|Gertrude]] could not see what was happening. [[The Web|The Spider]] smoothed things over and fed her a steady string of plans to foil and rituals to derail.

While [[Gertrude Robinson|Gertrude]] and [[Michael Shelley|Michael]] were away on their trip to [[Sannikov Land]], [[Emma Harvey|Emma]] received a statement about a man who, "Burned on the inside". She and [[Sarah Carpenter|Sarah]] investigated and found him standing in a smoking ruin. He was bald and dressed in dreary office clothes, seemingly unfit and unremarkable save for his surroundings. [[Emma Harvey|Emma]] lingered behind her companion and as [[Sarah Carpenter|Sarah]] approached the man, he split open like a blooming flower bud to reveal a terrifying heat inside. [[Emma Harvey|Emma]] ran and before [[Sarah Carpenter|Sarah]] could think to scream, the man enveloped her, closing tight until she was nothing but ash.

After sacrificing [[Michael Shelley|Michael]] to stop [[The Spiral]]'s ritual, [[Gertrude Robinson|Gertrude]] returned to [[Emma Harvey|Emma]]'s lies and finally started to suspect the truth. [[Emma Harvey|Emma]]'s actions inspired an ice-cold rage in her and so she went to [[Agnes Montague]], the one person she was certain she could trust on the matter.

They only ever met once in their lives and neither of them smiled. Their discussion was brief and tinged with melancholy as [[Agnes Montague|Agnes]] confirmed what [[Gertrude Robinson|Gertrude]] already knew. It was easy enough to convince [[Jonah Magnus|Jonah]], now in the body of [[Elias Bouchard]], to allow her to burn out the infection that was [[Emma Harvey]].

And even as her apartment burned around her, [[Emma Harvey|Emma]] never suspected [[Gertrude Robinson|Gertrude]]. When the flames took her, all she willed was not to give it the satisfaction of being afraid.

[[Gertrude Robinson|Gertrude]] never hired another assistant. She worked with those she deemed useful, [[Jurgen Leitner|Leitner]], [[Adelard Dekker|Dekker]], [[Gerard Keay|Keay]], [[Mikaele Salesa|Salesa]], but she never again allowed herself to trust.

# Post-Statement

[[Jonathan Sims|John]] is flustered and apologetic as he had not intended to do a statement. For his part, [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] could not interrupt or not listen.

[[Jonathan Sims|John]] thinks that [[Gertrude Robinson|Gertrude]] would have been lost in the apocalypse without a reason. She needed the purpose of her mission and without it, she would maybe have dedicated herself to a doomed quest or, more likely, resigned herself to ruling her domain.

[[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] does not know what he means by her [[Domains|domain]] and [[Jonathan Sims|John]] explains that they all have one in the new world. It is the place that feeds them and they have been travelling toward [[Jonathan Sims|John]]'s. [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] asks about his own [[Domains|domain]] but quickly changes his mind and asks [[Jonathan Sims|John]] not to tell him or Know about it. He does not want to know.

He coyly asks about the fact that [[Gertrude Robinson|Gertrude]] would not have been able to go on without a reason and [[Jonathan Sims|John]] admits that, yes, [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] is his reason.

Before they press on, [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] also brings up the fact that [[Fiona Law|Fiona]] was released when [[Angus Stacey|Angus]] died. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] confirms that his death would similarly have freed his assistants, but he had not known this before. [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] asks whether he would have told them, whether it would have changed what happened, but [[Jonathan Sims|John]] does not know the answer.
