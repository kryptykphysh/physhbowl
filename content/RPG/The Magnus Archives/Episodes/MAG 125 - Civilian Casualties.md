# Statement

Sergeant [[Terrance Simpson]] is heading to investigate a break-in with his partner, Constable [[Carla Ross]], when they spot someone out on the nearby croft. They stop to investigate and see [[Callum McKenzie]], a local pub owner. He is impaled on a pitchfork, the tines protruding from his back. They run toward him but stop dead in their tracks when he turns to face them.

[[Callum McKenzie]] is covered in blood, far more than than his wounds would suggest, and he is smiling. As he sees [[Terrance Simpson]] and [[Carla Ross|Carla]], he takes off in a sprint towards them, ignoring their calls to stop. They see his hands are covered in bits of broken glass and [[Terrance Simpson]] _knows_ that he intends to kill them.

[[Terrance Simpson]] manages to grab the handle of the pitchfork still sticking out of [[Callum McKenzie|Callum]]'s stomach, which keeps him at bay as he tries to tear into [[Terrance Simpson]] with his glass-covered hands. Determined to kill him, [[Callum McKenzie|Callum]] pushes against the pitchfork, driving it further into his body. Something within him tears and he collapses as he gushes blood. Even as he lies dying, he tries to claw at them, and his eyes are clear and focused. [[Terrance Simpson]] and [[Carla Ross|Carla]] try to radio for help but hear only static and the faint sound of bagpipes in response. [[Terrance Simpson]] hears sheep bleating in the distance.

They spot a one-storey cottage in the distance. The windows are broken and there are smears of blood by the door. Inside, an old woman is sitting in a rocking chair; she is pinned to the chair by a kitchen knife through the neck.

In front of her lies two corpses entwined in a violent embrace. They have torn into each other and are missing all their fingers. Their throats are cut and across their faces are smiles halfway between ecstasy and all-consuming anger. In the kitchen is a fourth corpse with her head is shoved into the oven and burned beyond recognition. The glass embedded in her back marks [[Callum McKenzie]] as her murderer.

They follow a trail of blood and gore to the cottage of [[Angus Stewart]] where all sixteen other residents of Lanncraig have torn each other to pieces. The day before, a young woman from Inverness had parked her small, mobile lending library outside. Now she lies at the centre of the carnage. In her hands is an old and worn paperback, torn in two along the spine. It is soaked in blood and the cover is unreadable and inside, every single page is blank. Backup eventually arrives and, officially, the incident is covered up as a, "Drunken brawl that got out of hand".

[[Terrance Simpson]] moves back to Macclesfield soon after, even at the cost of his marriage. He is still disturbed by the sounds of bagpipes and sheep.

# Post-Statement

[[Jonathan Sims|Jonathan]] talks about how [[The Slaughter]] fascinates him because he wonders how in-control the victims are. He feels focused and ready, but he finds himself alone. He believes [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] is avoiding him.

[[Jonathan Sims|John]] says [[Basira Hussain|Basira]] was right about the [[The Magnus Institute|Institute]] being watched. He has noticed two different people wearing symbols for [[The People's Church of the Divine Host]], and it is rare he goes anywhere without [[The Web|cobwebs]] anymore. However, he is more concerned with internal threats, such as [[Peter Lukas]].

As [[Jonathan Sims|John]] mentions that he is worried about [[Melanie King|Melanie]] and, "That bullet pumping violence into her, waiting to turn this place into another Lanncraig," a strong static plays over his words. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] pauses and wonders if he has read that somewhere. He muses about the bullet above her tibia that she described in [[MAG 117 - Testament]], which he knows is lodged inside and infecting her. He says he needs to go find [[Basira Hussain|Basira]].

Later, [[Jonathan Sims|John]] and [[Basira Hussain|Basira]] are talking while [[Melanie King|Melanie]] is snoring. [[Basira Hussain|Basira]] says, "The stuff she takes is pretty strong these days," and defends [[Melanie King|Melanie]]'s use of sleeping pills, saying, "Sleep is hard," and that she has a camp bed on the other side of the tunnels because she wants to keep an eye on them but give them some space. It is just not safe for them to live outside the [[The Magnus Institute|Institute]] anymore. [[Basira Hussain|Basira]] gives [[Jonathan Sims|John]] some local anaesthetic and asks if he knows the right nerve to hit. There is a crackle of static, and [[Jonathan Sims|John]] confidently says, "Here."

After thirty minutes, once the anaesthetic has started working, the [[Tape Recorders|tape recorder]] turns itself back on. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] cuts the leg of [[Melanie King|Melanie]]'s trousers, and there is static as he points out the bullet and something rotten under the skin, which [[Basira Hussain|Basira]] cannot see. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] removes the bullet, which clinks audibly, and at that exact moment, [[Melanie King|Melanie]] startles awake. She shrieks at [[Jonathan Sims|John]] to get away from her and starts attacking, stabbing him in the shoulder with a scalpel. She screams that she cannot feel her leg and [[Jonathan Sims|John]] tries to tell her it is the anaesthetic, but [[Basira Hussain|Basira]] pulls him away and they are going to leave [[Melanie King|Melanie]] alone. The [[Tape Recorders|tape recorder]] shuts off abruptly.
