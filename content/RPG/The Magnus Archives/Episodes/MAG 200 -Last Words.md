# Statement

Once, there was Fear: the fear of [[The Hunt|predation]], of the [[The End|death]] it brought. And the fear became. And it began to want. The fear man gave it satisfied it. But as man grew, slowly, more fears began to feed the Entity: the fear of death from predators became fear of [[The Dark]] they lived in. And still man evolved, fearing what it [[The Stranger|did not know]], that their eyes would [[The Spiral|lie to them]] or show them [[The Eye|things they did not wish to see]], that the fire they had mastered would [[The Desolation|burn them]]. As more fears grew, the [[The Entities|Entity]] fractured, becoming many. And in their new forms, they found freedom, and more fears formed, the fears of [[The Vast|insignificance]] and [[The Web|manipulation]] and [[The Slaughter|war]].

And as the fears grew, [[Avatar|some humans]] did not recoil from them. They reached out, hungering for the fears as much as the fears hungered for them. [[The Entities]] gave them powers, and they fed their patrons. And eventually, [[The Entities]] could just barely touch reality. [[The Entities]] began to experiment with their new abilities, creating monsters that fed them even more.

[[The Entities]] became greedy, wanting more. Wanting to exist. The [[Avatar|Avatars]] tried, but none of them managed to bring their gods into this world. The [[The Entities|Fears]] simply could not conceive of being.

One, [[The Web]], saw the truth. [[The Web]] could think and plan. It saw what would happen if they were brought into the world but it could not tell the other fears, for even if they could understand, [[The Web]] is not truthful. It lies, as is its nature. So it schemed and manipulated. It chose [[The Eye]], the most stupid of [[The Entities]], and manipulated it. It brought about the new world, so it could finally escape.

But [[Jonathan Sims|John]] will not let it.

# Post-Statement

[[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] rushes in, intending to stop [[Jonathan Sims|John]] from killing [[Jonah Magnus|Jonah]], but he is too late. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] tries to tell him he did the right thing, but [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] screams that he broke his promise. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] says [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] should tell the others, that they need to call off the plan, but [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] says he knew [[Jonathan Sims|John]] would try something, so the others are going to the gas main _early_. [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] thought he could stall [[Jonathan Sims|John]], keep him from killing [[Jonah Magnus|Jonah]]. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] says it will be okay, they can still be together, but [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] says it is not [[Jonathan Sims|John]]. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] reminds [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] that he had promised not to stop [[Jonathan Sims|John]] from sacrificing himself, but [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] will not listen. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] says it will all be okay, he can call off [[The Eye]]'s servants. Besides, [[Melanie King|Melanie]], [[Basira Hussain|Basira]], and [[Georgie Barker|Georgie]] cannot light the gas main without the [[Web Lighter]].

...Which [[Jonathan Sims|John]] said [[Georgie Barker|Georgie]] could hold on to.

The destruction begins, causing [[Jonathan Sims|John]] severe pain. He tells [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] to leave, but [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] will not leave without him. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] then tells [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] to kill him, finish the job, save the world. [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] does not want to, but [[Jonathan Sims|John]] says that live or die, whatever happens, they will be together. They kiss. [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] takes the knife and stabs [[Jonathan Sims|John]], and the recording turns off.

In a restored world, [[Basira Hussain|Basira]], [[Melanie King|Melanie]], and [[Georgie Barker|Georgie]] shift through the rubble of the Panopticon. [[Basira Hussain|Basira]] is surprised to find a tape recorder that still works. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] and [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] have not been found, dead or otherwise. [[Melanie King|Melanie]] says they should accept they are gone and admits that is probably for the best. Apparently, [[Simon Fairchild]], having lost his abilities as an [[Avatar]], was _not_ welcomed back into the normal world; imagine the world's reaction to '[[The Archivist]]'. It is revealed that [[Rosie Zampano|Rosie]] survived, and [[The Admiral]] is almost all back to normal, thanks to canned tuna. The three contemplate destroying the tape, but [[Basira Hussain|Basira]] says that they can just turn it off. [[Melanie King|Melanie]] and [[Georgie Barker|Georgie]] walk away and [[Basira Hussain|Basira]] addresses whoever is listening:

"Good luck."
