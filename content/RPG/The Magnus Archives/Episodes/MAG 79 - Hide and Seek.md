# Recording

**Note:** [[The Distortion|Michael]] uses both he/him and it/its pronouns.

[[Timothy Stoker|Tim]] and [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] disregard [[Jonathan Sims|John's]] request to take a few days off from work and opt to investigate. They take [[Martin Blackwood|Martin's]] tape recorder to get recorded evidence in case something goes wrong. While [[Timothy Stoker|Tim]] insists he took a blank tape, he accidentally records over one of [[Martin Blackwood|Martin's]] poems, _Streets_. [[Timothy Stoker|Tim]] and [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] find [[Jonathan Sims|John's]] office empty and worry that he is going to badly hurt himself or someone else.

While [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] is concerned [[Timothy Stoker|Tim]]'s motive is to get [[Jonathan Sims|John]] fired, [[Timothy Stoker|Tim]] explains that his concerns are related to the supernatural as there is something in the [[The Magnus Institute|Institute]] controlling their actions, including [[Timothy Stoker|Tim]]'s inability to quit and [[Jonathan Sims|John]]'s inability to fire him. They open a door and are confronted by [[Not-Sasha]] in her true form, the [[NotThem]]. [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] notices it looks like [[Not-Sasha]] but [[Timothy Stoker|Tim]] refuses to entertain the notion. [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] decides to follow the creature into the tunnels and [[Timothy Stoker|Tim]] reluctantly joins him.

The recording switches over to [[Jonathan Sims|John]], furious with himself. He explains he took [[The Distortion|Michael's]] door rather than face [[Not-Sasha]]. The door opened into the tunnels and he does not know where he is, so he resolves to wait. He does not believe the [[Not-Them]] will give up but will not risk attracting its attention, and notes it may already be down there with him. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] berates himself for destroying the [[Web Table]], realising too late that the [[NotThem]] was bound by the table rather than connected to it. He worries over the amount of tape he has left and decides to stop recording in order to conserve it. As he does, he hears [[Not-Sasha]] calling his name.  

The recording switches back to [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] and [[Timothy Stoker|Tim]]. [[Timothy Stoker|Tim]] is worried he heard something, and the two realise that they will not find [[Jonathan Sims|John]] by themselves. [[Timothy Stoker|Tim]] suggests they get [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]] from his office but refuses to leave [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] alone in the tunnels. They start to argue about [[Timothy Stoker|Tim]]'s recent behavior and [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]]'s reasons for staying at [[The Magnus Institute]] when they are interrupted by [[The Distortion|Michael]]. He explains that it is waiting to see if [[Jonathan Sims|John]] will die, and it finds amusement in the idea. He idly threatens to kill [[Timothy Stoker|Tim]] and [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] to keep them from aiding [[Jonathan Sims|John]], then manifests a door as he distorts his hands to be sharp and pointed. The two archival assistants flee into [[The Distortion|Michael's]] door and find themselves in a strange, distorted corridor.

The recording switches back to [[Jonathan Sims|John]], who is in a panic. He has fled deeper into the tunnels and has now armed himself with a metal pipe, something that brings him comfort despite its uselessness. He has realized [[Sasha James]] has been erased and hopes this tape will provide evidence of his voice in case the same happens to him. As [[Not-Sasha]] calls to [[Jonathan Sims|John]], her words distort into the [[NotThem]]'s true voice. She taunts him by saying she will take his body the way he took [[Sasha James|Sasha's]] and that no one will ever know. She goes on to say that the process will hurt, just as it hurt for [[Sasha James|Sasha]], and [[Jonathan Sims|John]] yells at her to shut up, revealing his hiding spot and prompting him to flee.  

The recording switches back to [[Timothy Stoker|Tim]] and [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]]. With distorted voices, they express confusion and fear over their surroundings and toward [[The Distortion|Michael]]. [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] remarks that the tape recorder seems to be malfunctioning as they continue to try navigating the corridors.

The recording switches back to [[Jonathan Sims|John]], where [[Not-Sasha]]'s voice is no longer distorted. She describes her existence in a mocking production of a statement, where she briefly recounts the history of the [[NotThem]]. She describes how she would take identities and feed off the fear of the few people who realised until being bound to the web table by [[Adelard Dekker]], at which point she had her friends take the table from one place to another so that she could continue inspiring this fear. Eventually the [[NotThem]] was taken to [[The Magnus Institute]] with the intention of stealing secrets, but she was unable to cause fear until [[Jonathan Sims|John]] destroyed the table after assembling the clues she had left for him.

[[Not-Sasha]] mocks [[Jonathan Sims|John]] for being so unaware and says he is a shadow of his predecessor, [[Gertrude Robinson]]. Her voice continues to distort as she states even she would make for a better Archivist than him and begins to wonder if such a thing would be possible, but remarks that [[Jonathan Sims|John]] would miss [[The Unknowing]]. As she speaks, [[Jonathan Sims|John]] quietly and incessantly apologizes to [[Timothy Stoker|Tim]], [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]], and [[Sasha James|Sasha]] for what he has done. [[Not-Sasha]] eventually concludes that taking [[Jonathan Sims|John]]'s body is unlikely to "rob [[The Eye]] of its pupil", and that she will simply kill him as originally planned.

There is a sound of stone and brick suddenly shifting, and [[Not-Sasha]] screams before disappearing. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] is approached by [[Jurgen Leitner|the strange man]] who has saved him. The unidentified man says it is time he and [[Jonathan Sims|John]] had a talk.
