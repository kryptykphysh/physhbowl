# Recording

[[Rosie Zampano|Rosie]] is showing [[Jonathan Sims]] his new office as head archivist. He notes the disorganised state of the Archives and they chat about his predecessor [[Gertrude Robinson]]. He only met her a few times and got the impression that she did not care for him; [[Rosie Zampano|Rosie]] remarks that she did not really like anybody much.

[[Jonathan Sims|John]] asks what actually happened to her, as he has heard rumours floating around the research department that she died at the desk in his new office. [[Rosie Zampano|Rosie]] seems to confirm that this was the case and offers to speak with [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]] about a replacement desk. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] assures her that it is fine as he does not believe in ghosts. [[Rosie Zampano|Rosie]] leaves, wishing him luck and notes that [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]] should be coming down to speak with him soon.

[[Jonathan Sims|John]] immediately gets to work. He sets his laptop to record and reads the entirety of case [[MAG 1 - Angler Fish|#0122204]].

He has just finished when [[[[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] Blackwood]] walks in, asking if he has seen a dog. When [[Jonathan Sims|John]] is confused, he clarifies that he thinks it was a spaniel. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] demands to know why there would be a dog in the Archives and just who [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] is. [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] nervously introduces himself and explains that he was making friends with the dog outside and accidentally let it in.

He mentions that he works in the Archives and [[Jonathan Sims|John]] immediately disbelieves him, as he only requested [[Timothy Stoker|Tim]] and [[Sasha James|Sasha]] as assistants. [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] clarifies that [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]] transferred him to the Archives from the library, apparently without telling [[Jonathan Sims|John]].

[[Jonathan Sims|John]] notes that, as [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]]'s boss, he could fire him if the dog situation is not resolved immediately. [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] laughs and agrees before realising [[Jonathan Sims|John]] is being serious. He leaves to deal with the dog, apologising profusely as he walks out.

[[Jonathan Sims|John]] gets back to work and reads the entirety of case [[MAG 2 - Do Not Open|#9982211]]. After the statement he clicks around on his laptop, unsuccessfully trying to play the recording.

[[Timothy Stoker|Tim]] shows up and checks in with [[Jonathan Sims|John]], who admits that he is having a bit of a rocky start in the Archives. He mentions that he is trying to record the Gillespie statement and [[Timothy Stoker|Tim]] notes that it is real weird how Gillespie was the only resident in that block of flats for two whole years. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] agrees, that _is_ weird.

[[Timothy Stoker|Tim]] gets back to the business at hand and reports that the dog is still in the Archives and it has made a mess of the doggy variety. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] closes his laptop and says that they will just have to deal with the situation before it escalates. He also asks if they have any better recording equipment since his laptop is acting up. [[Timothy Stoker|Tim]] thinks they might have some old [[Tape Recorders]] in storage and [[Jonathan Sims|John]] figures that might work.
