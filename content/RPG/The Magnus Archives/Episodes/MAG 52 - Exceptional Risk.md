# Statement

[[Phillip Brown]] was a prison officer at HMP Wakefield from 1990 - 2002. In 1995, [[Robert Montauk]] was admitted to the prison. He was over 6 feet tall with a flat, angular face and was built like a barge. Shortly after [[Robert Montauk|Robert]] arrived at the prison, [[Ivan Ilich]] attacked him, but [[Robert Montauk|Robert]] easily deflected the attack and dislocated his shoulder. [[Robert Montauk|Robert]] was shipped off to F-wing, and [[Dave Harrington]], another prisoner, started regularly spreading various rumors that he'd escaped and/or killed guards.

In 1998 the government set up Close Supervision Centres, the CSC, and the F-wing quickly became one: the inmates there were kept in clinically clean and empty rooms, under extremely close supervision. [[Phillip Brown|Phillip]] was asked to join the unit. Those in CSC were treated poorly to keep them in line.

In 2001 [[Robert Montauk|Robert]]'s [[Julia Montauk|daughter]] started to visit him, presumably just turning 18. In late March 2002, he had his only other visitor: a man in his late fifties with a well-tailored black suit who looked like he might be blind. The two of them stared at each other with a fierce hatred, before the light bulb blew out. [[Phillip Brown|Phillip]] heard the man whisper, "You didn't think you could kill it for long, did you?" When [[Phillip Brown|Phillip]] took him back to his cell, he noticed [[Robert Montauk|Robert]] was weeping but didn't remark on it.

In the next few months, the prison had problems with the plumbing, with brackish water coming out of the taps, as well as lights randomly going out. On November 1st, 2002, the lights went out; he and [[Peter Gordo|Pete]], a colleague in the CSC, went to check on the inmates. In [[Robert Montauk|Robert]]'s cell, they found impenetrable pitch darkness which their lights could not illuminate. There was a wet sound of tearing coming from the cell. As [[Phillip Brown|Phillip]] stepped closer to the cell his torch went out. He heard [[Peter Gordo|Pete]] scrabbling to find him, before saying, "There you are," but he had grabbed something else. There was [[The Still And Lightless Beast|a low growl]], [[Peter Gordo|Pete]] screamed, and [[Phillip Brown|Phillip]] heard him fall to the floor. Suddenly, the lights came back on and [[Peter Gordo|Pete]] was on the floor unharmed. They found [[Robert Montauk|Robert]] dead from stab wounds in his cell.

[[Phillip Brown|Phillip]] was blamed for [[Robert Montauk|Robert]]'s death due to 'gross incompetence' and was fired. [[Peter Gordo|Pete]] resigned 2 hours after the lights came back on.

# Post-Statement

[[Timothy Stoker|Tim]] was not able to find out much information on [[Peter Gordo]], but [[Sasha James]] found the 2002 visitor logs for Wakefield, which identified the man who visited [[Robert Montauk|Robert]] as [[Maxwell Rayner]].

[[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] was unable to track down [[Phillip Brown|Phillip]]. [[Caroline Brodie]], his wife, left him in 2004 when his dismissal caused him to drink more heavily and he became abusive. He sent a letter from Waterford in Ireland asking for forgiveness, but she did not reply.
