# Statement

[[Andrea Nunis]] has a passion for travelling and exploring alone that borders on a compulsion. She had stayed at a shack in Wales with her parents when she was young and she was always overcome with a feeling of wonder and excitement whenever she went there. She took a gap year and travelled around Europe for 4 months, just scraping by and occasionally needing to sleep on the streets. She found it hard to form relationships because of this.

On her 25th birthday she decided to travel to Europe again for a shorter stint. She went to Italy and visited Venice, Rome, and San Marino, then continued north via Florence. Whilst in a hostel there, she met [[Ethan Taylor]], a travelling Australian, who felt the same need to travel as herself. They accidentally ended up going north together and he convinced her to go to the small city of Genoa in Italy. They rented a private room there and spent the evenings dining and talking.

[[Ethan Taylor|Ethan]] explained about an older part of town he had found that he wanted to explore. That day, [[Andrea Nunis|Andrea]] went to a café to read and was confronted by a tall man in a bright t-shirt with long black hair, who said that she was marked. He was unhappy that she had appeared whilst he was on holiday, and told her to keep her mother's face in her mind, knowing she had a close relationship with her. [[Ethan Taylor|Ethan]] did not return back that evening. After a few days she decided to try and find him, heading into the area of town that [[Ethan Taylor|Ethan]] mentioned.

As she neared the place that [[Ethan Taylor|Ethan]] had mentioned, the sky became grey and the buildings duller. She saw no one for a long time, but heard a crowd in the distance. As she approached she found herself in a wider street surrounded by people. The noise, she realised, was not talking. As she focused on those that surrounded her, she realised that they all had blurred faces as though screaming played at great speeds on their face, they also wore different versions of the same clothes.

She remembered what the man in the coffee shop had said earlier and thought about her mother, concentrating on her face and the love she felt for her. After a short while, she realised that the creatures had gone and she was on another street. An hour later she got home, but there was still no sign of [[Ethan Taylor|Ethan]]. She decided to return home early and spend some time with her mother, and is not planning to travel by herself again.

# Post-Statement

[[Sasha James]] interviewed [[Andrea Nunis|Andrea]], who confirmed that she was back to travelling solo and had not had any similar experiences since. [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] found that there were missing travellers reported in Genoa, but no more than other cities of that size.

[[Jonathan Sims]] suspects that the man [[Andrea Nunis|Andrea]] met was [[Gerard Keay]], although this would have happened just after he had been acquitted of [[Mary Keay|his mother's]] murder. [[Timothy Stoker|Tim]] got in touch with the Manena Hostel, who confirmed [[Ethan Taylor|Ethan]]'s backpack was never claimed.
