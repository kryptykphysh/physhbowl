# Pre-Statement

[[Breekon & Hope|Breekon]] is in the Archives. When [[Basira Hussain|Basira]] finds him, she tells [[Jonathan Sims|John]] to get [[Melanie King|Melanie]], but he already knows that [[Breekon & Hope|Breekon]] is here and imagines that he is here to make a delivery. [[Breekon & Hope|Breekon]] proves him right, saying he is here to drop off a package. [[Basira Hussain|Basira]] asks if [[Breekon & Hope|Breekon]] is after revenge and [[Jonathan Sims|John]] compels him to answer. [[Breekon & Hope|Breekon]] admits that he is here for revenge, just like when he fed [[Alice Daisy Tonner|Daisy]] to [[The Coffin]]. He has realised that he is not tied to [[The Coffin]] anymore and thought that the Archives could have it, to pay their respects.

[[Jonathan Sims|John]] wants to know exactly why [[Breekon & Hope|Breekon]] is here and compels him. [[Breekon & Hope|Breekon]] is unsure, he is lost without Hope and he does not know what happens now. He thought he might kill [[Jonathan Sims|John]], but missed his chance, and figured he might just deliver something. So he brought them [[The Coffin]], in case the rest of them want to join [[Alice Daisy Tonner|Daisy]].

[[Basira Hussain|Basira]] tells [[Breekon & Hope|Breekon]] to get out, but he refuses. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] starts to use his powers on him, and [[Breekon & Hope|Breekon]] makes uncomfortable choking sounds as static rises. He cries out in distress as he flees, or is expelled, from the Archives. As the static recedes [[Jonathan Sims|John]] asks [[Basira Hussain|Basira]] to get him a pen and he starts to write.

# Statement

[[Breekon & Hope]] started in a plague. They had a cart of corpses with screaming, twisted faces and leaking pus, that was led by an old mule. They would knock on doors and cry for people to bring the dead to them. Everyone feared [[Breekon & Hope]] when they came towards them, not because of the plague or the death they awaited, but because of them: two strangers with faces they would only half remember, who were unstoppable and uncertain and would bring a fate they would beg their god to forget. People did not know them, but they knew what they did. Villages who had no bodies before they arrived would pile high their cart before they left. They did not kill people, they were only the bringers of their awful fate. When they left their destination, [[Breekon & Hope|Hope]] would reach onto the cart and find a new face from a corpse and place it over the one he already wore. [[Breekon & Hope|Breekon]] kept the face that they chose, but he loved [[Breekon & Hope|Hope]] for his levity.

They served as deckhands aboard the _[[Robert Small]]_ as it made its way to Australia. Enlisting was not needed, but they were on the list for any crew that deserved them. The quartermaster noticed them when counting rations and saw them for what they were, so [[Breekon & Hope|Breekon]] ate his pen and [[Breekon & Hope|Hope]] ate his tongue. They enjoyed the journey which was magnificent for them because there was no waiting or searching for a delivery; they were being moved towards the completion of their task. This was the first time they saw what would become of the place: [[The Eye]]'s pedestal, but they were too drunk on the approaching horror to notice. A young man called Jack tried to jump overboard, but [[Breekon & Hope|Hope]] caught him. Jack started begging and pleading to just be able to drown, but he died on land like he was supposed to.

They were conductors of a train, one that never seemed to get you where you wanted to go unless that place had something dreadful waiting for you. They threw those overboard who looked like they were having too good of a time. They did not like this job, as too many stepped off with a smile despite their work. They once had some luggage, a thrumming silk-wrapped thing of [[The Web]], hiding away in an old steamer trunk. They found an old woman near the caboose who they led to the luggage car; she followed but was crying silently. [[The Web]] was always an easy job for them, as it knows too much to be a stranger but hides its knowing well enough to endure. They knew the old woman would not scream, but Hope still thought to cover it up with laughter.

[[Breekon & Hopen]] worked for London auction-houses. They stole their first automobile from two men. They moved a lot of things in those years. [[Breekon & Hope|Breekon]]'s favourite was an old knife delivered to a leering banker who, upon seeing the two, knew exactly what they had done. The banker stabbed the two with the knife, but no blood flowed. The knife turned and cut the banker to pieces. The two delivered the knife back to Christie's and that was the end of their auction jobs. They would take whatever jobs they were offered, delivering things that would bring fear and misery, but there was no joy in carrying [[The Flesh|meat]] or [[The Spiral|spiral]] things.

Then the good times were when they joined [[Circus of the Other|the circus]]. Now, they were with their own kind at last. Their kind all had names, but no one would pretend they were real and faces changed more often than clothes. The only recognizable fact was their function in the show. [[Breekon & Hope]] carried and helped move the [[Circus of the Other|circus]] to the next town. Sometimes they even joined in, lifting weights and things that looked like animals. Sometimes they lifted audience members, and sometimes they even put them down. People always feared them. Russia's cold air made the absence of their breath apparent, but no one ever mentioned it.

They did not like the puppet when [[Gregor Orsinov|Orsinov]] began to carve it, as it seemed wrong to try and bring one like them about. However, when [[Gregor Orsinov|Orsinov]] carved into the thing that once called itself Grimaldi and fed the unneeded pieces to the shuddering organist, even they were impressed. When the [[Nikola Orsinov|faceless puppet]] peeled its creator and moved with its tendon strings, Hope looked at [[Breekon & Hope|Breekon]] and laughed and laughed. They followed her a while but she was unpredictable and they broke off from her when she lost the [[Gorilla Skin|ancient skin]].

They found themselves a lorry and took empty crates to and fro. It was not their cargo that brought fear, but they who brought fear to their cargo. However [[Breekon & Hope|Hope]] was not content and he did not laugh like he used to. Driving aimlessly and waiting for the cargo sat badly with them. They were meant to have cargo and an address, so [[they found a man named [[MAG 96 - Return to Sender|Breekon]]]] and broke him. They took the van and started delivering again, but they were reckless and desperate for the surety they had not felt since they left [[Circus of the Other|the circus]], so they took [[The Coffin]]. It was one like them (the man known as, "[[Jonathan Sims|John]]") that found it, thinking that it would be easy to control because it was in chains. When [[MAG 2 - Do Not Open|a victim passed the test]], [[The Coffin]] fed on the one that had tried to master it. There was no mention of accomplices like [[Breekon & Hope]] in [[The Coffin]]'s test, so they were not eaten but were now stuck carrying it for a long time.

Even when the mannequin, [[Nikola Orsinov]], came back to them and told them they could help with [[The Unknowing]], they still had to carry [[The Coffin]], but [[Breekon & Hope|Breekon]] thinks it was worth it in the end because when the hunter, [[Alice Daisy Tonner|Daisy]], killed [[Breekon & Hope|Hope]], [[The Coffin]] was there and [[Breekon & Hope|Breekon]] fed her to it. As punishment for making the two of them a one, she will live trapped, helpless, and entombed forever; this is his remaining comfort after their failure.

He can feel himself fading and weakening, with no reason to move and nothing to deliver. He is no longer tied to [[The Coffin]], so the Archives can have it and know that their friend is in there. When they cannot bear it anymore, they can join her inside. He has never felt hate or loss before now, and now he wants to share them with the Archives' group.

# Post-Statement

[[The Coffin]] leads to [[The Buried]] and [[Alice Daisy Tonner|Daisy]] is alive inside, but [[Basira Hussain|Basira]] and [[Jonathan Sims|John]] both agree that they cannot open it. They are not sure why [[Breekon & Hope|Breekon]] gave it to them; [[Jonathan Sims|John]] thinks he just wanted to make another delivery.

[[Basira Hussain|Basira]] decides she has to leave for a week or two to follow up on some leads. She tells [[Jonathan Sims|John]] she survived [[The Unknowing]] by reasoning her way out, and she can only trust herself. She does not want him to use his powers to know anything about her.
