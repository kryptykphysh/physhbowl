[[Father Edwin Burroughs]] was arrested in 2009 for the murder and cannibalism of two students. He had been incarcerated in a mental institution, and, when interviewed, had difficulty picking between reality and fiction, not being sure if the interviewer was real or an apparition.

As he tells his story, [[Father Edwin Burroughs|Edwin]] occasionally tries to say words such as "Lord" or "Jesus" but is unable to complete them. He explains that "It won't let me say the words. It won't let me pray, either."

[[Father Edwin Burroughs|Edwin]] trained as an exorcist for the diocese of Oxford from 2005 to 2009 with Father Harrogate. In his time he performed over 100 exorcisms, although he admits that it is hard to distinguish how many were actual possessions or had natural explanations. He did note that he had once been cursed in Sumerian by an illiterate young man and that on another occasion an old Jamaican man had told him the names of his--[[Father Edwin Burroughs|Edwin]]'s--childhood pets.

Whilst working at St. Aldate's his colleague Father Singh said that one of the students, [[Bethany O'Connor]], was in need of an exorcist as she was not in control of her mind. When they met, [[Bethany O'Connor|Bethany]] had difficulty separating fact and fiction, almost eating a stone without realising.

[[Bethany O'Connor|Bethany]] explained that the problem started when she moved to 89 Bullingdon Road. They went to the house, which was separated into flats. In her room, the wallpaper had been hacked away and the word "Mentis" was written on the bare brick. [[Bethany O'Connor|Bethany]] could not see it. [[Father Edwin Burroughs|Edwin]] provided a short blessing and said he would return later.

Later, Father Burroughs called Father Singh to organise a full exorcism, which he agreed to. However, [[Father Edwin Burroughs|Edwin]] got a call from The John Radcliffe Hospital, saying that [[Bethany O'Connor|Bethany]] had been brought in with facial lacerations after a fight with her housemate, caused by her falling into a mirror. Alarmed by this escalation, [[Father Edwin Burroughs|Edwin]] went to the hospital.

The police left after taking [[Bethany O'Connor|Bethany]]'s statement and [[Father Edwin Burroughs|Edwin]] attempted the exorcism. Unusually, he got no reaction from [[Bethany O'Connor|Bethany]], who eventually said "I'm so sorry, it wants your faith." She started to convulse and had a brain haemorrhage and died. [[Father Edwin Burroughs|Edwin]] was told off by his bishop for his involvement and [[Anne Willett|Anne "Annie" Willett]], the nurse who called [[Father Edwin Burroughs|Edwin]], was almost fired.

Over the next few years, [[Father Edwin Burroughs|Edwin]] drank more, causing the other priests to worry about him, though they did not do much about it. A couple of years after [[Bethany O'Connor|Bethany]]'s death he had another call from [[Anne Willett|Annie]], saying [[MAG 8 - Burned Out|a builder had been to Hill Top Road and had a scare]]. Although [[Father Edwin Burroughs|Edwin]] told [[Anne Willett|Annie]] he was not exorcising at present, she said it should just require a blessing and that she did not trust Father Harrogate. [[Father Edwin Burroughs|Edwin]] agreed to look into it.

At Hill Top Road, [[Father Edwin Burroughs|Edwin]] found the house under construction. When he knocked, the builder answered the door and [[Father Edwin Burroughs|Edwin]] explained that he was there to give a blessing. He prayed for protection as [[Ivo Lensik]] went into the back garden. As he did, he felt hot and could smell smoke but see no flame. He prayed for protection and something that was not God answered. He felt his lips moving without his control and he said "I am not for you. I am marked." The heat stopped and [[Father Edwin Burroughs|Edwin]] heard the tree being uprooted outside. He excused himself to the builder and left.

This statement ends here but is followed up in [[MAG 20 - Desecrated Host]].
