# Statement

[[Sasha James]] is one of the assistant researchers who assists [[Jonathan Sims]] with the statements of [[The Magnus Archives]]. She had read the statements regarding [[Jane Prentiss]], but did not view her as presently dangerous as [[Martin Blackwood]] had survived his encounter with her. Although [[Sasha James|Sasha]] has seen many of the silver worms outside, she has crushed them as she saw them.

[[Sasha James|Sasha]] lives in an old, Victorian building located near Finsbury Park. On 31st March 2016 she was leaving her flat when she saw a strange figure in the warped glass window in the stairwell of her building: there appeared to be a figure with a limp, boneless appearance and incredibly large, pointed hands staring at her from the street. When she looked at the figure through the unwarped glass she saw a man with long, blond hair purchasing lilies, but viewing him through the distorted glass gave him that same strange appearance.

She walked from Victoria Station to work and saw the same man in a coffee shop, but didn't confront him. After work, she saw him still seated in the café despite it usually being closed by that time. He motioned her to come inside and sit, and she obliged.

The two sat in silence until [[Sasha James|Sasha]] asked the man what he was. He gave a strange laugh and said it didn't matter, but that she could call him [[The Distortion|Michael]]. They again sat in silence until [[Sasha James|Sasha]] asked what he wanted, to which he replied he wanted to help. When asked if it meant helping to stop [[Jane Prentiss]], [[The Distortion|Michael]] laughed and told [[Sasha James|Sasha]] she had no idea what was going on. It went on to say that he didn't care if her coworkers died, but that it wanted to be friends. [[The Distortion|Michael]] put his hand in [[Sasha James|Sasha]]'s as he said this, and she noted that it felt unnaturally heavy and sharp. Upset by [[The Distortion|Michael]]'s deflection and strange, inhuman form, [[Sasha James|Sasha]] got up to leave. It called after her to say that if she wanted to save [[Jonathan Sims|John]], [[Timothy Stoker|Tim]], or [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]], he would be waiting for her at Hanwell Cemetery.

[[Sasha James|Sasha]] did not mention her encounter to her coworkers. She went to Hanwell Cemetry the next evening after work, where she found [[The Distortion|Michael]] waiting for her. He guided her away from the cemetery and into the residential area of Azalea Close, finally stopping at an abandoned pub. Inside the pub, [[Sasha James|Sasha]] heard a low moan from a dark corner. There, she saw a man covered in writhing, silver worms and realised that the floor was also covered in the worms. She gasped, prompting the worms inside and surrounding the man to quickly move toward her.

[[Sasha James|Sasha]] stumbled away from the worms while [[The Distortion|Michael]] looked on passively. She grabbed a nearby fire extinguisher and sprayed the worms. They immediately shrivelled and died, and she engulfed the worm-infested man in the CO2 gas. Once he stopped moving she went through his pockets and found a driving licence, identifying the man as [[Timothy Hodge]]. [[The Distortion|Michael]] stepped forward and reached into her shoulder, removing a small silver worm that [[Sasha James|Sasha]] hadn't noticed enter her skin.

[[Sasha James|Sasha]]'s recollection becomes hazy after this; the next thing she remembered was that [[The Distortion|Michael]] and [[Timothy Hodge|Timothy]] were gone. She returned to [[The Magnus Institute]] and accidentally woke [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]].

# Post-Statement

[[Jonathan Sims|John]] believes [[Sasha James|Sasha]]'s statement, and remarks that it is strange that [[Timothy Hodge|Timothy]] and [[Harriet Lee]] were affected differently. He also comments on [[The Distortion|Michael]] wanting to be friends with [[Sasha James|Sasha]], as other encounters with the paranormal had been exclusively antagonistic. [[Sasha James|Sasha]] has taken a few days off and [[Jonathan Sims|John]] is going to ask [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]] for more CO2 fire extinguishers.
