# Recording

[[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] visits and talks to an unconscious [[Jonathan Sims|John]] in the hospital. He says that things are bad but he is getting by. [[Basira Hussain|Basira]] and [[Melanie King|Melanie]] are still at the [[The Magnus Institute|Institute]] but they all need [[Jonathan Sims|John]]. [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] does not know how much longer he can do this. He needs [[Jonathan Sims|John]].

He starts to plead with [[Jonathan Sims|John]] but is interrupted by a phonecall. He answers the call and discusses things with the person on the other end. He says that he is with [[Jonathan Sims|John]] right now and that he other person was right about something. He asks if the others will be safe and agrees to do something after getting an answer. He ends the call and says goodbye to [[Jonathan Sims|John]] before leaving.
