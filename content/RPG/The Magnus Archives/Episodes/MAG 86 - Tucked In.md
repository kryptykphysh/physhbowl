# Statement

[[Benjamin Hatendi]] was always afraid of the dark, and used to use blankets to help himself get over the fear of the blackness. As he grew older he stopped worrying.

His school friend, [[Robin Patton]]'s, mother phoned [[Benjamin Hatendi|Benjamin]] to say she had not heard from [[Robin Patton|Robin]] in a month and asked if [[Benjamin Hatendi|Benjamin]] would check on him. 

[[Benjamin Hatendi|Benjamin]] went over to [[Robin Patton|Robin]]'s house to find all the lights off and a figure in a window. He knocked and went into the house, to find the house in an absolute mess and there was a rancid smell coming from the fridge. A calendar on the wall was pinned to January.

As [[Benjamin Hatendi|Benjamin]] went to look for a phone he saw movement from the corner of his eye. After retrieving a heavy duty torch he went into [[Robin Patton|Robin]]'s bedroom, which was a similar mess, including a calendar marked off the 12th February and the rancid smell from downstairs was stronger up here. He opened the cupboard to find the source of the smell, and a large slimy bundle slid out.

[[Benjamin Hatendi|Benjamin]] realised that this was a dead [[Robin Patton|Robin]], wrapped in a blanket and covered in a thick glistening liquid, which touched [[Benjamin Hatendi|Benjamin]]'s shoe. When the police arrived they found no evidence of foul play, nor any trace of another person in the house.

The next night, [[Benjamin Hatendi|Benjamin]] awoke at 2:40 with an intense fear of the darkness. He looked up from his bed and saw a formless shadow in the darkness. It started to slowly walk towards him in a slow undulated pulse towards the door and small feelers flicked out towards him excitedly. [[Benjamin Hatendi|Benjamin]] pulled the blanket up over his head. After a few minutes he pulled his head over the blanket and the moment he saw the creature it started to move again. Hours passed, and each time he came out from under the covers it started to move.

When morning came, the creature had gone, leaving only a small patch of dark, foul smelling water. The next night it returned and he spent the night under the covers. For the last week and a half he has hidden under the blanket all night.

However, he started to become complacent. The night before the statement he awoke, but then started to fall back to sleep. The creature was on top of him, feelers flicking, and it said to him, in a crisp, dry voice, "The blanket never did anything." It then attacked him across his back and shoulders.

# Post-Statement

Although [[Benjamin Hatendi|Benjamin]] says that he had given the [[The Magnus Institute|Institute]] photos, [[Melanie King|Melanie]] is unable to find any in the file, although his photocopied death certificate is there, with a cause of death 'unknown, possible biological agent, all samples incinerated'. It is dated as March 7th 1983, 5 days after the statement.

There are also a few newspaper cuttings about [[Robin Patton|Robin]], who had written a book about the best natural pools and lakes for swimming. 

With the statement is a zip-lock bag with a piece of fabric in it from bedding with dark stains on it.
