# Statement 

To begin his statement, [[Eugene Vanderstock|Eugene]] says that [[Arthur Nolan|Arthur]] asked him to tell [[Gertrude Robinson|Gertrude]] that [[Agnes Montague]] is dead. [[Arthur Nolan|Arthur]] asked him specifically because he knew [[Eugene Vanderstock|Eugene]] would have to write his whole story down, and it would hurt. [[Eugene Vanderstock|Eugene]] spoke out of turn to [[Arthur Nolan|Arthur]], suggesting they just burn [[The Magnus Institute]] down with [[Gertrude Robinson|Gertrude]] inside, and this statement is his punishment. [[Eugene Vanderstock|Eugene]] tells [[Gertrude Robinson|Gertrude]] she was smart not to actually be present for the statement, and that someday she will push her luck too far, and she had better hope it is not [[Jude Perry]] that comes to collect.

[[Eugene Vanderstock|Eugene]] says he was born in 1936 and that his sacrifices to [[The Desolation]] have kept him looking younger than his 70 years. He does not know how much longer they can keep him going, but when he dies, he intends to go big and make it hurt.

He got his start in [the Blitz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Blitz). A friend's house was destroyed by the bombs; the father was the only one who survived. Something terrible was awakened in [[Eugene Vanderstock|Eugene]] at the sight of his friends' fathers suffering. He committed many crimes during the Blitz, especially with arson. He was eventually found by [[Arthur Nolan|Arthur]] and [[Diego Molina|Diego]], and brought into [[The Cult of the Lightless Flame]].

Eventually, [[Arthur Nolan]] proposed that the Cult create a messiah, a chosen one to help usher in a new world, and bring about [[The Desolation]]'s [[Rituals|ritual]], the [[Scoured Earth]]. [[Eileen Montague]] volunteered. She was five months pregnant, and had already taken care of the child's father in, "The usual manner". Some objected, because the baby had not been, "Conceived of the flame," but since no one knew how that would even work, they decided it would have to be enough for the child to be born in flames.

The Cult burned down 5 acres of forest to create the site of the birth. They built a pyre and laid [[Eileen Montague|Eileen]] down on it. As [[Eileen Montague|Eileen]] went into labour, they set her on fire, and when the flames burned down, all that was left was the baby they named [[Agnes Montague|Agnes]], lying peacefully in the ashes of her mother, untouched.

[[Eugene Vanderstock|Eugene]] says that [[Agnes Montague|Agnes]]'s childhood was difficult. She was prone to fits of violent rage, she could not be allowed near other children or left alone for any length of time. She killed members of the Cult in her temper. Eventually she settled down and seemed to accept her destiny, and her strength grew. Some members of the Cult believed that [[Agnes Montague|Agnes]] should interact with normal children, some believed that [[Agnes Montague|Agnes]] needed to continue training, and others thought they had waited long enough and [[Agnes Montague|Agnes]] was powerful enough already. Eventually they compromised and [[Agnes Montague|Agnes]] was sent to live with [[Raymond Fielding]] at [[Hill Top Road]], a stronghold of [[The Web]]. [[Eugene Vanderstock|Eugene]] says they believed that [[Agnes Montague|Agnes]] would be safe there, though if they had known how powerful [[The Web]] actually was there, they may have reconsidered.

[[Eugene Vanderstock|Eugene]] says that the effect on [[Agnes Montague|Agnes]] of the events [[Hill Top Road]] was unexpected. Despite having destroyed the place utterly, she seemed to remain bound to it in some way. [[Arthur Nolan|Arthur]] considered [[Agnes Montague|Agnes]] keeping [[Raymond Fielding]]'s hand to be worrying. But none of them could know what [[Gertrude Robinson|Gertrude]] herself was going to do. [[Eugene Vanderstock|Eugene]] says that he does not, in truth, actually know what [[Gertrude Robinson|Gertrude]] did. Neither [[Arthur Nolan|Arthur]] nor [[Diego Molina|Diego]] would explain it to him, and [[Jude Perry|Jude]] flies into a rage whenever it is mentioned. But he assumes whatever [[Gertrude Robinson|Gertrude]] did is the reason they spent decades waiting to bring about their designs.

So they waited. [[Agnes Montague|Agnes]] had reached adulthood but would age no further. The Cult found her a place to live, gave her [[Jude Perry|Jude]] for a guardian, and provided for her material needs. The years began to slowly burn away. [[Eugene Vanderstock|Eugene]]'s job was to secure sacrifices for [[Agnes Montague|Agnes]]. He worked in a factory, with large hot furnaces. Eventually enough of his superiors suffered accidents or injuries that he obtained a management position, and was able to create a workshop beneath the main furnace. There, he took people, and melted them into foul-smelling candles made of their flesh and bone and hair. When the candles were lit, [[Eugene Vanderstock|Eugene]] says you could almost hear the victims screaming. [[Agnes Montague|Agnes]] would light these candles in her small flat, and inhale the agony and suffering from which they were created.

[[Eugene Vanderstock|Eugene]] says that the waiting was hardest on [[Agnes Montague|Agnes]], with so much fire and destruction trapped inside her. No one suspected what would actually sink it all. [[Eugene Vanderstock|Eugene]] does not know why [[Arthur Nolan|Arthur]] allowed the relationship between [[Agnes Montague|Agnes]] and [[Jack Barnabas]] that would eventually lead to [[Agnes Montague|Agnes]]'s death, or why [[Jude Perry|Jude]] did not act out when she was usually so jealous over [[Agnes Montague|Agnes]]. Perhaps, he speculates, everyone thought [[Agnes Montague|Agnes]] had some grand plan of suffering in mind for [[Jack Barnabas|Jack]].

In the end, [[Eugene Vanderstock|Eugene]] believes it was not love or happiness, but doubt that did it for [[Agnes Montague|Agnes]], which the [[Avatar]] of [[The Desolation]] could not have. In the end, [[Agnes Montague|Agnes]] told [[The Cult of the Lightless Flame]] that if they tried the [[Scoured Earth]] and failed, it would be centuries before they could try again, but if [[Agnes Montague|Agnes]] died a cold and quiet death instead of in flames, perhaps her spark would return to the [[The Desolation|Lightless Flame]] and she could try again. Thus the cult hanged her as she requested.

In the wake of [[Agnes Montague|Agnes]]'s death, the Cult allowed [[Jude Perry|Jude]] free reign over [[Jack Barnabas]]' fate. She has not yet harmed him, but [[Eugene Vanderstock|Eugene]] says that she will make sure that he is never happy and never without pain. And as for [[Gertrude Robinson|Gertrude]], whatever she did and whatever protection it afforded her is severed with [[Agnes Montague|Agnes]]'s death. [[Arthur Nolan|Arthur]] has told them not to harm her yet, but his authority has been weakened and many are looking to [[Diego Molina|Diego]] for leadership. [[Eugene Vanderstock|Eugene]] hopes they can burn [[Gertrude Robinson|Gertrude]] forever.

#  Post-Statement

[[Jonathan Sims|John]] notes that it is nice to see [[Gertrude Robinson|Gertrude]] also got her fair share of threats, not that any of them resulted in much. Except for [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]], but he did not threaten [[Gertrude Robinson|Gertrude]], he just killed her. He wants to know what [[Gertrude Robinson|Gertrude]] did to interrupt the ritual, as he is, "Ninety percent sure," she did not pay [[Jack Barnabas]] to fall in love with [[Agnes Montague|Agnes]].

It does seem like [[Eugene Vanderstock|Eugene]] is still around, but given what happened with [[Jude Perry]] when [[Jonathan Sims|John]] [[MAG 89 - Twice as Bright|met her]], he is not exactly eager to track him or any of the rest of the Cult down. [[Diego Molina|Diego]], [[Jonathan Sims|John]] says, is likely [[Diego Molina]], whom [[Basira Hussain|Basira]] crossed paths with when she was working as a sectioned officer, and [[Arthur Nolan|Arthur]] might be [[Arthur Nolan]], although [[Jonathan Sims|John]] does not know how he would have gone from a member of the Cult to [[Jane Prentiss]]' landlord.

[[Jonathan Sims|John]] has his own office politics to deal with. [[Basira Hussain|Basira]] is busy doing research for something secretive and trying to adjust to the new [[Alice Daisy Tonner|Daisy]], whom he is weirded out by, but actually liking now. [[Melanie King|Melanie]] is quiet, but therapy seems to be helping. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] has not seen [[The Distortion|Helen]] much. The door is sometimes there, sometimes not, but he has not knocked. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] does not trust [[The Distortion|Helen]], and he should not; whatever its relationship to the person who was or is [[The Distortion|Helen]], assuming he could ever know its motivations is a mistake. And that just leaves [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]].

[[Jonathan Sims|John]] wants to know why they were chosen. [[Agnes Montague|Agnes]] was created with such a specific purpose that a grain of uncertainty threatened the entirety of her being. But [[Jonathan Sims|John]] is so full of doubt there feels like there is no room for anything else, and he assumes [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] is the same. Is there destiny at work, or did they just stumble into this?

Maybe, he says, they are the opposite of [[Agnes Montague|Agnes]]. If doubts are exactly what they need, [[Jonathan Sims|John]] is an amazing chosen one. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] is worried about [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]]. Every other [[Avatar]] gets their feelings burned right out of them, but he just has to sit in his. He has to trust that [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] had whatever he is doing with [[Peter Lukas]] in hand, probably. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] just wants to know what [[Peter Lukas|Peter]]'s plan is. He wonders if he can simply _know_. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] seems to try to use his [[The Archivist]] powers, to ill effect. He shouts in pain and ends the recording.
