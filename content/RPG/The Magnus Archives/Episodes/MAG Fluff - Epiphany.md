In late 2019 Rusty Quill ran a writing competition for light-hearted scenes from life at [[The Magnus Institute]] and the Archives. Epiphany was written by Jack Beckwith and is one of the winning entries. It takes place in the latter half of [[The Magnus Archives - Season 1]].

Epiphany was originally released as a Patreon bonus on February 14, 2020 and Rusty Quill considers the Patreon bonus material to not be strictly canonical but a form of pseudo-canon.

# Recording

[[Martin Blackwood]] is recording a poem when [[Timothy Stoker]] enters the room without knocking. He asks [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] how he is sleeping in the Archives and brought him change of sheets, but then asks him if there's anything on his mind. [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] rambles that he is homesick and thinking of seeing his mother, but [[Timothy Stoker|Tim]] interrupts and asks if he has a crush on anyone. He tries to guess who [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]]'s crush is (his guesses are [[Rosie Zampano|Rosie]], David and Hannah), before realising that [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] likes someone in the Archives (guessing [[Sasha James|Sasha]]). [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]], embarrassed, begs him to go and he leaves after advising [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] to take care of himself and avoid being too selfless, for which [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] thanks him and says he will think about it. [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] resumes recording when he (again) gets interrupted by [[Timothy Stoker|Tim]] entering, who asks if he is [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]]'s crush, and says that the, "Dance card's open." [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] asks him to leave.

[[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] continues to read his poem but is interrupted when [[Jonathan Sims|John]] enters the room. He asks [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] to track down [[Marcus MacKenzie]] for a followup regarding [[MAG 27 - A Sturdy Lock|his father's statement]], which he says he is concerned about, despite his scepticism. He expresses worry about [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]], who insists that he is fine, and then tells him to get back to work.
