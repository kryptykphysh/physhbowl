# Pre-Statement

[[Jonathan Sims|John]] and [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] are walking through a [[Domains|domain]] of [[The Hunt]] in the form of a forest, looking for [[Basira Hussain|Basira]].

As they walk, they begin to argue. They are going very slowly, and [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] urges [[Jonathan Sims|John]] to tell him what he's keeping secret. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] reveals that the domain is based around the fear of betrayal, of one's pack turning on you. He informs [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] that in the new world, the boundaries between the fears are breaking down, if they ever mattered at all. A domain feeds off the fear of the domain, not necessarily the entity it is aligned to. He also claims that domains are not only fear factories of the people in them, but are also shaped by those same people. [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] tells [[Jonathan Sims|John]] he has a feeling they're being followed, and [[Jonathan Sims|John]] confirms his suspicions, and says that as long as [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] keeps calm, they'll be safe. [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] is not calmed by this, and demands to be told what is happening.

As they keep walking, and the argument gets more heated, [[Jonathan Sims|John]] feels the need to make a statement. [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] goes to keep watch, a little annoyed, as [[Jonathan Sims|John]] begins talking.

# Statement

The members of the pack are hunting someone.

They have no names anymore, no words, just the unity in hating the prey they are hunting. Perhaps, if they still had words, they could explain why, list what terrible crimes the prey had committed. But that is not really important here. Here, there is just an intense hatred, and an intense need to prove that hatred is justified by violence. And a subtle fear, almost but not quite drowned out by the thrill of the hunt, that their packmates may just turn on them next.

Ahead, the prey trips, ankle twisted and caught in a vine. The hunt is over, and the pack surrounds them. As they circle the prey, the prey finds one last burst of strength. Not to attack or to flee, but to protest. They are _innocent,_ they cry, and the pack are no righteous hunters but pathetic, bullying hypocrites who feed on the innocent. This is not fair. This is not right.

And maybe they _are_ innocent. Or maybe they are just as guilty as every other member of the pack. It does not matter.

The pack descends, and they savagely tear the prey apart. When its finished and the prey is dead, there is a brief moment of calm as they bask in righteous punishment. And then the paranoia comes back. Watching each other, the pack notice that one of their members has less blood on them than the others. Why is that? Could not reach the prey, perhaps. Or... maybe they felt some sympathy for the victim? For a fellow sinner?

There is no formal accusation, just the realization that one of them is no longer one of them. The prey turns to run.

And the pack begins to hunt.

# Post-Statement

[[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] returns, claiming he thinks he saw something following them. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] asks [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] if he trusts him. He, after some worry, says yes, and [[Jonathan Sims|John]] tells him to stay calm and not make any sudden movements, at which point [[Trevor Herbert]] bursts from the treeline and puts a blade to [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]]'s throat. He demands [[Jonathan Sims|John]] only talk slowly - he knows what he can do, and if he tries compelling him he will slit [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]]'s throat.

[[Jonathan Sims|John]] reveals that this threat is somewhat empty. [[Trevor Herbert|Trevor]] _could_ kill [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] permanently, were he still a hunter. But he is not. Now, he's prey. [[Julia Montauk|Julia]] is dead, killed by [[Alice Daisy Tonner|Daisy]], and now she is hunting [[Trevor Herbert|Trevor]] too. [[Trevor Herbert|Trevor]] wants revenge. He wants to kill [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]], and let [[Jonathan Sims|John]] feel the same loss he did. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] just apologizes for reducing [[Trevor Herbert|Trevor]] even lower than prey, to bait. Suddenly, [[Basira Hussain|Basira]] emerges and shoots [[Trevor Herbert|Trevor]], killing him.

[[Basira Hussain|Basira]] holds the duo at gunpoint, and demands they prove who they are by saying something only they would know. This does not work very well, as [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] does not know [[Basira Hussain|Basira]] that well, and [[Jonathan Sims|John]] is theoretically omniscient. [[Jonathan Sims|John]]'s demeanor, however, convinces [[Basira Hussain|Basira]] that they are indeed the real [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] and [[Jonathan Sims|John]]. She holsters her gun and tells them to follow her, and the three walk deeper into the forest.
