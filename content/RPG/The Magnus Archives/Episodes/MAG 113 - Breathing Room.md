# Pre-Statement

[[Jonathan Sims|John]], [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]], and [[Melanie King|Melanie]] investigate [[Gertrude Robinson|Gertrude's]] storage unit, which is packed with unmarked cardboard boxes. They find the remnants of [[Gorilla Skin]] she destroyed, a notebook full of nonspecific names and dates, and a large amount of plastic explosives. Then [[Melanie King|Melanie]] notices the sound of the [[Tape Recorders|tape recorder]], and she and [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] shout at [[Jonathan Sims|John]] for bringing it along.

# Statement

[[Adelard Dekker]] has delivered the plastic explosives on [[Gertrude Robinson|Gertrude]]'s behalf, to evade some sort of surveillance. He warns her of something "that might come to nothing," and delivers it in the form of a statement.

[[Adelard Dekker|Dekker]] was alerted to a series of suspicious deaths while researching what he calls "a new emergence." The victims apparently died in their sleep of carbon monoxide poisoning, but there was no carbon monoxide found in their homes. In addition, each victim was found with badly damaged vocal cords and blood in their throat. All three scenes had shown evidence of a second person in the home.

[[Adelard Dekker|Dekker]] investigated the matter and concluded that the perpetrator was [[Justin Gough]], an avatar of [[The End]]. [[Justin Gough|Gough]] had given himself severe carbon monoxide poisoning on a camping trip, and was clinically dead for 52 seconds while being treated in hospital. When [[Justin Gough|Justin]] regained consciousness, he was deeply distressed and spoke of awful visions and some sort of terrible debt he now had to pay. After being discharged, [[Justin Gough|Justin]] quit his job and became a recluse, as far as anyone could determine.

[[Adelard Dekker|Dekker]] staked out [[Justin Gough|Justin]]'s flat and caught him leaving under cover of night. [[Justin Gough|Justin]] had cherry-red blood dripping from his nose and did not appear to be breathing. He walked, and [[Adelard Dekker|Dekker]] followed him, to a house in Hackney Wick, where [[Justin Gough|Justin]] walked inside and directly into the bedroom. [[Adelard Dekker|Dekker]] found [[Justin Gough|Justin]] sitting in a corner, apparently asleep, while the man sleeping in the bed began to gasp as if being smothered.

[[Adelard Dekker|Dekker]] concluded that dreams were similar enough to a near-death experience that [[Justin Gough|Justin]] was using them to attack his victims. He also felt sure that [[Justin Gough|Justin]] was too far gone to easily kill. Instead, he used a metal skewer from the kitchen to lobotomize [[Justin Gough|Justin]], which he hoped would render him unable to dream. When the police eventually came to take [[Justin Gough|Justin]] away, [[Justin Gough|Justin]] was not even able to speak.

# Post-Statement

[[Jonathan Sims|John]] explains he found the note tucked into the case of explosives, and wonders if [[Gertrude Robinson|Gertrude]] ever even read it. He also finds it oddly refreshing to once again be completely baffled by a statement.

[[Basira Hussain|Basira]] found records of [[Justin Gough]] in a care home until 2015, but no sign of him after that date. Several staff of the care home died of carbon monoxide poisoning around the time [[Justin Gough|Justin]] disappeared.
