She asks if he was out drinking with [[Alice Daisy Tonner|Daisy]], but [[Jonathan Sims|John]] says [[Alice Daisy Tonner|Daisy]] was at the Archives last night. [[Basira Hussain|Basira]] wants to know what [[Jonathan Sims|John]] did, as communication works both ways. # Statement

[[John Flamsteed|Flamsteed]] writes to his trusted confidante, [[Abraham Sharp]], admitting he recently committed murder. [[Abraham Sharp|Sharp]] is already familiar with [[John Flamsteed|Flamsteed]]'s long-time antagonistic relationship with his former assistant, [[Edmond Halley]], who [[John Flamsteed|Flamsteed]] calls ["Reimer"](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicolaus_Reimers) in reference to the persecutor of, "The great [Tycho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tycho_Brahe)." [[John Flamsteed|Flamsteed]]'s hatred comes to a head with [[Isaac Newton]] and [[Edmond Halley]]'s unauthorised publication of [[John Flamsteed|Flamsteed]]'s star catalogue.

[[John Flamsteed|Flamsteed]], who has been in the habit of stalking [[Edmond Halley|Halley]], follows him out of London on an unusual errand. [[Edmond Halley|Halley]] meets a group of dully clothed men and women with black eyes in a forest clearing. Their words, unheard by [[John Flamsteed|Flamsteed]], have a profound effect on [[Edmond Halley|Halley]], who follows them deeper into the forest. In a clearing where the branches above shut out the sky, stands a pool, "As black as India ink." Hidden, [[John Flamsteed|Flamsteed]] observes the, "Vile pagan exultations," of the group, whose cries of worship form shapes in the water.

[[John Flamsteed|Flamsteed]] leaves, now more terrified than angry. He is determined to intervene himself instead of appealing to authority, as he is both suspicious that some of the celebrants may have been his peers at the Royal Society and conscious of [[Edmond Halley|Halley]]'s impeccable reputation. It is May 2nd, shortly after [[Edmond Halley|Halley]]'s betrayal with the star catalogue, that [[John Flamsteed|Flamsteed]] strikes. He again follows [[Edmond Halley|Halley]] to the forest pool, and seizes his advantage when he sees that they are alone. [[John Flamsteed|Flamsteed]] draws his short sword and, after a brief scuffle, overpowers [[Edmond Halley|Halley]] and drowns him in the pool. He removes the corpse from the water and hides nearby, concerned about being observed leaving the scene of the murder. [[Edmond Halley|Halley]]'s black-eyed compatriots arrive soon after, expressing dismay that their plans for [[Edmond Halley|Halley]] have been disrupted. They carry [[Edmond Halley|Halley]]'s body away.

Two days before the writing of the letter, [[John Flamsteed|Flamsteed]] is interrupted at his telescope by the arrival of [[Isaac Newton]], attended by [[Edmond Halley]]. [[John Flamsteed|Flamsteed]] manages to converse with [[Isaac Newton|Newton]], despite the shock of seeing what he first assumes is a ghost. On the way out, [[Edmond Halley|Halley]] turns back, grabs [[John Flamsteed|Flamsteed]] by both shoulders, and heartily thanks [[John Flamsteed|Flamsteed]] for giving him his life and freedom. [[John Flamsteed|Flamsteed]] recognises, in the black mist covering [[Edmond Halley|Halley]]'s eyes, that he is not looking at [[Edmond Halley|Halley]] at all, but "Reimer," who he had seen only brief flashes of before.

He concludes with a warning to [[Abraham Sharp]] that their mutual acquaintance, [[Edmond Halley|Halley]], is no longer what he seems to be.

# Post-Statement

[[Basira Hussain|Basira]] and [[Jonathan Sims|John]] discuss [[Maxwell Rayner|Rayner]]'s other seeming deaths, the police intervention in his attempted possession of [[Callum Brodie]] and his being shelled in WWI.

[[Basira Hussain|Basira]] inquires whether [[Jonathan Sims|John]] has Beheld anything about the [[Rituals|Ritual]] [[The Dark]] is attempting. He, "Rarely gets anything when the Dark is involved," but theorises it centers on an eclipse.

[[Basira Hussain|Basira]]'s competing theory, presented as she asks him for a piece of paper and a pen, is that [[The Dark]] plans to use [[Ny-Ålesund]] as a staging area and will conduct the ritual itself at the North Pole during the winter solstice to take advantage of, "Eleven weeks of pitch-black night." [[Jonathan Sims|John]] doubts [[Basira Hussain|Basira]]'s intel, as it comes from the same, "Sources," who sent her to the Australian outback while [[Jonathan Sims|John]] descended into [[The Coffin]] in [[MAG 132 - Entombed]], but she insists they are usually reliable.

She plans to book herself and [[Jonathan Sims|John]] passage on a boat to the North Pole in a fortnight, to arrive a week before the summer solstice, when she reasons [[The Dark]]'s power will be at an ebb. They will arm themselves with [[Alice Daisy Tonner|Daisy]]'s gun stash and the offensive capabilities of [[Jonathan Sims|John]]'s powers. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] is deeply dubious at the prospect and wonders whether [[Alice Daisy Tonner|Daisy]], [[Melanie King|Melanie]], or any of [[Basira Hussain|Basira]]'s police associates will accompany them. [[Basira Hussain|Basira]] confirms they will be on their own. She affirms the plan is their best chance, and that she cannot do it alone.
