# Statement

[[Craig Goodall]] was a cannibal, probably, but it wasn't his fault. He used to go to a small takeaway Higham Hill Road in Walthamstow run by [[John Haan|Haan Yong]], aka [[John Haan]], which was closed in May 2004 when he was arrested for killing his wife. He had been disposing of the body by putting the flesh into the meat he sold at the takeaway, although it's not clear which meat contained his wife. "Meat is meat" were [[John Haan]]'s last words as he was arrested.

The takeaway was never sold. One afternoon, [[Craig Gooddall|Craig]] heard a sound of something falling inside the abandoned takeaway, followed a little later by laughter. The window was slightly ajar so he called the police.

[[Craig Gooddall|Craig]] sat in a pub opposite and watched as the police arrived 20 minutes later. They dragged out a teenager with a spray can, told him off and then left. [[Craig Gooddall|Craig]] returned to his house and went to bed where he had dreams of spray paint on rotisserie spikes.

He decided to have a look in the building and called [[Leroy Yates]], a friend in Homerton, who had experience in urban exploration. [[Craig Gooddall|Craig]] refused [[Leroy Yates|Leroy's]] request to join him, but asked him to stop by in an hour if [[Craig Gooddall|Craig]] hadn't spoken to him.

[[Craig Gooddall|Craig]] took a crowbar and headed into the takeaway. The teenager had sprayed, "Meat is me," on the freezer. [[Craig Gooddall|Craig]] opened the freezer to check for meat, but there was nothing in there. As he breathed a sigh of relief he heard a laugh and saw a thin, pale Chinese man (likely [[Tom Haan]]) under a desk with a pair of bolt cutters around his ankle. The man chuckled and closed them, cutting [[Craig Gooddall|Craig]]'s Achilles' tendon, causing him to pass out. 

He regained consciousness lying on the floor, tied up. In the room he found himself in was a pile of chipped tea cups, a stack of old bibles and a pile of human fingers.

The Chinese man stepped out from the shadows chewing on something and holding a butcher's knife. He asked [[Craig Gooddall|Craig]] if he was awake, speaking with a crisp RP accent. [[Craig Gooddall|Craig]] asked who he was, but the man just laughed and he sliced three of [[Craig Gooddall|Craig]]'s fingers off with his knife.

Tossing the fingers into the pile, he wandered over and picked up a bible. He started to monologue about the Romans: how they persecuted early Christians for cannibalism, which may be related to unpleasant rituals involving babies. [[Craig Gooddall|Craig]] asked him if he was Christian, which infuriated the man who started rambling about meat and souls and blood.

Suddenly, [[Leroy Yates|Leroy]] appeared and threw the man to the floor. Kicking the man in the ribs he picked up the knife and freed [[Craig Gooddall|Craig]], who was surprised to see his fingers and ankle in perfect condition. He could see his silver ring on one of the fingers on the pile which was no longer on his own hand.

A candle fell onto the pile of bibles and the building went up in flames.

# Post-Statement

[[Jonathan Sims|Jonathan]] believes that [[Craig Gooddall|Craig]] gave a fake name. [[Timothy Stoker|Tim]] confirmed that the takeaway burned down on the 27th September 2009 which was put down to unknown local vandals. [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] looked into [[John Haan]], who married [[Lan Ying]] shortly after she arrived in the country and set up the takeaway. There were no defensive wounds on [[Lan Ying]]'s body and some of the wounds on her body may have been self-inflicted. 

Just before the shop was closed, [[John Haan]] let all his staff go and hired his nephew who recently moved from China. His name was [[Tom Haan|Haan Tao]], although he took the name [[Tom Haan]], who was the [[MAG 30 - Killing Floor|Dalston Meat Plant]] employee that disappeared.
