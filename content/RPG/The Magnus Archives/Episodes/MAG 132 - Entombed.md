# Pre-Statement

[[Jonathan Sims|John]] listens to [[MAG 61 - Hard Shoulder|Statement #0160112]] to hear [[Alice Daisy Tonner|Daisy]] describing her first encounter with [[The Coffin|the coffin]]. As he prepares to go into [[The Coffin|the coffin]] to rescue [[Alice Daisy Tonner|Daisy]], he makes a tape recording of his goodbye and apologies to [[Melanie King|Melanie]] and [[Basira Hussain|Basira]] and leaves it and his rib bone. He tells another [[Tape Recorders|tape recorder]], "You're coming with me," and picks it up before opening [[The Coffin|the coffin]]. Strong static is heard on the recording as [[The Coffin|the coffin]] compels [[Jonathan Sims|John]] to enter, until [[Jonathan Sims|John]] says, "No need for that; I'm willing."

[[Jonathan Sims|John]] travels down many stone stairs and into a passage that becomes progressively narrower, especially when he stops moving. It becomes a tunnel, and then a crawlspace, and then he can no longer move much at all. He calls for and eventually finds [[Alice Daisy Tonner|Daisy]], and the two catch up while being squeezed by [[The Buried]].

[[Jonathan Sims|John]] tries to find his anchor, but he can no longer feel it. He panics, stating that they are in, "Forever Deep Below Creation," where the weight of everything presses in on them and there is not even an up. He states they are trapped there. [[Alice Daisy Tonner|Daisy]] replies, "Not alone, though?" and [[Jonathan Sims|John]] agrees, "No, not alone."

After a moment of silence, [[Alice Daisy Tonner|Daisy]] checks that [[Jonathan Sims|John]] is still there. When he responds that he is, she says she wants to talk, so [[Jonathan Sims|John]] compels her to talk about how she is doing.

# Statement

[[Alice Daisy Tonner|Daisy]] is scared. She is cut off from [[The Hunt]] in here and she says that she feels more like herself now, and does not want to go back to hurting people like she did before. She recalls the statement [[[[Elias Bouchard|Elias]] Bouchard|[[Elias Bouchard|Elias]]] extracted from her in [[MAG 82 - The Eyewitnesses]] and reflects that [[The Hunt]] has always been part of her life. She does not want to go back to being a violent predator out there, but nor does she want to limp around like wounded prey, and she is most afraid she will not get the chance to choose. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] assures her that there are always choices.

[[Alice Daisy Tonner|Daisy]] admits that she planned to kill [[Jonathan Sims|John]] after they stopped [[The Unknowing]]. She decided to because she started seeing [[Jonathan Sims|John]] in her dreams. When she saw [[Jonathan Sims|John]] in her dream wearing a new shirt before he returned from the United States wearing that shirt, she realized that they were not normal dreams and that [[Jonathan Sims|John]] was not human. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] admits that he is now less human than he was before, but [[Alice Daisy Tonner|Daisy]] does not care right now. They strain as [[The Buried]] squeezes.

# Post-Statement

Suddenly, [[Jonathan Sims|John]] is able to feel his anchor better, and he guides [[Alice Daisy Tonner|Daisy]] out of [[The Coffin|the coffin]], where they find dozens of [[Tape Recorders|tape recorders]] turned on and playing in-person statements. [[Basira Hussain|Basira]] bursts in and begins to say, "[[Jonathan Sims|John]], you stupid idiot—" but breaks off when she sees not just [[Jonathan Sims|John]], but [[Alice Daisy Tonner|Daisy]] as well. [[Alice Daisy Tonner|Daisy]] greets [[Basira Hussain|Basira]] with a quiet, "Hi." [[Basira Hussain|Basira]] responds, "Oh my God."

# Trivia

- Many fans were curious about the use of the pronoun, "their," to describe [[Jonathan Sims|John]] in the show notes. Jonny Sims has clarified on Twitter that this was not indicative of any deeper canon meanings, but as [[The Archivist]]'s gender would likely be unimportant to the plot, there is also nothing to contradict a non-binary interpretation of the character.
- This is the first time [[Jonathan Sims|John]] is confirmed to have physically entered an entity and only one of two such incidents (along with [[The Lonely]].) It is possible he may have been inside [[The Stranger]] in [[MAG 119 - Stranger and Stranger]] and [[The Eye]] as documented in [[MAG 120 - Eye Contact]], though these are unconfirmed.
