# Pre-Statement

[[Jonathan Sims|John]] contemplates his powers and what he is. He breaks into [[Peter Lukas]]' office and once more goes through [[Gertrude Robinson|Gertrude]]'s tapes. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] chooses a tape that he is not drawn to and finds it difficult to hold or press play.

[[Gertrude Robinson|Gertrude]] reads [[Eric Delano]]'s death via garden shears at the hands of [[Mary Keay]] from a page of [[Mary Keay|Mary's]] [[Catalogue of the Trapped Dead|Leitner]] in order to summon [[Eric Delano|Eric]]'s bound ghost. [[Gertrude Robinson|Gertrude]] and [[Eric Delano|Eric]] discuss [[Mary Keay|Mary]] giving [[Gertrude Robinson|Gertrude]] his page, [[Mary Keay|Mary]]'s difficulty in disposing of his body, being bound to the book, aging, [[Gerard Keay|Gerry Keay]], [[Mary Keay|Mary]]'s intent to bind herself to the book, and the mystery behind why [[Mary Keay|Mary]] gave [[Gertrude Robinson|Gertrude]] the page.

[[Gertrude Robinson|Gertrude]] offers to burn [[Eric Delano|Eric]]'s page, but first she wants to find out why [[Mary Keay|Mary]] gave her [[Eric Delano|Eric]]'s page. [[Eric Delano|Eric]] reveals that he quit his job at the Archives months before his death. [[Gertrude Robinson|Gertrude]] expresses a great deal of shock and [[Eric Delano|Eric]] seems a little surprised that neither [[Mary Keay|Mary]] nor [[James Wright]], the previous head of the [[The Magnus Institute|Institute]], told her. [[Eric Delano|Eric]]'s reaction to the revelation that [[[[Elias Bouchard|Elias]] Bouchard]](https://the-magnus-archives.fandom.com/wiki/[[Elias Bouchard|Elias]]_Bouchard "[[Elias Bouchard|Elias]] Bouchard") is the new head of the [[The Magnus Institute|Institute]] implies a large shift in [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]]'s personality at some point after [[Eric Delano|Eric]]'s death. [[Gertrude Robinson|Gertrude]] tries to get him to tell her how he quit and [[Eric Delano|Eric]] revels in the power of holding something over someone else for once. He gives [[Gertrude Robinson|Gertrude]] the conditions that she must find and look after [[Gerard Keay|Gerry Keay]] and that she must take his statement.

# Statement

[[Eric Delano|Eric]] begins his statement explaining that he always loves the mystery of ghosts and that when he finished his mastery in library science, an assistant archivist position at the [[Magnus [[The Magnus Institute|Institute]]]](https://the-magnus-archives.fandom.com/wiki/The_Magnus_[[The Magnus Institute|Institute]] "The Magnus [[The Magnus Institute|Institute]]") seemed like a dream come true. For two years, the job was relatively enjoyable, and then he met [[Mary Keay|Mary]] Keay. [[Eric Delano|Eric]] found her dangerously beautiful, terrifying, and she made him aware of his blood. [[Mary Keay|Mary]] asked him out and he loved the feeling of danger she brought into their relationship, although he tried to convince himself he was safe with her. At one point he found [[Mary Keay|Mary]] sitting with a corpse she claimed was her drunk uncle. Despite all the horrible things [[Mary Keay|Mary]] did to him, [[Eric Delano|Eric]] does not feel that she ever betrayed him.

[[Eric Delano|Eric]] does, however, feel that [[Gertrude Robinson|Gertrude]] betrayed him. [[Gertrude Robinson|Gertrude]] lied, gaslit, and made him complicit in [[The Eye]]'s evils. He could not stand being a part of the [[The Magnus Institute|Institute]] and wanted to protect his son. After their marriage, [[Mary Keay|Mary]] explained the powers, the rituals, and [[Mary Keay|Mary]]'s own goals. [[Eric Delano|Eric]] valued [[Mary Keay|Mary]]'s honesty. For two years, he tried to quit, in large part to protect Gerry. Once he figured it out and how to leave, [[Mary Keay|Mary]] decided he was too much of a burden and murdered him.

After the statement is complete, [[Eric Delano|Eric]] reveals the way to cut ties with Beholding is to destroy your eyes. [[Gertrude Robinson|Gertrude]] agrees that destroying [[Eric Delano|Eric]]'s page is the best solution but does not do so on tape.

# Post-Statement

A [[Tape Recorders|tape recorder]] turns on in [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]]'s office and then [[Jonathan Sims|John]] bursts in. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] thanks him for the intervention and reveals that he has found out how to leave the [[The Magnus Institute|Institute]]. He begs [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] to gouge his eyes out and to run away with him. He tells [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] that he does not want to do it alone and there is no one he trusts more. [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] refuses and says that [[Jonathan Sims|John]] would not do it and that he only came to [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] because [[Jonathan Sims|John]] knew he would refuse. He says that [[Jonathan Sims|John]] only wanted a reason not to blind and potentially kill himself. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] responds by saying that [[The Lonely]] has really gotten [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] and that [[Jonathan Sims|John]] will always be there if [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] ever needs him but he implores [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] not to wait too long.
