# Recording

[[Timothy Stoker|Tim]], [[Sasha James|Sasha]] and [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] startle [[Jonathan Sims|John]] in his office with sudden birthday wishes. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] insists the bottle of wine they got him was quite enough, but he appreciates their effort. They chat a bit about how they went out for ice cream for [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]]'s birthday before [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]] shows up to partake of their cake.

[[Jonathan Sims|John]] claims to be turning 38 and [[Sasha James|Sasha]] calls him on his lie. [[Timothy Stoker|Tim]] implies [[Sasha James|Sasha]] has the password to [[Jonathan Sims|John]]'s computer and knows he’s not the grumpy old man he claims to be. [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]] interrupts and reminds everyone about cake again. They bring it out and sing Happy Birthday to [[Jonathan Sims|John]].

[[Jonathan Sims|John]] is profoundly uncomfortable having fire in the Archives, but [[Timothy Stoker|Tim]] lights the candles on the cake anyway. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] grumpily blows them out and wishes for some peace and quiet, but there is little peace to be had as [[Timothy Stoker|Tim]] opens up a bottle of wine. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] finds drinking on the job highly inappropriate, but [[Elias Bouchard|Elias]] happily allows it and they all have some. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] notices that [[Timothy Stoker|Tim]] has been recording this whole affair and [[Timothy Stoker|Tim]] turns the recorder off after various protestations.

In the present, [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] questions how many times [[Jonathan Sims|John]] has listened to the tapes now. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] claims to be trying to find some meaning in them, but [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] thinks sending them was just Jonah's way of gloating. [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] urges [[Jonathan Sims|John]] to get some sleep, but [[Jonathan Sims|John]] explains that he can't, that it's not something he does any more. It's hard to tell time; there aren’t days to count and the clocks have all stopped, but even when he gets tired, he doesn’t feel the need to sleep.

[[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] admits that sleep is not particularly pleasant in the changed world, but he is just relieved that he doesn't remember his dreams. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] states that he does know [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]]'s dreams now, thanks to his new powers. They seem to be safe at least—It does not want to harm [[Jonathan Sims|John]] and he won’t let It harm [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] either.

[[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] tries telling [[Jonathan Sims|John]] that everything is not his fault. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] advises that he doesn't want to go over the same argument again. He is mourning the world he killed and believes they are all trapped in its rotting corpse because of him. [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] cuts him off before he can spiral too deep into self deprecation.

They have several tapes from [[Gertrude Robinson|Gertrude]] and [[Jonathan Sims|John]] decides to play his favourite for [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]].

[[Gertrude Robinson|Gertrude]] addresses [[Jonathan Sims|The Archivist]]. If they are listening to the tape, it means that she is dead and they have been chosen to replace her. She hopes she is speaking to [[Sasha James]], but her words are intended for anyone filling the role of [[The Archivist|Archivist]]. She emphasises that she is deadly serious as she lays out the situation:

[[Jonathan Sims|The Archivist]] is now in grave danger and will be so for the rest of their life. They will also be unable to step down as Archivist or quit [[The Magnus Institute|the Institute]]. In fact, attempting to do so is likely to the quickest way of proving the truth of her words. She explains the existence of [[The Entities]], [[Avatar|Avatars]], and [[Rituals]]. She reveals that [[The Magnus Institute|the Institute]] serves the [[Beholding]] and that by accepting the role of [[The Archivist|Archivist]], the listener has unknowingly been tied to its ritual and become an [[Avatar]]. She warns them not to trust [[Jonah Magnus]], who currently inhabits the body of [[Elias Bouchard]] and possesses powerful clairvoyance. She has intentionally kept the Archives in a state of chaos for decades as she believes their organization would benefit [[Jonah Magnus|Jonah]].

Above all, she urges them to be ready. They are now a target of countless beings serving other powers. [[Gertrude Robinson|Gertrude]] laments her lack of time, but hopes her actions will ensure that no one needs to hear this tape. She wishes the Archivist good luck as [[Jurgen Leitner]] enters the room.

She admonishes him for leaving the tunnels, but time is of the essence. She asks him to use the [[The Seven Lamps of Architecture|Ruskin book]] to move the gas main in the tunnels to directly underneath [[The Magnus Institute|the Institute]]. By her estimate, the ritual of [[The People's Church of the Divine Host]] will collapse very soon so they need to move fast and set fire to the Archives. [[Jurgen Leitner|Jurgen]] notes that her tape recorder is still running and she turns it off.

In the present, [[Jonathan Sims|John]] muses on how things could have been different if they’d had this tape from the start. [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] does not think it’s healthy to dwell too much on the past, but [[Jonathan Sims|John]] knows that it was his craving for knowledge that condemned the world to endless torment, and at least the grief distracts him from other things he could be feeling. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] explains that he is hurting, and he needs time.

[[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] wants to leave, but [[Jonathan Sims|John]] does not see why, because at least they have each other. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] points out that they don’t even need food any more, that they are simply being sustained by some force now. It is a new world and the natural laws are whatever the Powers want them to be.

[[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] still wants to leave; there must be some way to stop the change and turn the world back to normal. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] does not know if there is one, and the agony and terror is so much louder out there. He tells [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] that he loves him, but he needs more time. [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] agrees to wait for him and notices a new tape recorder. They don’t know why it is recording them, but it is not a good sign.
