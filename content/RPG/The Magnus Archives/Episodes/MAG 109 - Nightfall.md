# Pre-Statement

[[Jonathan Sims|[[Jonathan Sims|John]]]], [[Julia Montauk|Julia]], and [[Trevor Herbert|Trevor]] are in a secluded cabin alongside [[Max Mustermann]], an agent of [[The Stranger]] that was dismembered by [[Trevor Herbert|Trevor]]. [[Jonathan Sims|John]], who was kidnapped by the two [[Hunters]], is fearfully waiting for [[Max Mustermann|Mustermann]] to reform his body to a point where he can be questioned on why he was following [[Jonathan Sims|John]]. Unless [[Julia Montauk|Julia]] and [[Trevor Herbert|Trevor]] can be convinced that [[Jonathan Sims|John]] genuinely means to stop [[The Unknowing]], they plan to kill him.

[[Jonathan Sims|John]] nervously proposes [[Julia Montauk|Julia]] give a statement about how she met [[Trevor Herbert|Trevor]] while they wait, and she agrees.

# Statement

[[Julia Montauk|Julia]] and [[Trevor Herbert|Trevor]] recall that they met in July 2010. 

[[Julia Montauk|Julia]] had trouble living a normal life due to the infamy of her father, [[Robert Montauk]], and relocated frequently. She moved to Manchester in the summer of 2010. To avoid other people and combat her long-standing fear of the dark, she started working the night shift in security and maintenance for an old office building. 

A company called [[DKN Systems]] rented space in the building and often did work at night. The only employee who spoke to her was [[Vardhan Darvish]]. He repeatedly approached her for help when a lightbulb had blown somewhere in the building. [[Julia Montauk|Julia]] joked that he was responsible for breaking the lights, which made him uncomfortable.  

[[Trevor Herbert|Trevor]] interjects that he had been tracking [[Vardhan Darvish|Darvish]] for weeks after suspecting a [[Vampires|vampire]] was responsible for a number of missing homeless people in the area. Although [[Vardhan Darvish|Darvish]] smelled strange, he did not smell of vampire. One night, [[Trevor Herbert|Trevor]] saw [[Vardhan Darvish|Darvish]] transporting deliveries from [[Outer Bay Shipping]] into the building and could tell that the box contained a body. They brought the box past [[Julia Montauk|Julia]] to their offices on the fourth floor. Shortly after, [[Trevor Herbert|Trevor]] snuck into the building, and [[Julia Montauk|Julia]] chased him. 

[[Julia Montauk|Julia]] found the fourth floor with the lights out. The [[DKN Systems]] rooms were empty, with the windows boarded up and painted black. She heard a faint dripping sound and found one room where the floor was somehow covered in still, black water that looked impossibly deep. At the center of the room was a table with a man lying atop it; liquid was trickling from his mouth and eyes to the floor. Three [[DKN Systems|DKN]] workers stood around the table on platforms, unresponsive and posed as if silently screaming. [[Julia Montauk|Julia]] tried to step into the room and fell into cold, black water, losing her torch. 

[[Trevor Herbert|Trevor]], who had been watching [[Julia Montauk|Julia]], reached in and helped her pull herself out. [[Vardhan Darvish|Darvish]] then attacked [[Trevor Herbert|Trevor]], almost killing him. [[Julia Montauk|Julia]] realized that the pupils of [[Vardhan Darvish|Darvish]]'s eyes had expanded to swallow the irises completely. When she saw [[Vardhan Darvish|Darvish]]'s tattoo of the hand with the closed eye on it, she recognized it from the pendant her mother left behind when she disappeared. [[Julia Montauk|Julia]] grabbed [[Trevor Herbert|Trevor]]'s fallen pocket knife and attacked [[Vardhan Darvish|Darvish]]. When [[Vardhan Darvish|Darvish]] died, the water immediately dried up and the posed workers collapsed. The man on the table was already dead. 

[[Julia Montauk|Julia]] and [[Trevor Herbert|Trevor]] immediately paired up, ensured the workers were dead, and then left together. They have travelled together, hunting monsters, ever since. 

# Post-Statement

[[Jonathan Sims|John]] asks how [[Julia Montauk|Julia]] and [[Trevor Herbert|Trevor]] get money, and [[Julia Montauk|Julia]] responds that they aren't concerned by petty crime while hunting. They came to America some years prior to follow rumors of a wolfman, but as they did not travel legally they do not have a way of returning home without attracting unwanted attention.

Officer [[Max Mustermann|Mustermann]] begins to speak, having healed himself well enough to form lungs. He was following [[Jonathan Sims|John]] in the hopes of harvesting his skin for [[The Unknowing]]. He demands [[Jonathan Sims|John]] turn off the [[Tape Recorders|tape recorder]] because he does not trust whatever is listening, and [[Trevor Herbert|Trevor]] and [[Julia Montauk|Julia]] agree.
