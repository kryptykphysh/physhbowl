The "NotThem" (as referred to by [[Adelard Dekker]]) is a shapechanging creature affiliated with the [[The Entities|Entity]] known as [[The Stranger]]. It has the ability to alter memories of those who knew its victims, as well as print and digital photos (although Polaroids seem to be immune). It then replaces them, living an apparently normal life, until moving on to a new victim. For each victim shown, one person is chosen to be immune to its reality-distorting effects, realizing that the NotThem looks distinctly unlike the person it is impersonating. In several cases, it takes a great deal of pleasure in tormenting these people.

In its natural form, it is described as "long and thin", with "stick-like limbs" and "too many joints". Even when encountered in a monstrous form, it was described as looking similar to its victim but "stretched out". It is the main antagonist of Season 2.

# History

In 1994, [[Rose Cooper]] is replaced by the NotThem in the form of [[Not-Rose]]. Only her daughter, [[Lucy Cooper]], is aware of the change, and the real [[Rose Cooper]]'s voice remains unchanged on tape recordings.

In 2001, [[Lawrence Moore]]'s cousin Carl is replaced by the NotThem. Only [[Lawrence Moore|Lawrence]] notices, and only a few Polaroid photos still show the real Carl. [[Adelard Dekker]] binds the entity, in the form of [[Not-Carl]], to [[Web Table|a web patterned table]].

In 2006, [[Amy Patel]] sees the NotThem become [[Not-Graham]] due to Graham's possession of the table. She calls the police immediately when she sees the creature crawl through Graham's window, but they leave after [[Not-Graham]] shows them his passport with his new photo.

In 2016, [[Breekon & Hope]] deliver the table to [[The Magnus Institute]]. [[Sasha James]] is replaced by [[Not-Sasha]] after escaping to the [[The Magnus Institute|Institute's]] [[Artefact Storage]] during [[Jane Prentiss|Prentiss']] attack. In 2017, [[Jonathan Sims]] becomes suspicious of [[Not-Sasha]] after some unusual comments from [[Melanie King]]. He breaks the table trying to destroy the creature, but only succeeds in releasing it. He is pursued into the tunnels beneath the institute by it. The creature is trapped in the tunnels by [[Jurgen Leitner]] in [[MAG 79 - Hide and Seek]].

On September 25th, 2018, [[Peter Lukas]] releases [[Not-Sasha]] in order to create a distraction. [[Not-Sasha]] sets out to hunt down [[Jonathan Sims|John]] but ends up fighting [[Julia Montauk]] and [[Trevor Herbert]] instead.

In [[MAG 165 - Revolutions]] [[Not-Sasha]] encounters [[Jonathan Sims|John]] and [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] in the realm of [[The Stranger]], where she insults [[Sasha James|Sasha]]. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] becomes enraged and he calls upon the [[The Eye|Ceaseless Watcher]], using the powers of [[The Eye]] to destroy it.

# Trivia

In [[MAG 200 - Last Words]], [[Jonathan Sims|John]] describes one of the first monsters as "long things that wear you like a suit". While this is not explicitly said to be the [[Not-Them]], it does seem to very much describe them. If this is the case, it would make the [[Not-Them]] by far the oldest character in the podcast- while the exact timeline is unclear, a creature from this period would be at least millennia old, and very possibly predate civilization itself.
