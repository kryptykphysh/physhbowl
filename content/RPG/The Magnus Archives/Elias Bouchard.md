Elias Bouchard is the Head of [[The Magnus Institute]]. He has held the position since 1996 when he took over after [[James Wright.]]

Elias earned a [third class honours degree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_undergraduate_degree_classification#Third-class_honours) in Philosophy, Politics, and Economics at Christ Church (Oxford). He was also apparently "something of a pothead" in his university days.

# Description

In [[MAG 192 - An Appointment]], during her job interview, [[Rosie Zampano|Rosie]] describes Elias Bouchard as seeming far too young for his role and having an old-fashioned haircut. She says his eyes are "cold and grey, and somehow so much older." In [[MAG 82: The Eyewitnesses]], [[Alice Daisy Tonner|Daisy]] calls him a "weird little freak".

Elias is a servant of [[The Eye|Beholding]] and the head of [[The Magnus Institute]]. Initially, he presents as skeptical of [[Jonathan Sims]]'s supernatural claims regarding [[Jane Prentiss]] and her abilities. Later, it is revealed that Elias is an [[Avatar]] of the [[The Eye|Beholding]] and possesses powers of his own.

He states that he is trying to help [[Jonathan Sims|John]] grow his abilities, but Bouchard is ultimately loyal to the [[The Magnus Institute|Institute]]. He killed [[Gertrude Robinson]] and [[Jurgen Leitner]], as he considered them both threats to the [[The Magnus Institute|Institute]].


Elias has used his [[The Eye|Beholding]] abilities to traumatise his employees into compliance, including implanting disturbing knowledge into the minds of both [[Melanie King|Melanie]] and [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] about their respective parents.

# History

At some point before joining the [[The Magnus Institute|Institute]], Elias experienced a supernatural event involving a mysterious [[List of Books|book]] which caused the death of his friend, [[Allan Schrieber]].

In 1991, Elias received a letter from [[The Magnus Institute]] inviting him to interview for a job, despite having not sent out a CV (resume). When asked by [[James Wright]] why he chose to "heed the call" of the [[The Magnus Institute|Institute]], Elias replied that "this is the place I know I should be."

The current Elias Bouchard holds the consciousness of [[Jonah Magnus]], founder of [[The Magnus Institute]]. [[Jonah Magnus|Jonah]] has been 'body-hopping' to both stay alive and remain Head of the [[The Magnus Institute|Institute]] by replacing the stolen body's eyes with his own. In 1973, he left the body of [[Richard Mendelson]] for that of [[James Wright]], and in 1996, he possessed Elias Bouchard, who climbed to the top remarkably fast as Head of the [[The Magnus Institute|Institute]].

In [[MAG 29 - Cheating Death]], [[Jonathan Sims|John]] says that Elias claims to have been working as a filing clerk at the [[The Magnus Institute|Institute]] in 1972. However in [[MAG 49: The Butcher’s Window]], [[Jonathan Sims|John]] observes that it "looks like Elias only joined the [[The Magnus Institute|Institute]] five years before, in 1991, working in the [[Artefact Storage]]." This was seen as a contradiction in the timeline until the reveal of Elias's true identity.

[[Gertrude Robinson|Gertrude]] became suspicious of Elias's promotion but didn't act until decades later when she planned to burn the [[The Magnus Institute|Institute]] down and destroy the original body of [[Jonah Magnus]], which resides in [[The Panopticon]] beneath the [[The Magnus Institute|Institute]]. It took her the better part of a decade to navigate the tunnels to find the centre. She hoped he would be distracted with [[The Dark's]] [[Rituals|ritual]], but Elias shot her in the chest, killing her, moments before she could set the fire.

After [[Gertrude Robinson|Gertrude]]'s death, Elias promoted [[Jonathan Sims]] to head [[The Archivist|Archivist]], grooming him to develop his powers with the ultimate goal of having him initiate [[The Mass Ritual]]. During seasons one and two, [[Jonathan Sims|John]] was kept largely in the dark. Elias slowly fed him information that he thought would best manipulate [[Jonathan Sims|John]] into accomplishing Elias's eventual goal. [[Jurgen Leitner]]'s sudden appearance to [[Jonathan Sims|John]] in [[MAG 80 - The Librarian]] threatened to ruin his plans if [[Jurgen Leitner|Leitner]] gave [[Jonathan Sims|John]] too much information. After a brief conversation with [[Jurgen Leitner|Leitner]] and getting his statement, [[Jonathan Sims|John]] stepped out for a cigarette. Elias seized this opportunity, entered [[Jonathan Sims|John's]] office, and beat [[Jurgen Leitner]] to death with the same metal pipe [[Jonathan Sims|John]] had been carrying for protection. (Elias would later admit in [[MAG 102: Nesting Instinct]] that he "possibly overreacted.") When [[Jonathan Sims|John]] returned and realized [[Jurgen Leitner|Leitner]] had been murdered in his own office with a pipe that was in his hands minutes before, he went into hiding.

While [[Jonathan Sims|John]] was wanted for [[Jurgen Leitner|Leitner's]] murder and hiding at [[Georgie Barker]]'s house, Elias anonymously sent him statements focused on how to stop [[The Unknowing]], the ritual of [[The Stranger]]. Elias had hoped that by showing him where to start, [[Jonathan Sims|John]] would find answers on his own and develop the powers and skills he needed to survive [[The Unknowing]].

After being confronted by [[Alice Daisy Tonner|Daisy]], [[Basira Hussain|Basira]], and [[Jonathan Sims|John]] about [[Gertrude Robonson|Gertrude]] and [[Jurgen Leitner|Leitner's]] murders, Elias revealed to them and an audience of [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]], [[Timothy Stoker|Tim]] and [[Melanie King|Melanie]], that he had killed them to protect the [[The Magnus Institute|Institute]]. He also threatened to tell the police of [[Alice Daisy Tonner|Daisy's]] past murders if she tried to kill him, which would land [[Basira Hussain|Basira]] in trouble as well. [[Alice Daisy Tonner|Daisy]] stood down, and Elias had [[Basira Hussain|Basira]] sign a contract of employment as a form of collateral. Elias explained that since they are all now tied to the [[The Magnus Institute|Institute]], should he or the [[The Magnus Institute|Institute]] be destroyed, they all would "follow suit." Later, in [[MAG 158 - Panopticon]], Elias admits he doesn't know if this is entirely true because their ties to the [[The Magnus Institute|Institute]] may not be as strong as he originally implied. However, he assures that at very least, "it won't be pleasant for them."

After escaping from [[Nikola Orsinov|Nikola]] in [[MAG 101 - Another Twist]], [[Jonathan Sims|John]] scolds Elias for not rescuing him. Elias reveals he wasn't able to see where [[Jonathan Sims|John]] had been taken. He tells [[Jonathan Sims|John]] he can't spend his time helping him develop his powers and explaining things to him "like a child" and that [[Jonathan Sims|John]] must find out information on his own to grow his abilities.

In [[MAG 120 - Eye Contact]], Elias chronicles [[Jonathan Sims|John's]] coma dreams. He commends [[Jonathan Sims|John's]] progress as [[The Archivist]] and hopes he can continue growing. [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] brings an officer to arrest Elias, who then spends [[MAG 121: Far Away]] to [[MAG 157: Rotten Core]] in prison, occasionally advising [[Basira Hussain|Basira]]. [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] comes to visit him once in [[MAG 138: The Architecture of Fear]] to ask a few questions.

Elias escapes from prison in [[MAG 158 - Panopticon]] and meets [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] and [[Peter Lukas]] in the centre of the tunnels. It is revealed that [[Jonah Magnus]] had long been inhabiting the body of Elias Bouchard and that it's [[Jonah Magnus|Jonah's]] original body that sits in [[The Panopticon]] "binding it all together." [[Peter Lukas|Peter]] had been manipulating [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] over time in hopes that when this moment came, [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] would kill Elias and take his place in [[The Panopticon]]. [[Peter Lukas|Peter]] is shocked and apparently frightened when [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] refuses to kill Elias. [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] explains that it was he who had been manipulating [[Peter Lukas|Peter]], allowing things to play out in hopes of discovering his end goal. In [[MAG 159: The Last]], [[Peter Lukas|Peter]] informs [[Jonathan Sims|John]] that he and Elias had made a bet. If [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] didn't kill Elias, then [[Peter Lukas|Peter]] would let [[Jonathan Sims|John]] in [[The Lonely]], the pocket dimension of the [[The Entities|entity]] with the same name. [[Jonathan Sims|John's]] entry to this dimension would cause him to be marked by [[The Lonely]], furthering Elias's ultimate goal to groom [[Jonathan Sims|John]] for performing [[The Mass Ritual]].

A few weeks after [[Jonathan Sims|John]] and [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] return from [[The Lonely]], Elias sends [[Jonathan Sims|John]] a statement in [[MAG 160 - The Eye Opens]] with the deceptive appearance of being about a fire in the childhood home of [[Hazel Rutter]]. Unaware that this statement is a trap with global repercussions, [[Jonathan Sims|John]] begins to read it aloud, soon discovering he is unable to stop. He quickly realizes the statement is in fact from Elias, detailing his experience as [[Jonah Magnus]] up until the present time. Elias talks of his failed attempt at [[The Watcher's Crown]] ritual and how he eventually learned that individual rituals always fail because the [[The Entities|Fears]] cannot be separated. To resolve this apparent impasse, he details how he prepared a [[The Mass ritual|mass ritual]] that would use [[The Archivist]] to bring all the [[The Entities|Fears]] into the world _at once_. However, in order for this to work, he explains [[The Archivist]] must be "marked" by every [[The Entities|Entity]] individually. [[Jonathan Sims|John's]] recent encounter with [[The Lonely]] in [[MAG 159: The Last]] was the final mark. At the end of the statement, [[Jonathan Sims|John]] is compelled to perform the ritual by reading an incantation aloud. As soon as he speaks the final words "I open the door", [[The Change]] takes place amidst the sounds of glass breaking and static rising. [[Jonathan Sims|John]] is immediately rendered unconscious as Elias's ultimate goal appears realized.

As of [[MAG 196 - This Old House]], Elias resides in [[The Panopticon]] at the "centre" of the nightmare [[Domain|domains]], though [[Jonathan Sims|John]] notes that it "wasn’t made for him."

# Known Powers

- **Limited Omniscience** — Elias has noted his ability to see and know anything he puts his mind to, but it requires concentration. He was not able to locate [[Jonathan Sims|John]] when he was taken by [[The Stranger]], was unaware of [[Basira Hussain|Basira's]] actions while he was talking with [[Melanie King|Melanie]], and was unaware [[Martin Blackwood|Martin]] knocked over a stack of papers during [[Melanie Kinf|Melanie's]] "performance review."
- **Memory Insertion** — Elias can put any information he wants into another person's mind. It is implied that this information must be true, and he is not capable of "removing" information.
- **Limited Technopathy** — Elias was able to doctor the CCTV footage of the security cameras inside of the [[The Magnus Institute|Institute]] to give himself an alibi during the murder of [[Gertrude Robinson]].
- **Incarnation** — Elias describes himself as the "heart" of [[The Magnus Institute]] and that if he were to be destroyed, everyone who serves the [[The Magnus Institute|Institute]] would die along with him. Later, he specifies this may not happen if their ties to the [[The Magnus Institute|Institute]] are not as strong as he had implied. Elias speculates that [[Jonathan Sims|John]] _might_ be strong enough to "weather" the effects.
- **Body-hopping** — Elias can take over the body of another person to extend his life. This is implied to happen when he transfers his eyes to the new body. If his original body were to be harmed, however, Elias has stated it would harm both him and those connected to him through [[The Magnus Institute]].
- **Watcher's Call** — Elias can call [[Jonathan Sims]] to himself, allowing [[Jonathan Sims|John]] to easily find and navigate to him. It's unclear whether this will work on anyone else since it has only been used on [[Jonathan Sims|John]].
