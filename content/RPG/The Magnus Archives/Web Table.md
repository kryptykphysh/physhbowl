# First Appearance
[[MAG 3 - Across the Street]]
# Connected Entity
[[The Web]]
# Last Known Location
Destroyed by [[Jonathan Sims]] in the [[Artefact Storage]] of [[The Magnus Institute]].

The Web Table is a large, dark wood table that has engraved upon it a hypnotic pattern that appears to lead towards the centre. In the centre of the table is a recess with a box within it.

It is first introduced in [[MAG 3 - Boatswain's Call]] and appears throughout the series.
  
Later statements reveal it as being an embodiment of [[The Web.]] [[Adelard Dekker]] used the table's connection with [[The Web]] to bind the creature known as '[[NotThem]]', a creature of [[The Stranger]], to it.

# Timeline

**1960s** – [[Raymond Fielding]] runs a halfway house for orphans on [[Hill Top Road]] in Oxford on behalf of the local diocese. He takes on a girl called [[Agnes Montague|Agnes]], whom the rest of the neighbourhood dislikes. As [[Ronald Sinclair]] tries to leave the house, he is compelled to return to the basement, where [[Raymond Fielding|Fielding]] is waiting for him with the table, but he is able to escape.

**April 2001** – [[Lawrence Moore]] realises that his cousin Carl has been replaced by [[Not-Carl]]. [[Adelard Dekker]] comes to him and performs a binding ritual to bind the creature to a web-patterned table.

**7th April 2006** - [[Amy Patel]] witnesses [[Graham Folger]]'s appearance change due to the [[NotThem]]. [[Graham Folger|Graham]] has in his possession a large table with a hypnotic pattern upon it.

**23rd November 2006** - [[Evo Lensik]] uproots a tree at a house on [[Hill Top Road]] after finding a box previously belonging to the table, engraved with the same pattern.

**Summer 2016** - A hypnotic table with a piece missing is delivered to [[The Magnus Institute]] by [[Breekon & Hope]].

**29th July 2016** - [[Jonathan Sims]], while trying to kill a spider, puts a hole in the drywall of the Archives, releasing the [[Silver Worms|worms]] that had been laying siege to the Archives. During the attack, [[Sasha James]] escapes into the [[The Magnus Institute|institute's]] [[Artefact Storage]], where she is attacked and replaced by The [[NotThem]], the creature bound to the table. Two tapes holding case recordings go missing - notably, they both contained recordings of [[Sasha James|Sasha's]] voice.

**16th February 2017** - In an attempt to kill [[NotSasha]], [[Jonathan Sims]] takes an axe to the [[The Magnus Institute|Institute's]] [[Artefact Storage]] and destroys the [[Web Table]], inadvertently releasing [[NotSasha]]'s binding at the hands of [[Adelard Dekker]].
