 The Coffin, also known as The Pit or The Cramped Casket, is an artefact of [[The Buried]] and a gateway into its [[Domains|domain]].

# Description

The coffin is rectangular and made of unvarnished, pale yellow wood and with the words “DO NOT OPEN” scratched into it, in letters three inches high.

When encountered by [[Joshua Gillespie]]([[MAG 2: Do Not Open]]), [[Alice Daisy Tonner|Alice "Daisy" Tonner]], and [[Jonathan Sims]], the coffin is wrapped in a thick metal chain closed with a heavy iron padlock. [[Joshua Gillespie|Joshua]] also describes the wood of the coffin as being very warm to the touch while the chain was as cold as you would expect from a thick piece of iron.

Inside the coffin is a staircase with roughly hewn stone steps leading deep into the ground. When opened, the coffin compels people to walk down its steps before closing behind them. Victims find the journey down increasingly tight and then become trapped in [[The Buried]].

# History

The exact origins of the coffin are unknown but "John]]", a servant of [[The Stranger]], found it in chains at some point and believed he could control and bargain with it. In or after 1993, he enlisted [[Breekon & Hope]] to help him transport it. According to [[Breekon & Hope|Breekon]], the coffin was a "test" that was given to multiple people. Presumably in the same manner as [[Joshua Gillespie]]'s experience: [[Jonathan Sims|John]] left the coffin with a person and they were eventually compelled to enter it and become lost in [[The Buried]]. The last of these instances is when, around 1996, "John" gave [[Joshua Gillespie]] £10,000 to look after an unspecified package.

After [[Joshua Gillespie|Joshua]] spent the money almost a year later, [[Breekon & Hope]] delivered the coffin to his apartment. [[Joshua Gillespie|Joshua]] spent about a year living with the coffin. Placing things on the coffin caused a soft but insistent scratching from inside and whenever it rained, a soft, melodious moaning emanated from within. The coffin seemed to affect his sleep, causing him to try and open the coffin while sleepwalking. [[Joshua Gillespie|Joshua]] counteract-acted this by encasing the key to the padlock in a block of ice.

After almost a year and a half, the coffin no longer moaned when it rained. [[Breekon & Hope|Breekon, Hope]], and "John" came to retrieve it and appeared surprised to see [[Joshua Gillespie|Joshua]] again. [[Joshua Gillespie|Joshua]] heard screaming from his living room as they fetched the coffin and there was no sign of "John" afterwards. According to [[Breekon & Hope|Breekon]], "when the test finally failed" and the coffin did not have another victim, it claimed the one who tried to master it. However, [[Breekon & Hope]] were not included in this arrangement and became bound to the coffin. They continued to carry and transport it without a destination.

[[Breekon & Hope]] eventually started working with [[Nikola Orsinov]] and [[Circus of the Other|The Circus of the Other]], bringing the coffin with them wherever they went.

On July 24th 2002, it was raining heavily as [[Breekon & Hope]] drove down the M6 near Preston, accompanied by a man who called himself Tom. They were stopped by police officers [[Isaac Masters]] and [[Alice Daisy Tonner|Alice "Daisy" Tonner]] for driving at about 25 miles an hour on the motorway and brought out the coffin after the police hear it moaning in the back of the van. [[Isaac Masters|Masters]] demanded the key from Tom and unlocked the coffin. The chains snapped off it as if they were spring-loaded and the lid opened on its own. [[Breekon & Hope]] restrained [[Alice Daisy Tonner|Daisy]] as [[Isaac Masters|Masters]], seemingly entranced, walked into the coffin and the lid closed behind him. [[Alice Daisy Tonner|Daisy]] was unable to stop them as they packed up the coffin and drove off.

[[Fiona Law]]'s death was officially listed as resulting from a failed liver transplant in 2003 but [[MAG 167 - Curiosity]] reveals that she was instead consumed by the coffin. It is unclear whether this also occurred in 2003.

The coffin, alongside [[Breekon & Hope]], is present in the [[House of Wax Museum]] in Great Yarmouth in May 2017 while [[Jonathan Sims|John]] is being held captive there.

The coffin is also present in the [[House of Wax Museum|House of Wax]] on August 7th 2017, when [[The Unknowing]] is attempted. [[Breekon & Hope|Breekon]] feeds [[Alice Daisy Tonner|Daisy]] to the coffin after she kills [[Breekon & Hope|Hope]]. With [[Breekon & Hope|Hope]]'s death, [[Breekon & Hope|Breekon]] is no longer bound to the coffin and he drops it off at the [[The Magnus Institute|Institute]] on 3rd March, 2018.

Later that month, [[Jonathan Sims]] willingly enters the coffin in order to rescue [[Alice Daisy Tonner|Daisy]] and they emerge together after three days. Afterwards, [[Jonathan Sims|John]] has the coffin sent to [[Artefact Storage]] with specific instructions on how to keep it locked up.
